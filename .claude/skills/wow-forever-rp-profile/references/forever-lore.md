# World of Warcraft: Forever — lore reference for RP profiles

**Abbreviations:** ADP = After the Dark Portal (years counted from the Dark Portal opening, the first orc invasion of Azeroth); RP = roleplay; IC / OOC = in character / out of character; TRP3 = Total RP 3, the profile addon.

Compiled 27 October 2026, a week before Forever's launch on 4 November 2026. Forever's own story will grow with patches. Where this file and the live game disagree, the game wins. Section 8 lists the sources.

## 1. What Forever is

- A permanent third branch of World of Warcraft, alongside retail and Classic. It launches **4 November 2026**, and the level cap stays at **60** for good.
- It is fixed in **25 ADP**, the first year of the original 2004 game. That comes **after** the *Forsaken Kingdom* campaign for Warcraft III: Reforged (the fall of Lordaeron, the Forsaken breaking free, the founding of the Undercity) and **before** Molten Core, so long before The Burning Crusade.
- It has **its own continuity**, separate from retail. Holly Longdale (executive producer) says that "some stories from the 'known' vanilla narrative simply won't happen the same way here." New characters and storylines fill the original game's unfinished threads.
- Design pillars that suit RP: "The world is the main character", "Not every story has to be about saving the world", "Azeroth remains dangerous". Grounded, ordinary-scale characters fit the setting best.
- Rulesets: Normal, PvP (player versus player), RP, and RP-PvP, with Hardcore to come. There is one "mega-realm" per ruleset per region, so the RP community is concentrated.
- "First and last names" are listed as a Forever feature. The exact naming rules weren't published at the time of writing.

**What this means for profiles:** treat the original game's world as the default backdrop, but don't present a specific vanilla quest outcome as settled fact. Forever may tell that story differently. Anything set after 25 ADP hasn't happened.

## 2. Calendar and age arithmetic

Key years (Warcraft Wiki timeline):

| Year (ADP) | Event |
|---|---|
| 0 | The Dark Portal opens; the First War begins |
| 3 | Stormwind falls; the First War ends |
| 4–6 | The Second War; the Alliance of Lordaeron wins |
| 7 | Orc internment camps begin |
| 10 | Day of the Dragon; Battle of Grim Batol |
| 15–18 | Thrall escapes internment and frees the orcs |
| 20 | The Third War: plague of undeath, Culling of Stratholme, Arthas slays King Terenas, Lordaeron falls, the Scourge invades Quel'Thalas |
| 21 | Battle of Mount Hyjal; Archimonde defeated; Nordrassil spent |
| 22 | Arthas ascends as the Lich King; the Forsaken break free under Sylvanas |
| 23 | Durotar settled; Orgrimmar founded |
| 25 | **Now.** Gnomeregan is lost after Sicco Thermaplugg's betrayal; the events of the original WoW begin |

**Age arithmetic:** birth year = 25 − age. A 30-year-old human was born in −5 ADP. They were 5 when the Portal opened, 8 when Stormwind fell, 9–11 during the Second War and 25 in the Third War. Check every "they fought in…" claim against this table.

**Hard limits worth checking:**
- **Forsaken** can have been undead for **at most about 5 years** (plague, 20 ADP) and free-willed for **about 3** (22 ADP). Their age is their living age plus those years. A Forsaken who "has haunted the Undercity for decades" is an error.
- **Night elves** lost their immortality when Nordrassil was spent at the Battle of Mount Hyjal (21 ADP). Ancient night elves are lore-legal, but they're newly mortal.
- **Skyborne** reached Azeroth only now (see section 7), so none of them has any history on Azeroth before 25 ADP.
- **Dwarves and gnomes** live considerably longer than humans; orcs, trolls and tauren live roughly as long as humans. The sources used here give no exact lifespans. Keep ages unremarkable unless the player wants an elder.

## 3. Playable races and classes

★ = new in Forever.

| Race | Faction | Classes |
|---|---|---|
| Human | Alliance | Hunter ★, Mage, Paladin, Priest, Rogue, Warlock, Warrior |
| Dwarf | Alliance | Hunter, Paladin, Priest, Rogue, Shaman ★, Warrior |
| Night elf | Alliance | Druid, Hunter, Priest, Rogue, Warrior |
| Gnome | Alliance | Mage, Priest ★, Rogue, Warlock, Warrior |
| Skyborne (High Order) | Alliance | Druid, Hunter, Mage, Rogue, Warrior |
| Orc | Horde | Hunter, Mage ★, Rogue, Shaman, Warlock, Warrior |
| Tauren | Horde | Druid, Hunter, Shaman, Warrior |
| Troll | Horde | Hunter, Mage, Priest, Rogue, Shaman, Warlock ★, Warrior |
| Undead (Forsaken) | Horde | Mage, Paladin ★, Priest, Rogue, Warlock, Warrior |
| Skyborne (Windshaper) | Horde | Druid, Hunter, Rogue, Shaman, Warrior |

- In the original game, paladins were Alliance-only and shamans Horde-only. In Forever both factions have both, through the Forsaken paladin and the dwarf shaman.
- Skyborne can't be priests. Alliance Skyborne can't be shamans, and Horde Skyborne can't be mages.
- Races added in The Burning Crusade or later (blood elves, draenei, worgen, goblins, pandaren, allied races) **aren't playable**.
- **The six new pairs are new to the lore as well as to the game.** Only the Forsaken paladin has a documented story so far: the *Forsaken Kingdom* campaign introduced it. For the other five, the sources give no official explanation yet. Write the character's own reason for their path (a dwarf who heard the earth speak in the Wetlands excavations, an orc taught by a renegade Kirin Tor tutor) and present it as that one character's story. Don't invent a new official order or claim it as canon. Suggest the player check their class trainer's quest text in-game.

**Off-model concepts.** TRP3's Race and Class fields are free text, so a profile may say "Half-elf" or "Arcanist". Forever's playable bodies are still the nine races above. Concepts the game can't show (a high elf played on a human model, a blood elf in 25 ADP Horde lands) are a community-etiquette question; many RP groups frown on them. Mention this, then follow the player's choice.

## 4. The world in 25 ADP (baseline)

This is the world as the original 2004 game presents it. Forever may change any of it.

**Alliance**
- **Stormwind** (humans): King Varian Wrynn is missing. The boy-king Anduin reigns, with Highlord Bolvar Fordragon as regent. Lady Katrana Prestor holds great sway at court. The Defias Brotherhood (stonemasons left unpaid after rebuilding the city, led by Edwin VanCleef) plague Westfall.
- **Ironforge** (dwarves): King Magni Bronzebeard; the Explorers' League. The Dark Iron dwarves of Blackrock Mountain are enemies.
- **Gnomes**: Gnomeregan has just fallen (troggs, radiation, Thermaplugg's treachery). Survivors under High Tinker Gelbin Mekkatorque are refugees in Ironforge's Tinker Town. Many gnomes are grieving, displaced and furious.
- **Night elves**: new to the Alliance. Darnassus stands atop Teldrassil, a new World Tree grown without the Aspects' blessing. High Priestess Tyrande Whisperwind and Archdruid Fandral Staghelm lead them. Malfurion Stormrage is lost in the Emerald Dream.
- **Theramore** (Dustwallow Marsh): Lady Jaina Proudmoore, who keeps an uneasy peace with Thrall.
- **Dalaran**: the Kirin Tor are rebuilding behind a magical barrier in the Alterac Mountains. Forever adds a City of Dalaran dungeon there; its story wasn't documented at the time of writing.

**Horde**
- **Orgrimmar** (orcs): Warchief Thrall leads a young Horde trying to break from demonic corruption. The Burning Blade cult works against them. Orcs were freed from internment only years ago, and many remember the camps.
- **Thunder Bluff** (tauren): High Chieftain Cairne Bloodhoof. The Grimtotem are dissidents.
- **Darkspear trolls**: Vol'jin leads them from Sen'jin Village in Durotar after their flight from the Broken Isles.
- **Undercity** (Forsaken): Banshee Queen Sylvanas Windrunner. The Royal Apothecary Society works in secret. Forsaken membership of the Horde is an alliance of convenience that many orcs and tauren distrust.

**Neutral powers and threats**
- **The Scourge** holds the Plaguelands. **The Argent Dawn** fights it; Tirion Fordring is an exiled hermit, stripped of his paladinhood. **The Scarlet Crusade** (Scarlet Monastery, Hearthglen, Tyr's Hand) are fanatics who see plague in everyone.
- **Blackrock Mountain**: Ragnaros sleeps beneath it (Molten Core hasn't happened), the Dark Iron dwarves serve him, and the black dragonflight (Nefarian, Onyxia) schemes within.
- **The Steamwheedle Cartel** goblins run neutral ports: Booty Bay, Gadgetzan, Ratchet, Everlook. The **Cenarion Circle** keeps Moonglade. The **Earthen Ring** and the **Kirin Tor** exist and matter to the Skyborne (section 7).
- **Other dangers**: Zul'Gurub and Hakkar's cult; the silithids behind the sealed Scarab Wall in Silithus (Ahn'Qiraj not yet opened); the **Syndicate** in Alterac and Arathi; **Stromgarde**'s ruins; centaur, quilboar, gnolls and kobolds. The world is dangerous.

## 5. Anachronisms: what doesn't exist yet

When a concept relies on any of these, point it out plainly and suggest a period-correct swap. Don't silently "fix" it.

| Anachronism | Why | Period-correct swap |
|---|---|---|
| Blood elves in the Horde; Silvermoon visitors | They join the Horde in The Burning Crusade. In 25 ADP most sin'dorei are isolated in ruined Quel'Thalas, and Kael'thas has gone to Outland | High elf survivors living among humans exist in small numbers |
| Draenei, the Exodar | They haven't crashed on Azeroth yet | — |
| Worgen as Alliance citizens; Gilneans abroad | Gilneas is sealed behind the Greymane Wall | Cursed worgen exist only as monsters (Arugal's in Silverpine and Shadowfang Keep) |
| Goblins in the Horde | Bilgewater joins after the Cataclysm | Steamwheedle goblins, who are neutral |
| Pandaren | Pandaria is hidden | Chen Stormstout is a known wanderer; pandaren are a traveller's tale |
| Player-style death knights, Knights of the Ebon Blade | Death knights still serve the Scourge | An ex-Scourge Forsaken warrior |
| Monks, demon hunters, evokers | Not classes | Warrior or rogue martial artists; warlocks for fel power |
| The Argent **Crusade** | Founded in Northrend (Wrath of the Lich King) | The **Argent Dawn** |
| Outland, Shattrath, the Cenarion Expedition, the Aldor and Scryers | Outland "doesn't factor into the story" in 25 ADP | — |
| Northrend campaigns, the Wrathgate, the Lich King's fall | Arthas is alive on the Frozen Throne | Rumours from the north |
| Deathwing's return, the Cataclysm, a flooded Thousand Needles, a split Barrens | Far future | The original geography |
| Garrosh as Warchief; the Burning of Teldrassil; the Blight of Lordaeron | Far future | Thrall's Horde, Teldrassil intact |
| Molten Core, Blackwing Lair, Ahn'Qiraj, Naxxramas as past victories | They haven't happened | The character can fear or rumour them |
| Floating Dalaran | Dalaran is shielded in the Alterac Mountains | Forever's Alterac Dalaran |
| Personal involvement in Hyjal Summit, Barrow Deeps or other Forever raid stories | Forever's raids unlock 9 December 2026 and their story is unknown at the time of writing | Leave it open |

Lore figures: having served under, met or been rescued by a major lore figure is fine as background. Close friendship, family ties or being their secret right hand needs a clear flag, because the setting is "not every story has to be about saving the world" and other players will notice.

## 6. What Forever adds

- **New race:** Skyborne (section 7). It requires the optional Heroic or Epic pack.
- **Six new race/class pairs** (section 3).
- **New zones:** Mount Hyjal, Riverglades (Krol'dok Stronghold, levels 40–45; Powderfuse Port links it to Gadgetzan), Shen'dralas, Zephras Isle (the Skyborne start). Dalaran is in the Alterac Mountains (City of Dalaran dungeon, levels 28–33). Original zones are expanded.
- **New dungeons and raids:** The Hall of Thanes (Ironforge), Ruins of Lordaeron (Undercity), the Wetlands Excavation Site, Krol'dok Stronghold, The Drowned City; the Barrow Deeps and Hyjal Summit raids. The Darkspear Islands battleground.
- **Camping:** players gather at campfires for tradeskill buffs. It unlocks around level 5 ("The Great Outdoors"). This is a natural RP venue and a good hook for "Currently" text ("Keeps a fire going at the Westfall crossroads most nights").
- **Over 1,000 new quests**, many finishing unfinished 2004 storylines.

Story details for these zones were thin before launch. Name them as places a character is drawn to, rumoured about or has just come back from, without inventing their plot.

## 7. Skyborne (shen'dorei)

**Origins.** The Skyborne descend from the people of Eldre'Thalas (now Dire Maul): Highborne magisters and ordinary night elf citizens. Prince Tortheldrin planned to bind the demon Immol'thar to feed his people's magic addiction. A masked rebel leader, **the Shal'nan**, opposed him. After heavy losses the rebels fled west from Eldre'narr and bargained with **wind spirits**, who lifted their island into **Skywall**, the Elemental Plane of Air. The Shal'nan later handed power to democratically elected provincial councils.

**Decline.** A few centuries ago the wind spirits and the Shal'nan both vanished. The pylons holding the islands aloft began to fail, and the islands lost contact with one another. The islands are **Zephras Isle** (the homeland), **Anvilas** and **Siroccas**. Their towns include **Shen'dramar** and **New Eldre'naar**.

**Now (25 ADP).** Al'Aketh cultists tried to destroy the pylons. The High Order, the Windshapers and the Peacekeepers fought them at the Shrine of Akir. Rohash of Al'Akir's Conclave of Wind warned that the Windlord would strike. So the Skyborne are **leaving Skywall for Azeroth**, and every Skyborne character is a **newcomer**. Culture shock, wonder, homesickness, a homeland in peril and unfamiliar allies are the race's natural RP material.

| Group | Descent | Outlook | Leader | Allegiance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| High Order | Highborne magisters | Recover lost arcane knowledge so the shen'dorei need not depend on spirits; wary of the elementals' motives | Supreme Magister Elaadrin Evengale | Alliance, through the Kirin Tor (Ansirem Runeweaver came to the Shrine of Akir) |
| Windshapers | Kaldorei citizens | Spiritual leaders; trust the elements; tend the standing stones of the Shen'dar Highlands | Grand Skyseer Ayessa Dawnsinger | Horde, through the Earthen Ring (Muln Earthfury came to the Shrine of Akir) |
| Al'Aketh | Shen'dorei who glimpsed Al'Akir through shamanic sight | A cult of Al'Akir; sowed fear to seize territory | High Priestess Lorthuna (fate unknown) | Neutral; the villains |

Other groups: **Nightclaw Druids** (Shadowgale Forest, independent), **Peacekeepers** (militia; Captain Valennia Stormfist), **Empyrean Blades** (the former Shal'nan guard, now Peacekeeper elites), **Rangers of Thendal Grove**. High Elder **Talaanis Shadowsong** leads Zephras Isle's council.

**Culture**
- Elune isn't mentioned on Zephras Isle, and Skyborne can't be priests. Their spiritual life centres on the elements. The Skyborne know very little about Al'Akir, and the air spirits avoided naming him.
- Windstones are their power source and recall crystals store sound. They have magical golems and skycutter airships, but no engineering.
- At coming of age, youths receive an ancient **heirloom**, which passes on when its owner dies. The dead are cremated and their **ashes scattered to the wind**. Many wear **hippogryph feathers** in their hair.
- Crab is everyday food on Zephras Isle and a delicacy on Anvilas and Siroccas.
- They speak Darnassian, Common and Orcish.

**Appearance.** Smaller, paler and more slender than night elves, closer to the high elves of Quel'Thalas, and lighter on their feet. The **Sky-Touched Blessing** gives some of them **blue skin**. It first appeared five generations ago in about one birth in twenty; now about three in ten are blue-skinned, and scholars expect the night elf heritage to fade within two or three generations. Blue skin can carry social meaning in a profile: pride, being seen as "the future", or the grief of older families.

**Names.** A personal name in Darnassian style with a nature or sky surname: Elaadrin Evengale, Ayessa Dawnsinger, Valennia Stormfist, Talaanis Shadowsong. Some share surnames with night elves (Shadowsong, Whisperwind). The sources say any real relation is unknown, so don't claim kinship with night elf lore families.

**Lifespan.** The sources don't state it. Their recorded history spans centuries, so an adult of a few decades to a few centuries is reasonable. Ask the player rather than assume.

**Racial abilities** (flavour for descriptions): Walk on Air and Wind Blessed (both groups); Read Ley Line (High Order); Skysight (Windshapers; one source spells it "Skylight").
