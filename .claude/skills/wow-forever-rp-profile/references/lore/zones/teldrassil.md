# Teldrassil

When Malfurion Stormrage vanished into the Emerald Dream, the archdruid Fandral Staghelm rose to lead the Cenarion Circle in his place and convinced the Circle of Ancients that the time had come to grow a new World Tree and win back the immortality the night elves had lost. In the Veiled Sea they raised Teldrassil, crown of the earth, and built Darnassus in its boughs. But Nozdormu, who had blessed the first World Tree, refused this one, calling the wish arrogant, and the immortality the night elves sought never came.

In time the land atop Teldrassil fell under a sinister presence. Furbolgs and grell in its forests went mad, satyrs appeared among the trees, and the great turtles and threshers that once swam the waters around the tree fled to beach themselves and die on the shores of Darkshore. The source of this corruption remains a mystery, and Malfurion has not returned from the Dream to seek it out.

## Aldrassil

Aldrassil rises at the heart of Shadowglen, a tree grown vast enough to hold a village within its boughs. At its very top, above the reach of the settlement below, Tenaron Stormgrip has made his home, watching over the glen from the crown of the great tree.

## Ban'ethil Barrow Den

Ban'ethil Barrow Den was raised by the night elves as a sacred barrow to hold their sleeping Druids of the Talon in peaceful rest.

That peace is broken now. The corrupted Gnarlpine furbolgs have overrun its Lower and Upper Den, and what was once sanctified ground is no place for a lone night elf to wander.

## Ban'ethil Hollow

A ravine cuts into the land south of the road between Dolanaar and Darnassus, its slopes held by hostile furbolgs who ambush travelers who stray too close. At the back of the hollow lies the Ban'ethil Barrow Den, sunk into the earth. Caution is warranted on the road above.

## Dolanaar

Dolanaar stands in the central-eastern reaches of Teldrassil, southwest of Shadowglen, its roots and rope-bridges woven into the great tree itself. Its central place in the island's paths has long made it a hub of comings and goings, a village that felt the pulse of everything happening around it.

## Fel Rock

North of Dolanaar lies a cave once beautiful, now corrupted. Grells infest its dark hollows, and within dwells the satyr Lord Melenas, whose presence has poisoned the place. The night elf druid Tallonkai Swiftroot watches this corruption with dread, for what was once fair ground is now foreboding.

## Gnarlpine Hold

In the far southwest corner of Teldrassil stands Gnarlpine Hold, home of the Gnarlpine furbolgs.

## Lake Al'Ameth

Lake Al'Ameth spreads wide south of Dolanaar, one of the great waters of Teldrassil. Little marks its shores but a small jetty.

Lately its calm has soured: enraged timberlings haunt the shore, and the lake is no longer safe to walk alone.

## Pools of Arlithrien

South of Darnassus, in western Teldrassil, the Pools of Arlithrien were once peaceful. A moonwell there was abandoned by the night elves when Gnarlpine furbolgs overran the area. The well still stands untended among the pools, now home to hostile wildlife.

## Rut'theran Village

Rut'theran Village lies at the roots of Teldrassil, where the World Tree meets the water, beneath Darnassus far above. Its buildings are humble beside the capital's, but kept exceedingly fresh, as though pride were taken in simplicity rather than size.

Here the kaldorei tend the hippogryph roost, gathering and caring for the eggs and raising the young until they can fly. It is quiet work, done by folk who otherwise labor to keep the village running in support of the city above, asking little recognition for it.

The village is not only labor, though. When the Midsummer Fire Festival comes, its braziers are lit, and the same hands that tend hippogryph eggs and load boats turn instead to celebration.

## Shadowglen

Beneath the boughs of Teldrassil, tucked into the island's northeastern reach, lies a glade that never quite sees full daylight. Shadowglen keeps a perpetual dusk about it, ringed by mountains on every side but one. Only to the south does the stone break, letting a path slip through toward Dolanaar.

At the heart of the glade stands Aldrassil, a great tree whose presence dominates the clearing entirely, as though the whole glen were arranged around it. To the northwest, the ground opens into Shadowthread Cave, a dark seam cut into the hills that ring the glade.

## Shadowthread Cave

Shadowthread Cave lies in the northwest corner of Shadowglen, in the roots of Teldrassil. It has always belonged to the great spiders of the island, but of late they have grown restless and the cave more dangerous.

Deepest within dwells Githyiss the Vile, largest of the brood, the ruin of more than one young Kaldorei who wandered too far in.

## Starbreeze Village

Starbreeze Village once belonged to the night elves, a cluster of small houses southeast of Shadowglen. It stands empty of them now, its houses claimed by the corrupted Gnarlpine furbolgs. Uruson paces its paths, and at its edges lurks Ferocitas the Dream Eater, a beast whose presence alone marks how far the village has fallen from what it once was.

## The Cleft

The Cleft lies northwest of Dolanaar, a den of corrupted Gnarlpine furbolgs led by Ursal the Mauler. They ambush travelers on the road to Darnassus and hold a group of slumbering druids captive within. Moon Priestess Amara and her bodyguards keep watch on the road near the entrance.

## The Oracle Glade

The Oracle Glade lies in Teldrassil's northwest, cradling the first and most powerful of the great tree's moonwells. Here stands the Oracle Tree, its wide trunk rising into boughs shaped like an eagle perched atop a bear. Many whisper that no ordinary growth could take such a form, and that nature magic alone shaped it.

The tree has watched over Teldrassil's growth alongside the druids.

Bloodfeather harpies have nested nearby, threatening the tree, and a small company of Sentinels has been sent to hold them back.

## Wellspring Lake

Wellspring Lake draws its water from a spring deep within Teldrassil itself, its overflow running off as the Wellspring River. Night elven fishermen once favored these quiet waters. Now the timberlings that dwell here have turned corrupted, and nightsabers and spiders have crept into the surrounding land, making what was once a peaceful fishing spot a place of danger.

## Wellspring River

The Wellspring River rises from a spring deep within Teldrassil, flowing from Wellspring Lake north to the tree's edge, where it falls in a great waterfall to the sea below.

Crazed timberlings now stalk its banks, attacking any who come near.
