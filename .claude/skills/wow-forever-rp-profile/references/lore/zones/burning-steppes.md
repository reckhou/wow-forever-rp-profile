# Burning Steppes

The sky over the Burning Steppes never loses its red hue, lit by sporadic eruptions from Blackrock Mountain, and the ground never cools. Two hundred years before the Dark Portal opened, Dark Iron dwarves came here in exile from Ironforge. Their Sorcerer-Thane, Thaurissan, sought to summon elemental minions to win the War of the Three Hammers against the Bronzebeard and Wildhammer clans. What answered was not a minion but Ragnaros the Firelord, once banished by the titans. His arrival melted the mountains around him, raised a volcano at the blast's heart, and destroyed the dwarves who had summoned him. What lay north of that new peak became the Searing Gorge; what lay south, the Burning Steppes, scarred ever since.

Orcs settled the volcano's slopes during the First War and held Blackrock Spire as a stronghold through the Second. The Blackrock orcs returned afterward and remain there still, following the old brutal ways from before Thrall's truce. Black dragons under Nefarian hold the spire's upper levels, and it is said Ragnaros yet broods somewhere beneath it all.

## Blackrock Mountain

Blackrock Mountain was not born of ordinary fire. When the Bronzebeard and Wildhammer armies marched to purge the Dark Iron clan during the War of the Three Hammers, Sorcerer-Thane Thaurissan reached into the world's molten heart and dragged its power up as a weapon. What answered him was Ragnaros. The Firelord's rebirth tore the mountains apart, drowned the surrounding land in fire, and sent both kings turning their armies back rather than face him. Where the Dark Iron kingdom had stood, a volcano now smoldered, with the Burning Steppes to its south and the Searing Gorge to its north. Ragnaros withdrew into the mountain's core and enslaved the surviving Dark Iron dwarves to carve Shadowforge City beneath their own ruined home.

## Blackrock Pass

Blackrock Pass is the threshold between two worlds. On one side lies Redridge, green and quiet. On the other, the Burning Steppes wait, scorched and twisted beyond recognition. A gate stands at the border, marking the line plainly.

To pass through is to leave safety behind in a single step. Those who wander through unprepared find the change brutal: the creatures beyond are far crueler than anything stirring in the mountains behind them.

## Blackrock Stronghold

The stronghold rises at the edge of the Burning Steppes, hard against the shadow of Blackrock Spire, a fortress of black stone held by the Blackrock clan. Outside its walls, in the Fields of Honor, the clan's warriors and their allies muster and drill, kept in line by the lash and voice of the Blackrock Sergeants.

Command of the stronghold falls to General Thorg'izog, who disciplines the troops gathered in the fields and sees that his orders are carried through without question.

## Draco'dar

Draco'dar's hills lie blackened and scorched, given over entirely to the black dragonflight. Their kind moves freely across the ground here, thick enough that none should walk into it without caution and without company.

## Dreadmaul Rock

Dreadmaul Rock is a stronghold of stone paths and hollowed ogre mounds, seat of the Firegut clan. From its heights and tunnels the Fireguts stage their raids southward into the Redridge Mountains, using the rock as their base before falling back into its depths.

## Flame Crest

Flame Crest sits on a bare outcropping in the Burning Steppes, reachable only by a crude bridge. In the small cave at its back lives Kibler, who keeps a talkative parrot.

## Morgan's Vigil

Morgan's Vigil lies in ruin in the southeastern Burning Steppes, its houses burned to the ground and its mage tower crumbled to rubble. Tents stand among the wreckage, sheltering those who remain.

From these tents the Alliance keeps its watch on Blackrock Mountain, striking at the danger closer to its source.

## Ruins of Thaurissan

Once these ashen flats in the Burning Steppes were green, and the city here bore the name of its founder: Thaurissan City, capital of the Dark Iron clan, exiled from Ironforge and led south by Sorcerer-Thane Thaurissan during the War of the Three Hammers.

The Dark Irons were losing that war. Their army had just been broken against the other two clans' strongholds when Thaurissan, desperate to turn the tide, gathered seven of his most trusted dwarves and called down into the powers sleeping beneath the earth for a servant to win the war back. What answered was no servant, but Ragnaros, Elemental Lord of Fire, and his coming split the mountains with a volcano that burned the valley to ash. The city was annihilated, and the surviving Dark Irons were enslaved to the Firelord. Abandoning the ruins, they built a new home beneath Blackrock Mountain, and from the ashes of Thaurissan rose Blackrock Spire and a new Dark Iron nation.

## Slither Rock

Slither Rock is a small cave hidden beyond the Terror Wing Path in the far northeastern Burning Steppes. It shelters the black dragonspawn Cyrus Therepentous, who will aid others against a common foe, though never without rude, derisive comments.

## Terror Wing Path

Terror Wing Path is a valley in the eastern Burning Steppes, given over entirely to the black dragonflight. Whelps and dragonkin nest among its rocks and ash.

## The Pillar of Ash

A tall tower of scorched stone rises at the eastern end of Black Tooth Hovel, its surface blackened as though fire itself had climbed it and stopped. Nothing grows near it, and nothing else here stands so high.
