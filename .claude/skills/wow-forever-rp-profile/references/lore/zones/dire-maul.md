## Dire Maul

Twelve thousand years ago, a covert sect of night elf sorcerers raised Eldre'Thalas to guard Queen Azshara's most prized arcane secrets. Among its Highborne, the arcanists known as the Shen'dralar lived in quiet splendor, and one of them, Millicent Serene, wrought the Fruit of Fertility, a vine whose aura of growth and protection let the whole city flourish.

Cut off from the Well of Eternity after the Great Sundering, the Highborne bound a demon, Immol'thar, and drew their power from his imprisonment. It sustained them for thousands of years, until the cost of holding him grew greater than what he gave. Their leader, Prince Tortheldrin, answered this by turning on his own people, slaughtering many of them to free enough power to keep the rest alive. The survivors retreated deeper into the ruins they once ruled, abandoning the rest of the city to whatever would come to dwell there.

The stone of Eldre'Thalas still stands, arcane energy bound within it by a craft none now remember, holding the shape of a city that fed itself on its own people to survive.
