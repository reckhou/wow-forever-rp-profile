# Azshara

Autumn has held Azshara for ten thousand years, its trees burning orange and red above cliffs that drop into coral where a city still lies. Azshara was once the greatest metropolis the night elves ever raised, and when the War of the Ancients came, most of it went into the sea. A few broken buildings still cling to the cliffside, staring down at their own ruins beneath the water.

Long after, when Illidan Stormrage fled north from Mount Hyjal with his naga, mur'guls and satyrs in tow, he tore through Azshara's coast, wrecking the night elven harbors so that none could follow him to the Tomb of Sargeras. Maiev Shadowsong and her Watchers came behind him, putting down the maddened creatures he left in his wake.

## Bay of Storms

The Bay of Storms lies off the eastern coast of Azshara, its waters known to travelers for the storms that gather there. Along its shore stand Scalebeard's Cave, Hetaera's Clutch, and the Temple of Arkkoran.

## Bear's Head

Bear's Head takes its name from the furbolg villages scattered across the land, small outposts that watch the approaches to Timbermaw Hold and Ursolan farther north.

On a cliff above the villages stands Xylem's Tower, home to Archmage Xylem, who keeps company with several apprentices and associates who study and work within its walls.

## Bitter Reaches

The Bitter Reaches lie in northeastern Azshara, thick with hippogryphs, oozes, and chimaerae. Along the northern edge, mountain giants have driven off the lesser wildlife and claimed the ground for themselves.

## Forlorn Ridge

Forlorn Ridge rises over western Azshara, dominating the land for miles around. Fortifications stand on its slopes in matching pairs, Alliance and Horde works raised within sight of each other -- built for a battle that has never come, and standing empty.

## Haldarr Encampment

The Haldarr Encampment lies thick with satyr in western Azshara, a corrupted host of goat-legged fiends, their fires burning northeast of Shadowsong Shrine and southwest of old Valormok.

## Hetaera's Clutch

Beneath the Bay of Storms off Azshara lies Hetaera's Clutch, a temple, home now to hydras.

## Jagged Reef

The Jagged Reef is the northern coast of Azshara, sandy beach broken again and again by jets of stone and small inlets of the sea. Rocks and shallows hide beneath the surface, making the shore hazardous to travel. Murlocs, hydras, and makrura hold this coast, swimming the shallows or plodding the sand for food.

## Lake Mennar

The waters of Lake Mennar lie still at their center, though the ground on every side belongs to the dragonspawn. Anyone who crosses that perimeter finds the lake itself free of danger, a place of quiet and refreshment amid the guarded shores.

This is one of the few places in the world, outside Northrend, where the blue dragonflight has made itself known. Only Mazthoril in Winterspring compares. Young azure whelplings are a common sight near these waters.

## Legash Encampment

The Legashi satyr hold their encampment in northern Azshara, east of Ursolan.

Here Archmage Xylem once found a grimoire, a discovery that drew notice to this otherwise unremarkable camp of fel-touched creatures.

## Ravencrest Monument

The cliffs at Azshara's southern tip bear the remains of a colossus, though little now remains to justify the word. Only the broken legs still stand, planted above the sea, while the rest of the great figure lies scattered across the stone around them, or has slipped beneath the waves below.

The Spitelash naga hold the ruins now. The place cannot be reached from the water; anyone seeking it must come overland and cross the bridge that leads out past the Ruins of Eldarath, to stand beneath the toppled legs of something that was once, undoubtedly, impressive to behold.

## Rethress Sanctum

Rethress Sanctum stands broken among the Ruined Reaches, southwest of the Ravencrest Monument. No one keeps it now. Murlocs and makrura wander through its fallen stones, the only ones who come here at all.

## Ruins of Eldarath

Before the world was broken, Eldarath rose as one of the great strongholds of the Highborne, a city of arcane learning where the most powerful mages carved runes and tablets into stone and left them standing in public places, so that any who passed might ponder their insights into the arcane.

The city did not fall quietly. During the War of the Ancients, the Kaldorei Resistance made a stand here against the Burning Legion. When the Great Sundering tore the world apart, Eldarath could not endure it -- most of the city crumbled away and sank into the waters of the Bay of Storms below.

What remains clings to the eastern cliffs, a broken cluster of towers long shunned by the night elves who once called it home. Naga have claimed the ruins now, and the warlord Krellian rules what is left from the Temple of Zin-Malor. Among the wreckage, the runic tablets of the old Highborne still stand, their arcane wisdom untouched by the ages since.

## Scalebeard's Cave

A cave on a tiny island in the Bay of Storms bears the name of Scalebeard, a rare old turtle. It is his home, though seldom his resting place, for he prefers to hunt far off in the Great Sea, leaving the den empty.

## Shadowsong Shrine

A ruin-covered hill in western Azshara, the Shadowsong Shrine keeps no clear record of why it bears that name. It shares its name with Jarod Shadowsong, who led the resistance in the War of the Ancients, and his sister Maiev, the warden remembered for holding Illidan Stormrage captive after his betrayal at Mount Hyjal. What either has to do with this hill, no one can say.

What can be said is what walks there. Ghosts of the highborne linger among the stones, hungry for any living thing that draws near.

## Southfury River

The Southfury rises high on Mount Hyjal, spilling from a mountain lake in a fall that drops thunderously into the lowlands. From there it runs wide and swift, cutting the land in two: it marks where Ashenvale gives way to Azshara, and further south where the Barrens ends and Durotar begins. At its mouth the river throws itself over a last cascade into the Great Sea, just northeast of the goblin port of Ratchet.

Bridges cross its length, one linking Azshara to Ashenvale, another joining Durotar to the Barrens.

The river still feeds the fields along its banks, still turns the soil green where it runs.

## Southridge Beach

Southridge Beach lines the southern edge of the Bay of Storms, its sands strewn with ruins of the once-great city of Eldarath. Naga now hold the shore, sharing it with makrura, hydras, murlocs, and the occasional sea giant risen from deeper water.

## Talrendis Point

Talrendis Point stands in southwestern Azshara, its two guard towers watching the border with Ashenvale, and a moonwell still waters the southern ground.

The Point began as nothing more than a small night elven encampment, a watch kept at the forest's edge.

## Temple of Arkkoran

The Temple of Arkkoran once belonged to the worship of Elune, its stones raised on the northeastern shores of Azshara. That sanctity is long gone. The sea giant Arkkoroc now dwells at its summit, guarded by murlocs and makrura that fall upon anyone who draws near. The surrounding shoreline, the Shattered Strand, offers no safer passage, watched over by hydras and sea giants as aggressive as Arkkoroc himself.

## Temple of Zin-Malor

The Temple of Zin-Malor stands among the Ruins of Eldarath, its stones shaped in the likeness of the nearby Temple of Arkkoran, and of the Temple of the Moon later raised in Darnassus. It was built in devotion to Elune, in the days before the night elves turned to arcane magic.

Within it once rested the Crystal of Zin-Malor, a gift meant to carry the Highborne a message: that they had no true need of arcane magic at all. The crystal is gone now, carried off long ago.

The temple has since fallen to the Spitelash tribe of naga. Warlord Krellian rules there now, taking for himself the place of honor once reserved for the worship of the goddess.

## Thalassian Base Camp

The hill above the Legash camps belongs to blood elves, its slopes broken into small fortified stations under Magus Rimtori. She struck a bargain with the Horde, promising a share of whatever her expedition unearthed in exchange for gold and steel to protect it. The bargain did not hold. Rimtori took the funding and gave nothing back, and word of the betrayal has spread until vengeance is said to be owed her and her followers in kind.

Rimtori herself is rarely at the camp, given over to exploring Azshara under a guard of her own choosing, leaving Magister Hawkhelm to hold the hill in her absence. What the two of them are truly after is not gold. On one of the camp's ridges lies a Kaldorei Tome of Summoning, a thing capable of calling demons into the world, and its presence there marks the expedition for what it is: not a hunt for treasure, but something darker dressed as one.

## The Ruined Reaches

The southern coast of Azshara wears its ruin plainly. Cliffs and beaches break into inlets, and the water hides shallows that have caught more hulls than any sailor cares to count. Wrecked ships lie half-buried in sand alongside older, stranger ruins whose builders are gone. Murlocs, hydras, and makrura haunt the shoreline, and even those who know the coast tread carefully along it.

On the western shores, above the beach, a gnomish building stands on a hill, reached only by a steep path from inland. Blimo and Jubie Gadgetspring have made their home and trade here. Upstairs, in their private quarters, sits a third bed. No one knows who it belongs to, or whether anyone sleeps in it at all.

## The Shattered Strand

The Shattered Strand runs along the north side of the Bay of Storms in Azshara, thick with hydras, sea giants, makrura, and murlocs, each holding their own stretch of sand. The Temple of Arkkoran and the Tower of Eldara rise from the shore despite the crowding of claws and tentacles.

Goblins, too, have staked claims along this coast, speculating on the beachfront property amid all the teeth and tide.

## Tower of Eldara

The Tower of Eldara stands on the far northeastern tip of land above the Bay of Storms, its sandy shore patrolled by murlocs, makrura, and hydras. It once belonged to the night elven civilization that spread across this coast, but the tower stands closed and abandoned now.

## Ursolan

Ursolan takes its name from Ursol, the bear demigod revered by the furbolg race. It is now the site of an extensive Timbermaw furbolg settlement, stretching south through the Hold to Bear's Head.

## Valormok

Valormok is a Horde outpost in Azshara, found in the northwest, north of the Haldarr Encampment. The warriors stationed there endure hard times but hold to their hope.

That hope was betrayed from within. Belgrom Rockmaul had sent Magus Rimtori, a blood elf, out with a company of orc warriors to find riches and powerful artifacts in the region. Rimtori found what she sought, and rather than share the discovery she turned on the warriors who had escorted her, killing them and claiming the spoils for herself. Ag'tor Bloodfist, who leads the camp, will not let the slaughter go unanswered. He seeks warriors to avenge his fallen and Rockmaul's wounded pride, and to see Magus Rimtori dead in her camp nearby.
