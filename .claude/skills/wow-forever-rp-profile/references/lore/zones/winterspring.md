# Winterspring

Snow lies unbroken across Winterspring, its beauty undisturbed even where demons have overrun the southern reaches. It was once home to many of the blue dragonflight, among them Haleh, consort to Malygos.

Ten thousand years ago the Highborne raised the city of Kel'Theril here, after stealing the Crystal of Zin-Malor from a temple in Eldarath. They could not resist testing its power, and when it shattered, the curse it loosed devoured them. The Great Sundering finished what the crystal began, and Kel'Theril fell to ruin. Its dead never left. Later, high elves came from Quel'Thalas seeking the crystal's shards, and the same madness took them. Now their ghosts walk the ruins beside the ones they came to study.

Goblins hold Everlook in the valley's heart, and the night elves keep their own watch here still, guarding a homeland older than either of them.

## Ban'Thallow Barrow Den

Behind Starfall Village in Winterspring lies the Ban'Thallow Barrow Den, a warren of tunnels holding blessed relics treasured by the village.

## Darkwhisper Gorge

Darkwhisper Gorge lies in the southern reaches of Winterspring.

When the Burning Legion's army broke upon Mount Hyjal in the Third War and Archimonde fell, the demons that survived did not vanish from the world. They fled into Darkwhisper Gorge and took refuge there, remnants of a host that had nearly unmade the world left to fester in the dark.

## Dun Mandarr

Dun Mandarr sits near an icy cavern in south-eastern Winterspring, its name betraying dwarven hands. Ferocious moonkin overrun the camp now, and little else remains of whoever raised it.

## Everlook

Everlook stands where Winterspring's trade routes cross, a goblin post of the Steamwheedle Cartel, its laboratory tucked among the buildings.

The Argent Dawn sent Gregor Greystone here, to speak for their interests before the Cartel's local governance.

## Frostfire Hot Springs

Steam rises from a chain of pools at the far western edge of Winterspring, water too warm for the frozen land around it. No one has explained why the springs burn hot beneath the snow and ice. Donova Snowden, camped nearby, wonders if some demonic taint seeping north from Darkwhisper Gorge might have found its way here to warm the stone.

The pool nearest Timbermaw Hold has never come free of the Winterfall tribe's grip. They hold it still, and the furbolgs are not inclined to share the warmth they've claimed.

## Frostsaber Rock

At the top of Frostsaber Rock stands Rivern Frostwind, keeper of the bond between the frostsabers and those who would ride them. He teaches friends of the Alliance who earn it how to tame and ride the great white cats that prowl the icy stone below.

## Frostwhisper Gorge

Frostwhisper Gorge cuts through southern Winterspring, its floor and ridges broken with night elven ruins. Among these old stones the Frostmaul giants and their towering Preservers dwell.

## Ice Thistle Hills

The Ice Thistle Hills rise southeast of Everlook, home to the Ice Thistle yeti, who den in a cave climbing up the hillside and range through the slopes around it. Their thick fur and pale horns are prized by those who brave the cold to take them.

Icecaps grow across the open slopes, and inside the cave veins of thorium run through the rock. Rarest of all, at the very lip of the cave, a black lotus is sometimes said to bloom, fragile and strange amid the yeti's ground.

## Lake Kel'Theril

The frozen waters of Lake Kel'Theril lie still at the heart of Winterspring, ringed by the ruins that share its name. The ruins are old Highborne work, and those who once dwelled there did not leave when their halls fell. Their spirits linger still, tormented, drifting across the ice and broken stone.

## Mazthoril

Deep in the ice of Winterspring, near Lake Kel'Theril, the cavern of Mazthoril opens beneath the earth, cold and vast and thick with magic. Here dwells a brood of blue dragons, sworn to Malygos, and here their matriarch keeps her solitary watch.

Haleh rarely takes any form the world would call true, showing herself only as a high elf standing at the cavern's uppermost reach, reached through a rune of passage set into the floor far below. Few who are not of her own kind ever see her face.

Around her she has set a guard worthy of her reclusiveness. Four powerful drakes answer to her alone. Spellmaw walks a slow, unbroken circuit through a third of Winterspring's lower reaches, while Manaclaw and Scryer stand over the runes at the cavern's rear like sentries over a locked door.

## Moon Horror Den

A short walk northeast of Everlook's gates, the ground rises toward a small cave known as the Moon Horror Den, home to fierce Raging Owlbeasts. A mountain stands between the den and Winterfall Village to the south, close as the two places seem, so that no direct path connects them, only a long ride around the high ground.

## Owl Wing Thicket

The caves of Owl Wing Thicket cut through the southern reaches of Winterspring, home to owlbeasts gone berserk, moontouched, and crazed. It is said the ancient Guardians of Elune tell here how the moonkin first came into the world, their tale kept by Ranshalla.

## Starfall Village

Starfall Village stands along the road through Winterspring, the only place in that cold country where the Alliance keeps a foothold. A night elven lodge and a smaller house make up the settlement, and nearby lies the Ban'Thallow Barrow Den, sunk into the earth like the burial mounds it takes its name from.

## The Hidden Grove

The Hidden Grove lies sunk within a crater in Winterspring, walled in on every side by sheer cliffs. Only a single cave, cut into the southeastern ridge, offers a way through, and dropping from the heights above is a path few would dare.

Once this hollow held nothing more fearsome than white hares, thriving undisturbed in its shelter. That has since changed. The grove is now claimed by moonkin, hostile to any who enter, and their presence has sealed the place as surely as its cliffs ever did.

## The Ruins of Kel'Theril

The Ruins of Kel'Theril lie broken on the edges of the lake that bears its name, all that remains of a great night elven city. It fell during the Great Sundering, when the world itself was torn apart, and it has never risen again.

What lingers there now is grief made restless. The Highborne who died when Kel'Theril fell did not pass on; their spirits remain among the fallen columns of the northern ruins, tormented, unable to let go of what they lost. High elven ghosts haunt the eastern section, among them betrayed rangers and an archmage.

A crystal called Zin-Malor is bound up with the fall of this place, tied to a destruction that swallowed generation after generation of elves here. That story belongs to Kel'Theril itself, to the fallen stone and the ghosts who still walk among it.

## Timbermaw Hold

Timbermaw Hold lies in the tunnels of northern Felwood, the passage that opens onto Moonglade and Winterspring, and it is the last stronghold of the Timbermaw tribe of furbolgs.

When the corruption of the Burning Legion crept into the sacred forests of Kalimdor, the furbolgs of that land fell beneath it, twisted into something feral and cruel. The Timbermaw tribe alone escaped that fate, holding to their tunnels while the forest they loved was turned into a festering perversion of itself around them. Even after the source of the corruption was destroyed, the taint on the woods did not lift.

The Timbermaw remain wary of all strangers, fearing they will be mistaken for their corrupted kin and cut down before they can prove otherwise. Yet they know they cannot endure alone, and to those who earn their trust they are generous: they hold to the old shamanistic ways and share freely what healing knowledge they keep. What they want most is an end to their corrupted brethren's suffering, and a cure for the wounds Felwood has taken.

## Timbermaw Post

Timbermaw Post keeps a name that no longer fits. The camp once belonged to the Timbermaw tribe, but it now answers to the corrupted Winterfall furbolgs, who hold it completely, southeast of the Frostfire Hot Springs and west of Lake Kel'Theril.

## Winterfall Village

Winterfall Village climbs the eastern slopes of Winterspring, near Everlook, its furbolgs long corrupted. At its summit sits a small cave, the seat of High Chief Winterfall, who rules the settlement from there.
