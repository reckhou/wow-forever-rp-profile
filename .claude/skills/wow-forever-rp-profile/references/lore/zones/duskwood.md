# Duskwood

A blanket of unnatural night lies over Duskwood, and it has never lifted. What was once a fair stretch of Elwynn Forest is now a cursed, dying land: the trees grew gnarled beneath the black fog, the dead rose from their graves, and worgen came slinking in with the darkness itself, drawn by the workings of the Scythe of Elune. Ogres pressing in from Deadwind Pass have only deepened the misery.

Stormwind's protection long ago ceased to mean anything here, and the people who remain have learned to hold their ground without it. Their militia, the Night Watch, patrols the roads and guards Darkshire against whatever moves in the shadows -- skeletal warriors and ghouls, ferocious worgen, and Defias men besides.

At the wood's heart lies the crater of a long-dead volcano, cloaked in a twilight gentler than the dark around it. Within it stands one of the Great Trees, its roots reaching toward the Emerald Dream, and the sleeping druids' influence colors everything that grows nearby. Beyond it stretch abandoned farmsteads and the sprawling graves of Raven Hill Cemetery, filled year by year since the darkness fell.

## Addle's Stead

Addle's Stead lies abandoned among the fields of southwestern Duskwood, one of many farmsteads left to rot when darkness crept over the land. The barn stands empty, the farmhouse silent, and only Forlorn Spirits linger now, said to be the shades of dead Defias Brotherhood who once claimed the place.

The Defias took Addle's Stead entirely, creeping through the overgrown field, hiding in the barn, stalking the farmhouse rooms.

## Beggar's Haunt

The tower called Beggar's Haunt stands abandoned above the road into Deadwind Pass, northeast of Manor Mistmantle. Raised as a scout post for the Stormwind Guard, it now serves the Forsaken, whose Deathstalker Zraedus and Apothecary Faustin work experiments within its walls.

A path winds down to a small pool fed by a waterfall, where an unmarked grave rests beneath two great statues carved like those at the Dark Portal. Near the tower lies another small cemetery, holding the grave of an Unknown Soldier.

## Brightwood Grove

Brightwood Grove lies just west of Darkshire, its few buildings long since fallen to ruin. What remains of the place belongs now to the Nightbane worgen, who prowl the dark fields in packs, raising crude camps among the wreckage and falling upon anyone who wanders too close.

## Darkshire

Darkshire was not always so named. It rose long ago as Grand Hamlet, a small woodland town built up as Stormwind's people spread into the nearby lands. But the land itself never recovered from what happened at Karazhan. When the Guardian Medivh was slain in his tower there, the fel energy loosed by his death crept into the surrounding forest, twisting it into the thorned, lightless place it remains. Brightwood became Duskwood, and Grand Hamlet took the truer name of Darkshire.

The town's militia, the Night Watch, exists because Stormwind abandoned it. When Lady Katrana Prestor's schemes in Stormwind emptied the kingdom's garrisons from the forest, the townsfolk armed themselves rather than wait for soldiers who were never coming. Formed and trained entirely by the people of the forest, they have since held the roads against wolves, walking corpses, and worse, with no army left to call upon but themselves.

## Dawning Wood Catacombs

Beneath Raven Hill Cemetery, the earth opens into the Dawning Wood Catacombs, a warren of tunnels twisting deep enough that few who enter alone come out again. Two ways lead down. The eastern passage opens into a den of ghouls, diseased and ferocious. The western passage opens into a hall of skeletons, standing sentinel over the tombs of the dead they once were.

## Forlorn Rowe

A hill on the northern edge of Raven Hill Cemetery, the Forlorn Rowe lies wrapped in a darkness that clings closer than the shadow of the surrounding trees. Here stands the home of Morbent Fel, a necromancer who bends the forest's undead to his will through evil arcane magic. Only a single weapon of great power could hope to still him.

## Manor Mistmantle

Manor Mistmantle stands in northeastern Duskwood, its grounds rotten, its gardens gone to ruin. It was once the home of Stalvan Mistmantle, a schoolteacher and tutor who became an agent of the undead, leaving ghouls to shamble through the property in his place. Amid the corruption, one thing still grows: a single flower called the Tear of Tilloa, the only sign of life left in a garden otherwise given over to rot.

## Raven Hill

The houses of Raven Hill stand empty in western Duskwood, given up to rot and dark. A few souls held on regardless: Jitters, and Bliztik, a goblin who kept out of sight on an upper floor.

The town is haunted by the Unseen, unseen in more than name, moving through its empty rooms.

## Raven Hill Cemetery

Raven Hill Cemetery sprawls north of the town it takes its name from, grown far beyond its old bounds since the corruption of Duskwood took hold. Undead now walk among the stones, and massive spiders and gangs of grave robbers work over the graves undisturbed, driving off anyone who stumbles into their business.

In the cemetery's northwest corner stands Forlorn Rowe, home of the necromancer Morbent Fel, whose dark work stirs the dead of the cemetery.

That name, Ladimore, marks the cemetery's oldest grief. Morgan Ladimore returned from the Third War to find his wife and children already buried here. Grief broke him; he murdered three of the cemetery's attendants over their graves. Now the undead Mor'Ladim patrols the grounds, and death comes swiftly and unseen to anyone he finds unwary among the headstones.

## Roland's Doom

Roland's Doom is a mine in southeastern Duskwood, east of the Rotting Orchard. The night elven priestess Velinde Starsong carried the Scythe of Elune into these hills before she vanished, and the relic was lost in the mine. The man Jitters found it there, and its finding drew more worgen down upon Duskwood and Darkshire.

The mine now belongs to Gutspill and Nefaru of the Nightbane pack, whose worgen range as far as the Rotting Orchard.

## The Darkened Bank

The Darkened Bank runs along the northern shore of Duskwood, from the western edge of the land east to the bridge joining it to Redridge. At its western end stands a small camp where the refugees Sven Yorgen and Lars have taken shelter, driven to the water's edge by whatever has made the rest of Duskwood too dangerous to hold.

## The Hushed Bank

The Hushed Bank runs along the western river of Duskwood, from where Westfall, Elwynn Forest, and Duskwood meet, south toward Stranglethorn Vale. A covered bridge crosses the river here, joining Duskwood to Westfall.

At the northern corner of the bank, the brothers Lars and Sven Yorgen keep a camp.

Wolves and spiders range the bank among the trees.

## The Rotting Orchard

The orchard lies in southern Duskwood, its trees gone black with rot, its buildings fallen to ruin between the Yorgen Farmstead and the Tranquil Gardens Cemetery.

The worgen of the Nightbane pack have made it theirs. Shadow Weavers work their magic amid the ruined buildings while Stalkers wait unseen in the dark between the trees.

To stand here now is to stand in a place that has stopped being a farm and become a hunting ground.

## The Yorgen Farmstead

The Yorgen Farmstead lies abandoned in southern Duskwood, its fields gone to rot. Sven Yorgen once worked this land, but now keeps to a small camp on the Darkened Bank. The farmstead itself has since fallen into the hands of the Defias Brotherhood, whose assassins move through the fields unseen, striking only when it is too late.

## Tranquil Gardens Cemetery

The Tranquil Gardens Cemetery lies southeast of Darkshire, its name now a bitter joke. The dead here do not rest, though they are weaker than the undead found elsewhere in Duskwood, wandering among the headstones and the ruined church at the graveyard's heart. The Insane Ghoul haunts this ground, sometimes within the broken church, sometimes among the graves. Just north stands Blind Mary's house.

## Twilight Grove

A great tree stands unnamed at the heart of the Twilight Grove, deep in Duskwood, beside a moonwell and an old night elf gazebo. Beneath its boughs lies one of only four portals into the Emerald Dream, sister to those hidden in Feralas, the Hinterlands, and Ashenvale.

The grove's portal did not go unguarded. Four great dragons, once proud lieutenants of Ysera, Aspect of Dreams, kept watch here as her chosen protectors of the Dream. But the Emerald Nightmare corrupted them in turn, twisting guardians into destroyers who wanted nothing but death, and it fell to Alliance and Horde alike to take up arms against the very dragons who had once stood sentinel over this place.

## Vul'Gol Ogre Mound

Southern Duskwood dips into a small basin where the road splits toward Stranglethorn Vale, and where farmhouses once stood before the ogres came. The Splinter Fist ogres hold it now, and it takes its name from their leader, Zzarc'Vul, who rules the mound from within it.

The ruined farmhouses are the only sign that people lived here before the ogres took the basin for their own. Whatever became of those families, the ogres left nothing behind but broken walls.

The Night Watch does not spare its own for this place, judging the threat here less urgent than others pressing on Duskwood, and so it has been left to outsiders to thin the ogres' numbers when needed. Among the ogres' plunder was a crate of tools and herbs stolen from the necromancer Abercrombie, carried off into the mound after they overran him. And Zzarc'Vul himself wears a monocle that a researcher once coveted for his own studies, a small vanity on a brutal creature.
