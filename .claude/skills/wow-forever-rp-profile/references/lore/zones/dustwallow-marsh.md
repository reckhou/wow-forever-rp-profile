# Dustwallow Marsh

The mosquitoes never stop here, and neither does the mud. Trees knot their fronds so thick over the water that sunlight comes down broken, and crocolisks and murlocs wait in the brackish shallows where the sea meets the swamp. In the south, black dragons gather in such numbers that folk simply call it the Wyrmbog.

Jaina Proudmoore made the first true home of this wilderness, leading her people here after the battle at Mount Hyjal to found Theramore on its rocky island. Her city's walls gleam, but they also contain her: beyond the scattered checkpoints on the road to the Barrens, the marsh remains as wild as ever, and her garrison has thinned with quiet desertions no one can explain.

It was here, too, that the peace between Horde and Alliance broke for good. Admiral Daelin Proudmoore brought the Kul Tiras fleet against the Horde in the marsh, and the skirmish that followed ended in his death, sealing the truce into open enmity.

Brackenwall Village stands where the Stonemaul ogres opened their gates to the Horde. Beyond it the road to the Barrens lies empty and falling into disrepair, watched by nothing but the raptors and spiders that have always owned this ground.

## Alcaz Island

Alcaz Island rises out of the sea off Dustwallow Marsh, thick with a tropical green that has no business growing so close to the marsh's grey mud, as though some older, warmer power once claimed this ground and never let go. The naga of the Strashaz hold it, myrmidons and sirens thick along its shores.

Yet during the war against Ahn'Qiraj, Doctor Weavil came to the island by flying machine and settled its hilltop with his minions, turning a stretch of the shore into a workshop of technological invention.

## Beezil's Wreck

A goblin zeppelin bound south with supplies went down in the deepest reaches of Dustwallow Marsh, east of Tabetha's Farm. Its hull lies broken in the mud, cargo scattered among the wreckage, Seaforium Boosters still lying where they fell.

## Bloodfen Burrow

A cave in south-western Dustwallow Marsh shelters an unusual number of Bloodfen raptors. Outside the entrance lie the remains of some settlement, torn apart by the creatures, its people now lost to the marsh mud. Somewhere within, one part of Cortello's Riddle waits among the wreckage.

## Bluefen

Bluefen lies in northern Dustwallow Marsh, its low ground thick with reeds and standing water. Spiders, crocolisks, raptors, and marsh birds move through the fen unchecked, and Theramore infiltrators pass through it unseen among the reeds.

## Brackenwall Village

Brackenwall Village stands among huts and ogre mounds in northwest Dustwallow Marsh, home to the Stonemaul ogres and sworn to the Horde. It was not always their home. The Stonemaul once held a village to the south, until the black dragonflight fell upon it and burned it to ruin. Overlord Mok'Morokk, who had taken up leadership after Rexxar, led the survivors north and raised Brackenwall in its place.

Mok'Morokk built his rule on staying put. When the ogre Draz'Zilb learned the attackers had been Onyxia's brood and brought the truth to him, Mok'Morokk would not act. Brackenwall was safe, no black dragons dwelt here, and that was enough for him. Draz'Zilb will not let the matter rest, and quietly weighs what it would take to see the Overlord fall and the Stonemaul led back to what they have lost.

## Darkmist Cavern

A small mine sits in the northwest of Dustwallow Marsh, long since given over to spiders grown terrifyingly large in the dark. The marsh folk call them the Darkmist Spiders, and the cavern Darkmist in turn, though none can say which name came first, the place or the creatures that claimed it.

Outside the mine's mouth stand the ruins of small houses, half-sunk in the wet ground, all that remains of some settlement long forgotten.

## Dreadmurk Shore

The shore between Theramore Isle and Northwatch Hold lies thick with the Mirefin murloc tribe, who claim the coastline and its scattered islands as their own. Sea turtles and other creatures share the waters around them.

## Dustwallow Bay

Dustwallow Bay is part of the Great Sea, reaching into the eastern coast of Dustwallow Marsh west of Theramore Isle. On a small island off the bay, in Tidefury Cove, the fisherman Nat Pagle keeps his solitary watch over the water.

## Emberstrife's Den

A wide cave opens into the reeking Wyrmbog, home to the black drake Emberstrife. Here he tests young black dragonlings through trials of endurance and cruelty, for only those who prove worthy may go on to enter Onyxia's Lair.

## Foothold Citadel

Foothold Citadel stands at the heart of Theramore Isle, headquarters of the Theramore Guard.

It was here that Daelin Proudmoore met his end, when Thrall's forces stormed Theramore, led into the keep by Rexxar and his companions. The admiral who defied his daughter Jaina's peace with the orcs fell within these walls.

## Lost Point

Lost Point, a decrepit scout tower once raised by Theramore in western Dustwallow Marsh, no longer serves its builders. Theramore deserters hold it now, led by Balos Jacken, who knows of Private Hendel's hand in the kidnapping of a person of great importance.

## North Point Tower

North Point Tower watches over northern Dustwallow Marsh beside a well-traveled road, held by a sizable contingent of Theramore guards. Since deserters seized Lost Point, this tower stands as Theramore's farthest outpost.

## Onyxia's Lair

The passage into Onyxia's lair is shaped like the broodmother's own gaping jaw. Beyond it the path is lined with magma, the ground cracked and glowing with fire from below. The stone ceiling above groans and shifts, loosing constant falls of rock, and the walls themselves weep lava where the heat cannot be held back. Onyxia carved this place herself, deep beneath the Dragonmurk, the dismal swamp within Dustwallow Marsh.

She is not like her kin. Where her father rules through brute force and her brother shapes twisted abominations to do his bidding, Onyxia works through whispers and charm, delighting in the tangled politics of mortals and bending them quietly to her own purposes. For a time she wore a stolen name, calling herself Katrana Prestor, and in that guise wound herself into the heart of Stormwind's court, working to unravel it from within. It was Marshal Windsor who saw through the mask and named her for what she was. Exposed, she fled the city and returned to her fiery hall beneath the marsh, where she remains, guarded still by her own kin, the black dragonflight.

## Sentry Point

Sentry Point rises just across the bridge from Theramore, a scout tower built by the citadel to watch the road below. It stands unbroken, keeping its silent vigil over all who pass toward the city.

## Shady Rest Inn

The Shady Rest Inn once stood near the border between Dustwallow Marsh and the Southern Barrens, a human-built waystop.

It burned to the ground without warning. Two men might have named the culprits, Paval Reethe and Lieutenant Caldwell, but both were killed before either could speak the truth, and the burning remained unsolved.

## Stonemaul Ruins

Stonemaul Village once stood in Dustwallow Marsh, home to the ogres of the Stonemaul clan, until the warlord Kor'gall, having seized the ancient orcish axe Serathil, took the clan and ruled it through brutality. It was here that Rexxar came and was refused entry, fighting his way in. Kor'gall offered him a place among the clan if he could survive the Gauntlet, a valley thick with deadly beasts. Rexxar endured it and returned, and was made Stonemaul. When Kor'gall refused to lend the clan to the Horde's war against Kul Tiras, Rexxar challenged him for leadership and killed him, then led the Stonemaul to war himself. Afterward he named Mok'Morokk to rule in his place and departed.

Mok'Morokk did not rule as Rexxar had. He took from his people rather than serving them, and when Onyxia's brood descended on the village and claimed it, he led the survivors away to safety and was content to remain there, far from the dragons, rather than fight to take the old home back.

The ruins stand now within Dragonmurk, infested with Firemane dragonspawn.

## Swamplight Manor

Swamplight Manor is a modest house on Witch Hill in Dustwallow Marsh, home to the cook "Swamp Eye" Jarl.

In the garden outside, a pile of turned dirt hides something Jarl's cooking never explains: a report buried by an orcish spy, left beneath the soil to keep its secret from prying eyes.

## The Den of Flame

The Den of Flame was raised by the Stonemaul ogres as their mound, its stonework still bearing their craft. It lies west of the Stonemaul ruins, in marshland claimed by Onyxia.

The ogres are gone from it now. Black dragonkin have overrun the den, holding it as a nest of the black dragonflight. Only the old stonework and the Stonemaul banner, left standing within, recall its first builders.

## The Dragonmurk

In the southern reaches of Dustwallow Marsh lies a stretch of fetid ground called the Dragonmurk, thick with the whelps and spawn of the black dragonflight. Nearby stand the Den of Flame and the Stonemaul Ruins, both likewise overrun with black dragonspawn.

## The Quagmire

The Quagmire is the sodden heart of Dustwallow Marsh, running between Lost Point and Dustwallow Bay, hemmed by Brackenwall Village to the north and the Dragonmurk to the south.

Crocolisks lurk in its black water, oozes and spiders crawl its reeds, and Grimtotem tauren hold ground here, far from any kin who would claim them. It is better crossed than lingered in.

## Theramore Isle

After the Third War, Jaina Proudmoore led the survivors of Lordaeron across the sea to Kalimdor. On a rocky island off Dustwallow Marsh, they raised Theramore, a walled city meant to shelter what remained of the Alliance in a strange land.

The city stands ringed by a rectangular wall studded with cannon towers, broken only at the harbor, which two scout towers guard instead. At its heart rises Jaina's Tower, the Citadel, tallest building in Theramore, its many wooden-floored stories linked by winding stairs and warded against attack, holding Jaina's private quarters, her library, and the city's small throne room. Near it stands the Foothold Citadel, the guard quarters, where soldiers drill against practice dummies and recruits spar under the watch of priests ready to call on the Light the moment someone is hurt.

The marsh gives poor soil and poor timber, and Theramore's sailors grumble that the oaks the orcs cut above Orgrimmar are far better than anything Dustwallow offers. Even so, humans and orcs trade fish for surplus Durotarian crops, and for now the city's crime is low and its people, for the most part, at peace.

## Tidefury Cove

Tidefury Cove lies at the end of Dustwallow Bay, south of Theramore Isle, its waters dotted with small islands and hidden reefs. It is treacherous water to sail. Two ships lie broken upon its shallows, given over now to roaming makrura.

Among its islets sits Nat's Landing, home to the fisherman Nat Pagle.

## Witch Hill

Swamplight Manor stands at the center of Witch Hill, home to "Swamp Eye" Jarl. The Mirefin murlocs keep their camps nearby in the mire.

## Wyrmbog

South of the Dragonmurk in Dustwallow Marsh, the mire thickens into the Wyrmbog. Black dragons make their home here, and the earth opens into two hidden places: the entrance to Emberstrife's Den, and the lair of Onyxia herself.
