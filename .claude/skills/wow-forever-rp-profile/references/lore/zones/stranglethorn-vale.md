# Stranglethorn Vale

Stranglethorn Vale is a vast jungle south of Duskwood. Jungle trolls patrol this steaming rainforest, and naga hunt along its coasts, while stranglethorn vines and beasts make travel dangerous.

The vale was once the heart of the Gurubashi Empire, whose great cities stood throughout the region. After the empire's fall, those cities crumbled into ruin, though trolls still dwell among them. Zul'Gurub, in the northeast, was once the Gurubashi capital.

Booty Bay was originally a small port town settled by humans, until it was overrun by trolls. The goblins of the Steamwheedle Cartel later drove the trolls from the cove and claimed it for their own, building it into their center of operations in the Eastern Kingdoms.

In the north stands a compound raised by Colonel Kurzen's expedition. There the colonel's mind was seized by an ogre mage, Mai'Zoth, who used an artifact called the Mind's Eye to bend Kurzen's will and command his men. A number of Kurzen's soldiers rebelled against their possessed commander and fled west, founding a rebel camp in defiance of him.

## Bal'lal Ruins

Broken columns rise near the Vile Reef, north of Grom'gol Base Camp, all that remains of a settlement raised long ago by trolls. Little of what it once was still stands. Now Bloodscalp trolls hold the ruin, dwelling among the stones their ancestors left behind.

## Balia'mah Ruins

The stones of Balia'mah are old troll work, standing east of the river below Mosh'Ogg. The Skullsplitter trolls hold them now, having made a home of what their ancestors raised.

## Bloodsail Compound

The Bloodsail Buccaneers keep a loose camp along the western coast of Stranglethorn Vale, tucked toward the middle stretch of the shore. No banners of order fly here, no discipline of a proper port -- only the scattered gathering of pirates who call the place their own.

## Booty Bay

Booty Bay sits in a blue lagoon at the southern tip of Stranglethorn Vale, entered on foot through the bleached jaws of a giant shark. Life is cheap here, and no one is to be trusted; it is a rogue's paradise, thick with informants and traders of every stripe.

Before it wore that name, the cove was called Blackwater Cove, a small human port until jungle trolls overran it. The goblins of the Steamwheedle Cartel drove the trolls back from the shore and claimed the cove for themselves, before the First War was ever fought, and built there their center of operations in the Eastern Kingdoms.

The Blackwater Raiders run the port now, in close company with the Cartel, and its doors stand open to any traveler regardless of banner. Baron Revilgaz rules from the top of the inn, alongside Fleet Master Seahorn of the Raiders, and he has spent freely to hire swords against the Bloodsail Buccaneers, pirates who have plotted, unsuccessfully, to seize the bay itself.

## Crystalvein Mine

Crank Fizzlebub called them Singing Crystals, unique to Stranglethorn and worth a fortune. They seam the rock of Crystalvein Mine near Zul'Mamwe, and the basilisks nesting there eat them raw, hardening their hides to stone. The Venture Company pushed in hoping to strip the mine, but the basilisks have beaten back every attempt. Outside the entrance, trolls and humans stand crystallized where they fell.

## Grom'gol Base Camp

Grom'gol Base Camp sits fortified on the southwestern coast of Stranglethorn, its palisades raised against the jungle trolls of the coast, against naga rising from the surf, and against the Alliance making their way in from Booty Bay and Duskwood. Commander Aggro'gosh holds the camp, keeping it as the safe link in the chain of supply running to Stonard in the Swamp of Sorrows.

## Gurubashi Arena

The Gurubashi Arena stands in the northwestern reach of Stranglethorn, a relic raised in the days when the Gurubashi Empire ruled vast tracts of the southern Eastern Kingdoms.

In older times the fights held here were not sport but slaughter. The sand went muddy with troll blood as combatants fought to the death. Now Short John Mithril oversees matches of a gentler sort, offering fame and plunder rather than death. Old stories still warn that whoever finds a blood-red amulet at midnight will be devoured by a demon before daybreak.

## Jaguero Isle

Off the coast of the Wild Shore lies Jaguero Isle, jungle held by panthers and gorillas that answer to no one. Chief among them is King Mukla, an enormous ape, who rules the isle and keeps a captive Princess Poobah, a tauren held against her will.

Half-buried along the shore are bottles washed up from the sea, one holding a folded note.

## Janeiro's Point

Janeiro's Point is a small island off Booty Bay, marked by a great stone goblin with arms flung wide, raised as a beacon to welcome ships into harbor. No one now recalls whose likeness it bears. The sea giant Mok'rash the Cleaver has made the island his abode.

## Kal'ai Ruins

The Kal'ai Ruins lie half-swallowed by the sea in Stranglethorn Vale, ancient troll stonework worn by tide and age. The Murkgill tribe of murlocs now nests among the fallen columns, having claimed the drowned halls for its own.

## Kurzen's Compound

Kurzen's Compound stands east of the Rebel Camp, seat of Kurzen's Expedition. Colonel Kurzen leads it, though madness has taken him. What he became is not spoken of, only the result: the men under him split. Those who would not follow him further broke away and became the jungle fighters of the Rebel Camp, and the compound has been at war with them since, men once of one command now killing each other in the trees.

Kurzen himself is no longer often seen among his men, said to keep to the Stockpile at the back of the compound, where the fighters store their arms. Near the stables, panthers and Stranglethorn tigers are held in cages, broken and trained into war beasts for the expedition's use.

## Lake Nazferiti

Lake Nazferiti's waters run thick with frenzies and crocolisks, and a large Venture Company base crowds its shore.

The lake is fed by the Nazferiti river, thought by some to be the longest in the southern Eastern Kingdoms, rising at a waterfall in the Redridge Mountains, perhaps further still in Deadwind Pass.

## Mistvale Valley

Fog settles low over Mistvale Valley, thick enough to blur the tree line in the southern reaches of Stranglethorn Vale. The great Mistvale gorillas make their home here, moving through the mist between the trees, undisturbed rulers of this quiet, damp hollow.

## Mizjah Ruins

The Mizjah Ruins lie southeast of Grom'gol Base Camp, held by Mosh'Ogg Brutes and their Witch Doctors. From these broken stones the ogres strike at the caravans bound for Stonard, ambushing the supply lines and vanishing back into the ruins before any answer can be made.

## Mosh'Ogg Ogre Mound

The Mosh'Ogg Ogre Mound lies south of Zul'Gurub, its dark hollows home to the Mosh'Ogg tribe of ogres. Deep within, their leader, the ogre mage Mai'Zoth, holds a powerful relic known as the Mind's Eye, and it is from these depths that he rules the mound today.

## Nek'mani Wellspring

The Nek'mani Wellspring rises in the cliffs above the Southern Savage Coast, on the western edge of Stranglethorn.

Scholars of every race claim the waters were made sacred for their people alone, and none has ever settled it.

## Nesingwary's Expedition

Hemet Nesingwary, a dwarven master hunter, led an expedition into Stranglethorn, drawn by the wild game of the jungle. He made his camp just below the river north of Lake Nazferiti, and it still stands, a hunter's foothold cut into the green sprawl of Stranglethorn.

## Rebel Camp

The Rebel Camp stands in the northern reaches of Stranglethorn Vale, west of the road running south from Duskwood, northwest of Kurzen's Compound.

Its founders served under Colonel Kurzen's expedition until madness took him and his men, twisting their minds beyond recognition. A small band under Lieutenant Doren broke free before the madness claimed them, fleeing Kurzen's Compound westward through the jungle.

There they built the Rebel Camp, a foothold from which to undo what Kurzen had become. Krazek of Booty Bay keeps them supplied with land mines and other means of defense, enough to let the rebels hold out against the cursed garrison they once served beside.

## Ruins of Aboraz

Aboraz was raised by ancient trolls, but it belongs to none now. Zanzil the Outcast, exiled from his own kind, has claimed the ruins as his domain, filling them with ghouls, naga, and troll minions who answer to his witch doctor's craft.

## Ruins of Jubuwal

The Ruins of Jubuwal lie east of the Gurubashi Arena, an old troll settlement now held by ghouls, naga, and trolls serving Zanzil the Outcast. Among them hide Jon-Jon the Crow and Maury "Club Foot" Wilkins, men fled into that grim company to escape debts owed to the ruthless "Sea Wolf" MacKinley.

## Ruins of Zul'Kunda

Zul'Kunda's stone terraces belong to the Bloodscalp trolls now, ruled by Nezzliok the Dire and Gan'zulah. The path east from Zuuldaia Ruins leads straight into their territory, where old troll stonework stands claimed by the living.

## Ruins of Zul'Mamwe

The Ruins of Zul'Mamwe lie ringed by mountains on every side but the north, south of the Mosh'Ogg Ogre Mound. They are the seat of the Skullsplitters, second of Stranglethorn Vale's troll tribes, ruled by their chieftain Ana'thek the Cruel.

## South Seas

The South Seas fill the water between the known continents, a portion of the Great Sea. Zandalar rises from these waters, ancient home of the troll empire. Kezan lies among them too, a volcanic island holding its own people apart from the mainland powers. Nearer Stranglethorn Vale, the sea grows treacherous with reefs and small islands, some no more than a sandbar, others holding ruins whose builders are gone. The South Seas run along the borders of Stranglethorn and Tanaris.

## Southern Savage Coast

The Southern Savage Coast lies where the South Seas break against open beaches and shallow water, running down to the waters near Booty Bay and Janeiro's Point, where the shore gives way to the Wild Shore beyond.

The Vile Reef marks the line between this coast and its northern counterpart, where the Great Sea gives way to the South Seas. Bays and inlets bring the coast into contact with the Gurubashi Arena and the Nek'mani Wellspring.

## Spirit Den

The Spirit Den lies hidden in a cave at the northern end of Mistvale Valley. There the witch doctor Unbagwa keeps to the shadows of the den, tending to whatever spirits linger in that quiet, unseen hollow.

## The Cape of Stranglethorn

The southern reaches of Stranglethorn Vale lie thick with troll ruins, half-swallowed by jungle, and long stretches of sandy shore where the trees give way to sea.

Along the coast sits Booty Bay, a harbor city run by goblin pirates, its docks and market open to anyone with coin regardless of banner.

That neutrality is not peace. Booty Bay's goblins hold their harbor by trade and by force, and their rivals are the Bloodsail Buccaneers, pirates who have turned against them and taken to raiding the waters and shore the goblins call their own. The conflict between the two has only worsened, ship against ship and camp against camp, the goblins guarding what they built while the Bloodsails press to take it from them.

Beyond the harbor and the ruins, the land is quiet: sand, old stone, and the sea beyond.

## The Crystal Shore

The Crystal Shore is a sandy coastline in southeast Stranglethorn, where the South Seas break against the mainland and the eastern beaches of Jaguero Isle. It runs from the Wild Shore to the cliffs where the land rises northward, cut off from the interior by a steep ridge crossed by only two paths, one climbing toward Mistvale Valley.

The Ruins of Aboraz sit at the north end of the shore.

## The Savage Coast

The Savage Coast runs along the northern shore of Stranglethorn Vale, from the Westfall border down past the Vile Reef to Grom'Gol.

Crocolisks haunt its shallows and basilisks stalk its rocks, their gaze turning the unwary to stone. Several troll settlements watch over these waters.

## The Stockpile

In a cave at the back of Kurzen's Compound, Colonel Kurzen keeps the equipment of his war.

## The Vile Reef

Off the western coast of Stranglethorn Vale, near Grom'Gol, the sea breaks bright over coral. Beneath it lie sunken troll ruins, and among that drowned stone the murlocs now make their home, thick shoals moving through halls that once belonged to someone else. The Altar of the Tides still stands there. Giant clams litter the reef floor, said to hold Blue Pearls, and wreckage from ships that never cleared the reef rolls in along the shore.

## Tkashi Ruins

The Tkashi Ruins lie in Stranglethorn Vale, old troll stonework reclaimed by jungle. The Bloodscalp tribe holds them now, its shamans working ancient rites among the broken walls, artifacts still scattered through the wreckage.

## Venture Co. Base Camp

The Venture Company keeps a base camp here, given over to the search for Tumbled Crystals. Nearby stands their Operations Center, the two working in tandem toward the same find.

## Venture Co. Operations Center

The Venture Company oil platform rises from a lake in the jungle, the operations center for their push into the wilds. Mechanics, geologists, and other goblin crews crowd the rig, working the machinery of an outfit bent on stripping the land for profit.

## Wild Shore

The Wild Shore is where the Eastern Kingdoms give way to the South Seas, its beaches held by the Bloodsail Buccaneers, whose fleet rides offshore and whose crews claim the sands as their own. Somewhere along the shore wanders Gorlash, a lone giant, and between pirates and giant the coast answers to no crown.

The land narrows westward to a rocky point before turning toward Booty Bay. East across a shallow strait lies Jaguero Isle. Northward the sands blur into the Crystal Shore.

## Yojamba Isle

On these small islands off the northwestern coast of Stranglethorn, near the border with Westfall, the Zandalar Tribe made its stand against a god gone mad.

Hakkar the Soulflayer had turned Zul'Gurub into a place of blood and madness, and the Zandalari knew they could not face him alone. So they came to Yojamba Isle and called on Alliance and Horde alike to set aside their war long enough to help destroy him. It was an old, proud tribe trusting outsiders with the fate of its god-touched city.

## Ziata'jai Ruins

The Ziata'jai Ruins are ancient stones raised long ago by trolls, now claimed by the Skullsplitter tribe, who hold the old walls as their own.

## Zul'Gurub

Zul'Gurub rises out of the jungle in northeastern Stranglethorn Vale, its terraces and idols overgrown but still standing, a temple city that was once the beating heart of an empire. Thousands of years ago it was the capital of the Gurubashi Empire, seat of every jungle troll tribe bound to it. Now it belongs only to the Gurubashi tribe that remains, and the jungle around it does not feel empty.

The empire tore itself apart when a circle of priests called the Atal'ai summoned the avatar of an ancient blood god, Hakkar the Soulflayer, and were driven into exile for it. They learned that Hakkar could only take physical form in one place: the old Gurubashi capital itself. It is here that Jin'do the Hexxer has enslaved several high priests of the Gurubashi, bending them to the work of calling Hakkar into the world. Champions sent to stop him, each bound to a Primal God, have fallen under Hakkar's sway instead, their power now feeding the very thing they came to destroy.

## Zuuldaia Ruins

Weathered stone rises above the western coast of Stranglethorn Vale, ruins of a settlement long since fallen. The Bloodscalp trolls now hold the broken walls, thick among the old foundations, claiming ground their ancestors never raised.
