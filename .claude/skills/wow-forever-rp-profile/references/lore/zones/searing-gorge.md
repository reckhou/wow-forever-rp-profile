# Searing Gorge

Once this was green land, part of the Redridge Mountains, until the ground itself was torn open more than two hundred and thirty years ago.

In the War of the Three Hammers, Sorcerer-Thane Thaurissan of the Dark Iron dwarves sought a weapon to break his enemies. He and seven of his most trusted dwarves reached beneath the world and called upon powers that should have stayed sleeping. What answered was not a servant but a master: the ancient Firelord Ragnaros. His waking tore Blackrock Mountain up out of the earth, blackened the land for miles, and crushed the Dark Iron capital of Thaurissan to rubble beneath it. The scorched steppes to the south became known as the Burning Steppes; this ravaged valley to the north became the Searing Gorge.

Not all Dark Irons stayed loyal to their clan after the ruin. A number of them broke away, calling themselves the Thorium Brotherhood, and built Thorium Point overlooking the great excavation their former kin had driven into the valley floor, watching the Dark Irons' labors from above.

## Blackchar Cave

Blackchar Cave lies sealed near Blackrock Mountain, its dark stone quiet and closed off from the world outside.

## Blackrock Mountain

Blackrock Mountain was not born of ordinary fire. When the Bronzebeard and Wildhammer armies marched to purge the Dark Iron clan during the War of the Three Hammers, Sorcerer-Thane Thaurissan reached into the world's molten heart and dragged its power up as a weapon. What answered him was Ragnaros. The Firelord's rebirth tore the mountains apart, drowned the surrounding land in fire, and sent both kings turning their armies back rather than face him. Where the Dark Iron kingdom had stood, a volcano now smoldered, with the Burning Steppes to its south and the Searing Gorge to its north. Ragnaros withdrew into the mountain's core and enslaved the surviving Dark Iron dwarves to carve Shadowforge City beneath their own ruined home.

## Dustfire Valley

Dustfire Valley cuts through the Searing Gorge, windswept ground leading north to the gates of Stonewrought Pass and the Valley of Kings beyond in Loch Modan.

## Firewatch Ridge

Firewatch Ridge is the main camp of the Dark Iron dwarves in Searing Gorge, overlooking the great excavation of the Cauldron. Dark Iron dwarves hold the slopes with mechanical golems and giant flame spiders, making the ridge a dangerous place to tread.

## Grimesilt Dig Site

Ironforge dwarves once dug here, until the Dark Iron dwarves came and seized the site for themselves. It remains in Dark Iron hands.

## Stonewrought Pass

The tunnel known as Stonewrought Pass cuts through the mountains, joining Searing Gorge to the Valley of Kings in Loch Modan, at the northern end of Dustfire Valley. It is the work of Franclorn Forgewright, who also raised the Stonewrought Dam and much else of dwarven stonework across the Eastern Kingdoms.

The Loch Modan gate is held shut by Mountaineer Pebblebitty, barring the way to all but those who have proven themselves against Margol the Rager.

## Tanner Camp

Sarah Tanner, master of elemental leatherworking, keeps a lone tent in Searing Gorge. Behind it a long passage winds into the mountain, ending where the thunder lizard Margol the Rager waits.

## The Cauldron

The Cauldron is a vast excavation gouged into the center of the Searing Gorge, fortified by the Dark Iron dwarves some time after they summoned Ragnaros. The great machines they used to tear it open still ring its edges. Below, fire and magma elementals move through the cracked, glowing crevices.

Slave workers taken by the Dark Irons labor endlessly down there, broken by the work and the oppressive heat. They no longer know friend from captor, and meet anyone who enters with the same mindless violence as any overseer.

## The Sea of Cinders

The Sea of Cinders lies flat and open in the southern reaches of Searing Gorge, just north of Blackrock Mountain. Nothing grows there. The ground still smolders, and ash rises constantly on the wind, drifting across the barren flats. Fire and wind left nothing behind but waste, and the burning has never stopped.

## The Slag Pit

The Slag Pit sinks deep into the Cauldron excavation in Searing Gorge, worked by slaves under the cruelty of Dark Iron dwarves. Slavers and taskmasters drive the labor on, and even the cowed workers will turn on anyone who strikes at their overseers. The Dark Irons answer any interference with their mining with open hostility, and in the pit's deeper dark, ravenous Incendosaurs nest among the diggings.

## Thorium Point

In the scorched north of Searing Gorge stands Thorium Point, a small neutral town held by the Thorium Brotherhood, populated mostly by Dark Iron dwarves who hold to the Brotherhood rather than the dark powers beneath the mountains. A forge and anvil stand ready here, kept lit amid the ash and heat, where metal is still shaped despite what the Gorge has become.
