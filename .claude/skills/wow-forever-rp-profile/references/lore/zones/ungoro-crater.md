# Un'Goro Crater

Un'Goro Crater lies walled off between three deserts, a wound of green sunk into the south of Kalimdor, where the world forgets to be barren. Dinosaurs older than memory move through its jungle, some grown to lengths no living thing should reach, and elementals of earth and fire walk untroubled among them. On the ridges above, pylons stand that no one has ever explained.

During the War of the Shifting Sands, the night elves were driven back through this crater to the edge of the Tanaris desert, and there the Qiraji advance broke. Something in Un'Goro itself would not let the invaders take the land, though no one has said what.

An expedition led by Williden and Hol'anyee Marshal once tried to make a foothold here, but the savagery of the crater proved more than they had prepared for. Their camps were overrun, and the survivors fled to higher ground, making their stand at what is now called Marshal's Refuge.

## Fire Plume Ridge

Fire Plume Ridge rises at the heart of Un'Goro Crater, a mountain of scorched rock where lava creeps down the slopes and fire elementals stir in the heat. Shallow veins of magma run beneath the crater floor, feeding the steam pools and geysers that hiss throughout Un'Goro, so that the whole basin seems to breathe from the mountain's fire.

Within a cave at the peak dwells Blazerunner, the strongest of the fire elementals that haunt the ridge, master of the mountain's burning heart. The spirits of fire that flicker and dance here speak of centuries spent burning, dying down to embers, and reigniting again, over and over, without end.

Not all who come to the ridge master it. A wanderer named Ringo came to explore its slopes and lost his way, and heat exhaustion overtook him. He can still be found in the southern cave, having never made it back out.

## Fungal Rock

Fungal Rock opens in the northeastern wall of Un'Goro Crater, a cavern where gorillas live among the ghost mushrooms growing in its dark. Outside, a small pool gathers beneath a waterfall, and there stands Karna Remtravel's mechanical ape, A-Me 01.

## Golakka Hot Springs

Steam rises constantly from Golakka Hot Springs, where the volcanic heart of Un'Goro Crater bleeds heat into pools and geysers in the crater's west. Fury elementals born of that steam and fire pace the shores, drawn from the earth by the same forces that keep the springs boiling.

Krakle once came here to trace what bound the hot springs to the fires of nearby Fire Plume Ridge, before his search carried him elsewhere.

## Ironstone Plateau

Bare rock breaks through the green of Un'Goro along its northeastern wall. Ironstone Plateau lies quiet compared to the rest of the crater, largely empty of predators. Fledgling pterrordax nest here, and the plateau's stillness sets it apart from Un'Goro's restless growth.

## Lakkari Tar Pits

The black lakes north of Marshal's Refuge sit thick and foul-smelling amid Un'Goro's green. Bones jut from the surface in their hundreds, remains of beasts that stepped in expecting solid ground and found none. Scavengers linger at the edges, waiting for the tar to finish what it started, while tar beasts have risen from the pits themselves.

## Marshal's Refuge

Marshal's Refuge sits at the northern edge of Un'Goro Crater, raised by Marshal Expeditions under Williden and Hol'anyee Marshal. Their venture into the crater was not ready for what it found; Un'Goro's beasts proved too savage, and the expedition's base camps were overrun one by one. What remained of the troupe fled to higher ground, making their escape here, at the crater's edge.

The refuge is what came of that retreat, a foothold barely kept rather than won.

## Terror Run

In Terror Run, west of the Slithering Scar, red crystals grow up from the ground unlike anywhere else in Un'Goro Crater. The beasts of the run have drunk in their power, and it shows in them.

## The Marshlands

The marsh spreads across the southeastern reach of Un'Goro Crater, a low sprawl of standing water and rank growth that greets most who first descend into the crater from Tanaris, since any other approach is treacherous. Raptors move through the reeds in great numbers, some carrying poison in their bite.

Blue power crystals can be found here, and their presence has worked into the marsh itself, seeping into the raptors that hunt through the wet ground and leaving its mark on them.

On a mound rising from the water, Torwa Pathfinder keeps watch, having come to the spot by canoe from elsewhere in the crater.

## The Slithering Scar

The Slithering Scar spreads across the southern reach of Un'Goro Crater, a hive of the Gorishi sect.

The hive once had a queen, summoned by disturbing the Gorishi Silithid Crystal within.
