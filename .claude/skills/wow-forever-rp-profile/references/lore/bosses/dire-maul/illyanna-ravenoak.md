# Illyanna Ravenoak

A boss of Dire Maul, number 7 of 19 in Forever's order.

Illyanna Ravenoak was a night elf hunter of the Shen'dralar. With her faithful companion, the bear Ferra, she served as Prince Tortheldrin's most valued hunter. Then the prince ordered her to execute her friends, and she refused.

As punishment Tortheldrin killed Illyanna and her bear. He used his demonic power to bind their spirits to their bodies, and he condemned them to remain in Dire Maul as undead protectors. The huntress and Ferra now walk the Capital Gardens, and each is a fierce enemy alone.
