# Prince Tortheldrin

A boss of Dire Maul, number 10 of 19 in Forever's order.

Prince Tortheldrin was a loyal servant of Queen Azshara and the leader of the Shen'dralar, the Highborne who guarded her arcane secrets in the hidden city of Eldre'Thalas, now called Dire Maul. When the Great Sundering struck, he and his followers wove a spell that shielded the city from destruction. The Well of Eternity was gone, though, and without it their immortality faded.

To keep control of the surviving Shen'dralar he built pylons in the west wing and imprisoned the demon Immol'thar, so that his subjects could siphon the creature's strength. The demonic power twisted his mind. When the demon faltered, he ordered the deaths of many of his vassals so that only he and his most fanatical followers could enjoy the corrupt energy. Among the dead were his hunter Illyanna Ravenoak and her bear.

Tortheldrin still rules the halls. He welcomes visitors and trades stories with them, but he guards the secret of Immol'thar jealously, and those who meddle in his domain can expect a brutal end.
