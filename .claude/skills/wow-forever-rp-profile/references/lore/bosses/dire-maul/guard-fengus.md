# Guard Fengus

A boss of Dire Maul, number 13 of 19 in Forever's order.

Fengus is a Gordok ogre guard. Cho'Rush the Observer once told him he was destined to meet a mate while on duty. Since then Fengus has spent almost every waking moment patrolling Gordok territory.
