# Lethtendris

A boss of Dire Maul, number 3 of 19 in Forever's order.

Lethtendris is an elf warlock with an unbearable craving for arcane magic. The warped energy of Dire Maul drew her from afar, and she made a lair in the Warpwood Quarter, with her imp Pimgib at her side.

From the sinews and ligaments of sacrificial victims she built a web, a device that slowly siphons the corrupt power out of the ruins. She found that it can gather vast reserves of overwhelming strength, enough to destroy all of her enemies.
