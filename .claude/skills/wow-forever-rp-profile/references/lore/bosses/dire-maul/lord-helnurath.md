# Lord Hel'nurath

A boss of Dire Maul, number 18 of 19 in Forever's order.

Lord Hel'nurath is one of the nathrezim and the ruler of Xoroth, where he keeps the stables. Xoroth is one of the fiery, chaotic worlds of the Burning Legion, a place of infernal heat and scorched bones. Dreadsteeds live there, and the nathrezim ride them as mounts.

Hel'nurath prizes his steeds. When a warlock tears one of them out of Xoroth, as happens in the ruins of Dire Maul West, he comes through a portal in a rage. He demands to know who dares to steal his precious mount and swears the thief will pay for the insolence.
