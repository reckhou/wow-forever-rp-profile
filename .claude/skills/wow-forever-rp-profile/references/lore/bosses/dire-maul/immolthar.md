# Immol'thar

A boss of Dire Maul, number 9 of 19 in Forever's order.

Immol'thar is an ancient demon, a void terror held captive in the western wing of Dire Maul. When the Well of Eternity was destroyed, the Shen'dralar lost the power and immortality it gave them, and many of the Highborne slipped into lethargy. Prince Tortheldrin ordered pylons built to raise a force field. The Shen'dralar summoned the demon into the Capital Gardens and trapped him inside it.

Hungry for magic, they quickly mastered siphoning his demonic energy for their spells and for their immortality. For nearly ten thousand years Immol'thar could not fight back or even move. He could only watch and wait for the day he might break free and rend the flesh of his captors.
