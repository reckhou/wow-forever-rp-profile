# Captain Kromcrush

A boss of Dire Maul, number 15 of 19 in Forever's order.

Captain Kromcrush leads King Gordok's elite ogres and commands the fearsome guards who roam the ruins. Even among ogres he is infamous for his hair-trigger rage toward anyone who insults him or the king. He crushes dissent ruthlessly. He has still less patience for those who spread the rumor of his torrid affair with a gnoll long ago.
