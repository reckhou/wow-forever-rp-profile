# Zevrim Thornhoof

A boss of Dire Maul, number 1 of 19 in Forever's order.

Zevrim Thornhoof is a satyr warlock who holds the Warpwood Quarter of Dire Maul. He wanted to prove his worth to the Burning Legion, and he told his masters that he could enslave the formidable ancients who still roamed the ruins. His own power fell short. He turned to other living creatures for extra energy, butchering them on a sacrificial altar and harvesting their essence in their last, agonizing moments.

The energy of his victims helped him bend the water elemental Hydrospawn to his will. He also keeps Old Ironbark in his grasp, and the ancient can do nothing while Zevrim lives.
