# Edwin VanCleef

A boss of Deadmines, number 8 of 8 in Forever's order.

Edwin VanCleef was the guildmaster of the Stonemasons, the craftsmen who rebuilt Stormwind after the orcs burned it. He grew up in the city and learned a rogue's trade alongside his childhood friend Mathias Shaw. When the work was finished, the House of Nobles refused to pay what the guild was owed. VanCleef argued the case and was ignored, and he led his followers out of the city.

In the hills of Westfall he turned the Stonemasons into the Defias Brotherhood, a band of thieves and killers who wear red bandanas. They drove the farmers off their land and seized farms and mines across Westfall and Elwynn. VanCleef made his seat in the abandoned Deadmines under Moonbrook, where goblin engineers are building a great ship to strike at Stormwind from the sea.

He commands from the cabin of that ship. Baros Alexston, the city architect and an old friend, remembers him as a peerless fighter, an idealist and a romantic, and says revenge has consumed him.
