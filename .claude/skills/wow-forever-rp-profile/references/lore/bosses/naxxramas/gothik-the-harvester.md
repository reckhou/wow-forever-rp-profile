# Gothik the Harvester

A boss of Naxxramas, number 8 of 15 in Forever's order.

Gothik the Harvester is a necromancer, a master of necromancy and conjuration. He is said to be able to call forth legions of the undead at a moment's notice, and it is with his guidance that even the weakest death knights learn to raise the dead.

He serves Kel'Thuzad in the Military Quarter of Naxxramas.
