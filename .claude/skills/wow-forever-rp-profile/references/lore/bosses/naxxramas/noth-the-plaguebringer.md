# Noth the Plaguebringer

A boss of Naxxramas, number 4 of 15 in Forever's order.

Noth the Plaguebringer was once a respected mage of Dalaran. He heard the call of the Lich King in much the same way Kel'Thuzad did, and like Kel'Thuzad he was driven by hunger for power. He accepted the summons and gave the Scourge his skill in necromancy and curse-weaving.

The Third War troubled him. As it took the lives of countless innocents, Noth began to doubt his choice and to doubt Kel'Thuzad. Kel'Thuzad answered that growing compassion quickly, by freezing the living heart in Noth's chest. Noth now serves on in Naxxramas.
