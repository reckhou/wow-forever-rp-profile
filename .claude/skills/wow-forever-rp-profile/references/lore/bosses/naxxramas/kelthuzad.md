# Kel'Thuzad

A boss of Naxxramas, number 15 of 15 in Forever's order.

Kel'Thuzad was an archmage of the Kirin Tor and a member of the Council of Six in Dalaran. His interest in the dark arts cost him his rank and holdings, and Grand Magus Antonidas exiled him. Driven by a voice in his head, he traveled to Northrend and climbed the Frozen Throne to offer his soul to the Lich King.

As a necromancer of the Scourge he came back to Lordaeron and founded the Cult of the Damned in secret, recruiting among nobles and commoners with promises of immortality. Through the cult he became one of the Lich King's principal agents in spreading the plague of undeath across Lordaeron. Arthas Menethil killed him, then raised him again as a lich, and in the Third War Kel'Thuzad summoned the demon lord Archimonde into the world.

When Arthas sailed for Northrend, he left Kel'Thuzad to command the Scourge in Lordaeron. From the floating necropolis of Naxxramas he rules the Eastern Plaguelands and fights the Scarlet Crusade, the Argent Dawn, the Alliance and the Horde.
