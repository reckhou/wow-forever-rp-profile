# Gluth

A boss of Naxxramas, number 12 of 15 in Forever's order.

The undead plague-dog Gluth sits obediently within Naxxramas, waiting for orders from his masters. His appetite is so great that even the living were not enough to feed it.

It is rumored that Feugen feeds him an army of zombies every day. Gluth uses them to knit his own decaying flesh back together, and in this way the remains of undead too weak to fight are put to use.
