# Thaddius

A boss of Naxxramas, number 13 of 15 in Forever's order.

Thaddius is a massive abomination pieced together from the flesh of innocent women and children. He dwells in one of the experimental laboratories of Naxxramas, a vast hall of strange machinery where powerful bolts of electricity arc through the air and paint the walls with jagged shadows. The current charges Thaddius and the two huge wights that flank him, Stalagg and Feugen.

It is said that the souls contained in Thaddius are fused together, eternally bound within that foul prison of flesh.
