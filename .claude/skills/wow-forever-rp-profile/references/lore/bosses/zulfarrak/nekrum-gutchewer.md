# Nekrum Gutchewer

A boss of Zul'Farrak, number 6 of 8 in Forever's order.

Nekrum Gutchewer has mysteriously lived far past his natural life, and his decaying body is still strong. A medallion he carries is rumored to have given him his unnatural long years, at a cost, and Thadius Grimshade in the Blasted Lands wants it to study its hidden powers.

With Shadowpriest Sezz'ziz, Nekrum has gathered sacrifices for the mythic beast Gahz'rilla, who slumbers at the heart of the city. Many troll followers eagerly await her awakening, and they will do anything to see the ritual through.
