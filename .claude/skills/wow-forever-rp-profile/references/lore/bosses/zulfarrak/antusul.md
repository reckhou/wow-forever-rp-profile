# Antu'sul

A boss of Zul'Farrak, number 3 of 8 in Forever's order.

Antu'sul is a Sandfury troll who raises the sul'lithuz basilisks of Zul'Farrak, and he prefers them strong and ruthless. He treats the scaled beasts like family and calls them his children, and he strikes down anyone, even a fellow troll, who harms them. He lives in a cave set into the hills beside the city.

When intruders come he greets them with the words that lunch has arrived and tells his beautiful children to tear them to pieces. He gives his enemies no proper burial, and lets the basilisks feast on whoever dares cross him.
