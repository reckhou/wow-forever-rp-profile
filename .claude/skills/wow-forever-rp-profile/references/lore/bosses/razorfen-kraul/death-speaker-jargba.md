# Death Speaker Jargba

A boss of Razorfen Kraul, number 3 of 6 in Forever's order.

Death Speaker Jargba is a prophet of the quilboar religion that preaches the fallen god Agamaggan will rise from the dead. He is part priest and part warrior, and a powerful one in both roles.

The Death Speakers of the Death's Head tribe have two tasks. They spread the tribe's propaganda, and they discipline anyone who refuses to obey Charlga Razorflank and her mysterious new allies. Jargba has made a name for himself among them by dominating the minds of dissidents and forcing them to accept the will of their leaders.
