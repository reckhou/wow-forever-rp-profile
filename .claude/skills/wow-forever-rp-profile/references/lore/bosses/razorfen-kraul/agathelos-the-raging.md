# Agathelos the Raging

A boss of Razorfen Kraul, number 5 of 6 in Forever's order.

Agathelos the Raging is a colossal armored boar, roughly twice the size of a thunder lizard and larger still when enraged. Boars are revered in quilboar culture, and particularly large and tenacious ones are seen as a favor from Agamaggan, the titanic boar who is the god and progenitor of the quilboar.

The Death's Head keep him in a short tunnel in Razorfen Kraul, guarded by several quilboar who maintain a field to hold the beast in. Already powerful, Agathelos has been fed Kraul bat guano, which maddens him and adds to his stamina and strength. The Death's Head plan to use him as a weapon against all who oppose them.
