# Baron Rivendare

A boss of Stratholme, number 13 of 19 in Forever's order.

Baron Rivendare was a wealthy landowner in Stratholme who fell under the sway of Kel'Thuzad. He helped build the Cult of the Damned, and by the time of the Third War he was known as the Master of Stratholme. In the end the plague corrupted him completely, and he took the title and powers of a death knight.

He now rules the Scourge half of the burning city from the Slaughterhouse. He rides an unholy skeletal steed, Deathcharger, and the skeletons of the Black Guard are his elite. He holds Stratholme against the Scarlet Crusade, coordinates the Lich King's efforts across the Plaguelands, and serves Kel'Thuzad eagerly. He fights the agents the Argent Dawn sends against him under Duke Nicholas Zverenhoff, and he holds one of them, Ysida Harmon, prisoner. One of his greatest joys is corrupting paladins.
