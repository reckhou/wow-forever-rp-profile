# Black Guard Swordsmith

A boss of Stratholme, number 14 of 19 in Forever's order.

The Swordsmith is an armored skeleton of the Black Guard, the elite corps of skeletal warriors that serves Baron Rivendare on the Scourge side of Stratholme.

The Black Guard stole the plans of the sword Corruption, and the Swordsmith watches over them near the ziggurat of Baroness Anastari. He tells any thief that payment for those plans comes in bones.
