# Nerub'enkan

A boss of Stratholme, number 9 of 19 in Forever's order.

Nerub'enkan was a fearsome nerubian warrior who fought the Scourge in the war that broke her people. She succumbed to her wounds and was raised into undeath as an obedient crypt fiend in the Lich King's service.

She now guards a ziggurat in the Gauntlet of Stratholme, one of three that bar the way to the Slaughterhouse and Baron Rivendare, and she defends it as fiercely as she once defended her home.
