# Balnazzar

A boss of Stratholme, number 6 of 19 in Forever's order.

Balnazzar is a nathrezim dreadlord, one of three brothers left behind in Lordaeron to oversee the demonic conquest of that land. When Arthas returned from Kalimdor the brothers scattered, then plotted against him and failed. Sylvanas Windrunner later besieged Balnazzar's stronghold in the ruined capital and ordered his brother Varimathras to kill him, and the dreadlord was thought destroyed.

He lived. Wearing the face of Saidan Dathrohan, one of the first paladins of the Silver Hand, he became Grand Crusader of the Scarlet Crusade, and its zealots follow him without knowing what he is. They are his unwitting pawns.

Balnazzar has never tasted true death, though he has known defeat. He sits in the Scarlet Bastion of Stratholme and plots vengeance.
