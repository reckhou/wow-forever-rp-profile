# Ramstein the Gorger

A boss of Stratholme, number 12 of 19 in Forever's order.

Ramstein is one of the Scourge's most infamous abominations, a horror stitched together from many corpses and driven by a ceaseless hunger. He is dim-witted, yet fearless and tenacious, and he can press a fight for hours without growing weary.

When Scourge forces overran Stratholme he committed unspeakable atrocities on countless innocents, and thousands died by his hands, among them the ranger lord Nathanos Marris. Marris now serves the Forsaken as Nathanos Blightcaller. He hunted Ramstein across the Plaguelands for months, until the abomination fled to the safety of Stratholme.
