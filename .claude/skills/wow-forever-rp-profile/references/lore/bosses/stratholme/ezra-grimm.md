# Ezra Grimm

A boss of Stratholme, number 16 of 19 in Forever's order.

Ezra Grimm owned and ran Ezra Grimm's Premium Tobacco, a smoke shop in Stratholme. When the Scourge invaded during the Third War he refused to abandon his shop. He fought to make sure the undead would never touch his famous tobacco, and became one of them in the process.

He is still in Stratholme, still guarding his stock, and he turns on anyone who tries to salvage the tobacco.
