# Kam Deepfury

A boss of Stormwind Stockade, number 2 of 5 in Forever's order.

Kam Deepfury is a Dark Iron dwarf, and Alliance intelligence at Dun Modr names him as the planner of the attack on the Thandol Span. The blast destroyed one of the two great bridges that join the Wetlands to the Arathi Highlands, and many dwarves died, among them the brother of Longbraid the Grim.

Stormwind holds him in the Stockade for political reasons and has not put him to death. Unlike most of the prisoners there he does not belong to the Defias Brotherhood. Motley Garmason of Dun Modr wants him dead and blames human bureaucracy for letting him live.
