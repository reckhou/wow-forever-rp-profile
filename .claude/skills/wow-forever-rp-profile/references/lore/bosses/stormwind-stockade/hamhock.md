# Hamhock

A boss of Stormwind Stockade, number 3 of 5 in Forever's order.

Hamhock is a two-headed ogre held in the Stormwind Stockade. When the prisoners rose against their guards he joined the ringleaders of the riot, and he now watches over the chamber of three cells where Bazil Thredd sits in the middle one.
