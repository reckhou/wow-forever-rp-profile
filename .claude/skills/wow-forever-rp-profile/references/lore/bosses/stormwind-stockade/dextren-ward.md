# Dextren Ward

A boss of Stormwind Stockade, number 4 of 5 in Forever's order.

Dextren Ward came from Duskwood, where he was caught selling corpses from the Raven Hill Cemetery to the necromancer Morbent Fel. Lord Ello Ebonlocke and the Darkshire council sentenced him to death. The House of Nobles in Stormwind claimed the prisoner for itself and locked him in the Stockade instead of letting the sentence be carried out.

The people of Darkshire took that as an insult. Councilman Millstipe wants Ward's hand as proof that justice was done and says the nobles ignore Duskwood's needs. In the riot that followed, he is counted among the instigators.
