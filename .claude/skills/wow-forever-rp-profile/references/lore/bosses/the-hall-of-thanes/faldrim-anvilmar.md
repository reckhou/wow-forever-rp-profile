# Faldrim Anvilmar

A boss of The Hall of Thanes, number 1 of 4 in Forever's order.

Deep beneath Ironforge lies the Hall of Thanes, an ancient burial site of the dwarves. Something has disturbed its tombs, and the spirits of the dead, who are light sleepers, have woken angry. The keepers refuse to enter the hall to do their duties.

Faldrim Anvilmar, a thane, is among the restless. His spirit cannot rest. His ghostly attendant, who can barely speak, says he is consumed by an ancient grudge and begs for help to lay him to rest.
