# Caldoran

A boss of Scarlet Enclave, number 8 of 8 in Forever's order.

Baelin Caldoran served as a Knight-Captain of the Alliance of Lordaeron and stood at Prince Arthas's side in the Culling of Stratholme. When Leonid Barthalomew tried to protect his own family there, Caldoran ordered them killed. After Leonid was freed from the Scourge's control, he went home and found that his son Percival had survived. Caldoran found them together and ordered Leonid hanged and Percival slain for consorting with the undead.

As an inquisitor of the Scarlet Crusade he joined the attack on Naxxramas. There he took the corrupted Ashbringer from the Four Horsemen and left the Blade of Inquisition in its place. Asked what became of the blade he carried there, he calls it a tool that served its purpose and refuses to say more.

He now names himself Grand Crusader. He destroyed Balnazzar with the Light, enthralled the dragon Solistrasza, and draws on the souls of the pious to feed his crusaders' weapons. The Argent Dawn has sworn to bring him to justice.
