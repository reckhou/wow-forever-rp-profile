# Mason

A boss of Scarlet Enclave, number 5 of 8 in Forever's order.

Mason is called the Echo. He says his blades resonate with the song of the ocean, and he names the Eastsea as the source of his strength. Cannons stand behind him in the Scarlet Enclave, and a crew of Eastsea scallywags fights at his side. He calls newcomers greenhorns and landlubbers, and he grows angry when they prove tougher than he expected.
