# Lillian Voss

A boss of Scarlet Enclave, number 7 of 8 in Forever's order.

Lillian Voss was raised from childhood by her father, High Priest Voss of the Scarlet Crusade, to be a weapon against the plagued undead. She gave up her childhood for it and trained in stealth, sorcery, martial arts and anything else that would make her stronger. As a young woman she counted Scarlet Lieutenant Gebler among her friends, and she took part in the defense of the Scarlet Enclave against Argent Dawn attackers.

Within the Enclave she presides over the Crusade's captives and over the Grand Apothecary Putress, who is held prisoner there. She watches his concoctions as a jailer would, and she looks for the weakness in anyone brought before her.
