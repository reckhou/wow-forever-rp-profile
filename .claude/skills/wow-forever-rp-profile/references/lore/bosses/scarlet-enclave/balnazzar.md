# Balnazzar

A boss of Scarlet Enclave, number 1 of 8 in Forever's order.

Balnazzar is a nathrezim, one of three dreadlord brothers left in Lordaeron to oversee the demonic conquest of the kingdom. When Arthas came back from Kalimdor and announced that Archimonde was dead, the brothers fled his anger and began to plot against him. Sylvanas Windrunner, whom they had called to their side, turned on them instead. She laid siege to Balnazzar's stronghold in the ruined capital and ordered Varimathras to kill his own brother. Balnazzar was thought destroyed, but he survived.

He took the body of the paladin Saidan Dathrohan and became Grand Crusader of the Scarlet Crusade, a zealous order sworn to destroy the Scourge. Its soldiers did not know that a demon led them.

After the campaign against Naxxramas, a force of Dathrohan's loyalists left Stratholme and seized the Scarlet Enclave at Tyr's Hand from their more measured brethren. Balnazzar rules there in his true shape. Caldoran has sworn to end him and destroys the dreadlord with a blast of the Light.
