# Beatrix

A boss of Scarlet Enclave, number 2 of 8 in Forever's order.

Beatrix commands Scarlet soldiers and was at Naxxramas with a strategist and a tactician who quarrelled over how to break the floating necropolis. She cut them short and declared that the Argent Dawn would help the Crusade crush the Scourge. While the Crusade was away, Dathrohan's loyalists came out of Stratholme and took its stronghold at Tyr's Hand.

Beatrix gathered the loyal remnant at a base camp to the west, sure that Tyr's Hand must be reclaimed at any cost, and won the Argent Dawn's help to retake it. Once Balnazzar fell, she took command of the leaderless defenders and turned the whole force on her allies. As High Commander she leads cannon crews, archers, mages and cavalry, and she follows Caldoran's vision of a world the Crusade will build alone.
