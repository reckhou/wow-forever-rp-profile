# Atrexis the Grave Knight

A boss of City of Dalaran, number 8 of 8 in Forever's order.

Atrexis the Grave Knight is a necromancer of disturbing talent. He works in the underbelly of Dalaran, the mage city, where he raises an army of the dead under the very nose of the Kirin Tor.

The Forsaken call him a traitor to their own kind. Melisara in Tarren Mill says he spits in the face of everything the Forsaken stand for, and that no one is beyond the reach of the Dark Lady.
