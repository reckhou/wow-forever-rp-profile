# Hellscream's Phantom

A boss of Demon Fall Canyon, number 6 of 7 in Forever's order.

Grommash Hellscream drank the blood of Mannoroth, once on Draenor and again in Ashenvale, and it made him the demon's slave. In the canyon in southern Ashenvale he found the will to turn on his master. While Thrall lay stunned, Grom's axe cut into Mannoroth's chest. The pit lord's death burst in fire and light and tore the life from Grom as well, and with it the blood curse on the orcs ended. The orcs named the place for him and raised a monument to the warrior who freed them.

His phantom still stands there, a fel orc ghost. Its cries swing between loyalty to Lord Mannoroth and the relief of a mind freed from the blood haze.
