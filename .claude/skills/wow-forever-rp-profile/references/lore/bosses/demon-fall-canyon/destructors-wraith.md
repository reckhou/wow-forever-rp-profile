# Destructor's Wraith

A boss of Demon Fall Canyon, number 2 of 7 in Forever's order.

The Destructor's Wraith is a large felguard, a demon bound to Demon Fall Canyon. Mannoroth, the pit lord who died in this canyon, bore the title of the Destructor, and the wraith carries the name of that fallen terror. It speaks of breaking and ruin, and tells those who come before it that they will wither.
