# Zilbagob

A boss of Demon Fall Canyon, number 3 of 7 in Forever's order.

Zilbagob is an imp who has climbed aboard a goblin shredder and rides it through Demon Fall Canyon. He loves fire and blades and cackles that things go boom. With the shredder's whirling blades he cuts at anyone who comes near, and he is glad to burn whatever the blades miss.
