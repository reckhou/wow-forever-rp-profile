# High Inquisitor Fairbanks

A boss of Scarlet Monastery, number 6 of 7 in Forever's order.

High Inquisitor Fairbanks was the most trusted advisor of Highlord Alexandros Mograine, a founder of the original Scarlet Crusade and the knight called the Ashbringer. Fairbanks followed him into an ambush by the Scourge at Stratholme, where Mograine's son Renault had led his father like a lamb to the slaughter.

Fairbanks fell first and the undead ignored him. He lay under the corpses for two days, heard Renault drive the Ashbringer through his father's heart, and then escaped the ruined city. He told the tale to anyone who would listen, and the Crusade murdered him for it and dragged his body to a hidden chamber. Some had listened, and from them the Argent Dawn was born. Fairbanks still lies in that chamber, undead.
