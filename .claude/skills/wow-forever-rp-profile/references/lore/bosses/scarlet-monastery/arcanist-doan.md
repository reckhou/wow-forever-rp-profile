# Arcanist Doan

A boss of Scarlet Monastery, number 4 of 7 in Forever's order.

Arcanist Doan keeps the library of the Scarlet Monastery, a guardian of the arcane that the Scarlet Crusade is rumored to distrust. All the same he is a trusted official, counted among the most powerful mages of the Crusade, and he holds a vital key that grants access to Scarlet operations across the Plaguelands.

Doan believes that arcane magic is the only way to rid Lordaeron of the Scourge. He guards the library's mysteries closely. He warns intruders that they will not defile them, and he calls down righteous fire on those who try.
