# High Inquisitor Whitemane

A boss of Scarlet Monastery, number 7 of 7 in Forever's order.

Sally Whitemane was young when her family fell to the plague of undeath as they traveled through northern Lordaeron. She was forced to destroy her parents and siblings when they rose as mindless Scourge minions. Guilt and rage have left her with one source of fulfillment, the destruction of the undead.

Now High Inquisitor Whitemane is the true master of the Scarlet Monastery, a fearsome priestess who directs the Crusade from its cathedral and can raise fallen warriors to fight in her name. Raleigh the Devout in Southshore and Varimathras in the Undercity both want her dead, one to return the Crusade to its first purpose and the other to end a threat so near his capital.
