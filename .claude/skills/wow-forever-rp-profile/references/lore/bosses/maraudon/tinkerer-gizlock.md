# Tinkerer Gizlock

A boss of Maraudon, number 3 of 8 in Forever's order.

Tinkerer Gizlock and five other gem hunters snuck into Maraudon to mine its highly prized crystals. The plan went horribly wrong when satyrs slaughtered everyone in the party but Gizlock.

Trapped within the caves, the goblin was driven to the brink of madness. He now sees himself as the ruler of the whole subterranean realm, and he will not suffer anyone to reveal his presence.
