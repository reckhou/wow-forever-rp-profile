# Archaedas

A boss of Uldaman, number 8 of 8 in Forever's order.

Deep within Uldaman lies a chamber with one of the greatest treasures of the titans, the Discs of Norgannon, which hold countless secrets of the Makers. No explorer has managed to reach it, for any attempt wakes the great watcher Archaedas.

Archaedas is an immense guardian, crafted from some of the oldest, strongest stone taken from Uldaman's depths. He has a veritable army of stone servants at his command, and he calls them to defend the discs, demanding to know who dares the wrath of the Makers. Treasure seekers who try him are dispatched with ease.
