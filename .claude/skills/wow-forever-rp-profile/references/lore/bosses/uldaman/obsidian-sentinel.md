# Obsidian Sentinel

A boss of Uldaman, number 4 of 8 in Forever's order.

The Obsidian Sentinel is one of the stone keepers the titans built to protect Uldaman from would-be invaders and to oversee its daily operations. It was responsible for guarding the vault's secrets until an unknown force corrupted it.

The Sentinel was crafted from the same stone as the halls of Uldaman itself, but most of its body has been replaced with dark glass that reflects spells. Whatever caused the change must be incredibly potent, for it is not easy to alter the enchanted stone of a titan construct.
