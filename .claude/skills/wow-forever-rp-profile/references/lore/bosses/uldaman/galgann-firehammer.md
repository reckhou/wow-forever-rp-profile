# Galgann Firehammer

A boss of Uldaman, number 6 of 8 in Forever's order.

When Bronzebeard dwarves discovered Uldaman, the Dark Iron clan answered with an invasion of the vault. The Dark Irons meant to keep their ancestral foes from the powerful artifacts inside.

Galgann Firehammer, a senator from Shadowforge City, was sent to oversee the continued excavation at Dig Three, so that the emperor could turn the artifacts' destructive power against the Dark Irons' enemies. Geologists of the Shadowforge guard him, and he swears by Thaurissan's beard as he orders intruders slain.
