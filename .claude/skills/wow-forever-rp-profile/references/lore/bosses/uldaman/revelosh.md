# Revelosh

A boss of Uldaman, number 1 of 8 in Forever's order.

Revelosh is a trogg who prowls the Hall of the Keepers in Uldaman. Some luminaries of the Explorers' League in Ironforge have theorized an ancestral link between the dwarves and the troggs. They point to the troggs' hoarding of titan artifacts as a reflection of the dwarves' own reverence for such wondrous devices.

If that is true, Revelosh is the trogg equivalent of an archaeologist. He holds the Shaft of Tsol, one half of the Staff of Prehistoria that opens the way to Ironaya. A dwarf with a historian's mind who meets him would be more inclined to call him a thief.
