# The Lost Dwarves

A boss of Uldaman, number 2 of 8 in Forever's order.

Baelog, Eric and Olaf are three brothers who went into Uldaman seeking fame and, more to the point, fortune. They took a contract from the Explorers' League to clear a path for a group of researchers, but clearing a path turned into getting hopelessly lost in an ancient labyrinth. Then the troggs awoke and overran the dig site. The brothers have been trapped behind enemy lines at Dig Two ever since.

Baelog, the muscle of the trio, worked out how to open the hidden Chamber of Khaz'mul. Olaf, unusually big and not particularly bright, bet him that his beard would reach his knees before they saw another non-trogg. Proud dwarves to the end, they fight to keep the secrets of Uldaman and the Explorers' League out of Horde hands.
