# Grimlok

A boss of Uldaman, number 7 of 8 in Forever's order.

Among the troggs, the biggest, strongest and most vicious often becomes chieftain through displays of power. Grimlok rules the Stonevault troggs in the Stone Vault of Uldaman, and he calls himself king.

He is something of a genius by trogg standards. He has kept control through intimidation and what might be called crude psychological warfare. Grimlok trained a deadly basilisk, the Jadespine Basilisk, and the threat of petrification by his beast keeps any would-be usurper in line.
