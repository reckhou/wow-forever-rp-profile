# Shade of Eranikus

A boss of Sunken Temple, number 3 of 8 in Forever's order.

Eranikus was among the greatest of the green dragons, consort to Ysera the Dreamer and keeper of a shard of the Scepter of the Shifting Sands. After Ysera smashed the Temple of Atal'Hakkar beneath the marshes, she sent him to guard its ruins and stop Hakkar from emerging, and four young drakes served under his command.

Inside the temple the dark forces of the place corrupted Eranikus and his retinue. He went mad not long after he entered and became one of the first dragons to fall to the Emerald Nightmare. Now he is a shade, pulled between the realms of the Emerald Dream and the physical world. Even so he can still spew corrosive acid and lull intruders into a deep slumber.
