# Avatar of Hakkar

A boss of Sunken Temple, number 2 of 8 in Forever's order.

Hakkar the Soulflayer is the Blood God of the Gurubashi trolls, a loa who grew stronger with every drop of blood spilled in his name. His demands for sacrifice never ended, and he longed to enter the physical world so that he could drink the blood of every living creature. In the end the jungle trolls rose against him. In a terrible war they destroyed his avatar and banished him from the world.

The avatar in the Temple of Atal'Hakkar is a skeletal wind serpent, the lasting presence of the god, and its spirit still haunts the drowned ruins as it waits for the right time to regain its power. Long ago Hakkar instructed the Atal'ai to bring forth his avatar with blood ritually drained from living victims. The priests who hold the temple now carry out their gruesome rituals in the hope of summoning him again, and if they succeed, his return could spell doom for Azeroth.
