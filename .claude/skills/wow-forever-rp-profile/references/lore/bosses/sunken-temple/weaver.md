# Weaver

A boss of Sunken Temple, number 6 of 8 in Forever's order.

Weaver is a young green drake, one of four who followed Eranikus into the Temple of Atal'Hakkar, eager to prove themselves by serving a great and powerful wyrm. Ysera had sent them to watch over the ruined temple. The Emerald Nightmare corrupted them there, and the trolls they were set to watch over took the dragons captive.

Weaver keeps the central chamber of the temple together with Dreamscythe, and the two lash out at anyone who comes so far.
