# Atal'alarion

A boss of Sunken Temple, number 1 of 8 in Forever's order.

Atal'alarion is a dire troll of the Atal'ai tribe, huge even among trolls, and his title is Guardian of the Idol. He is the sworn protector of the idol that stands on the lowest floor of the sunken Temple of Atal'Hakkar.

By his own words he was banished once. When he rises he declares that the banishment has ended and that the blood will flow, and he promises to feast on the bones of anyone who comes near his charge.
