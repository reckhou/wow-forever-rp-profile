# Jammal'an the Prophet

A boss of Sunken Temple, number 8 of 8 in Forever's order.

Jammal'an is the spiritual leader of the Atal'ai, the extremist sect of priests who fanatically worship Hakkar, and of the Hakkari of Zul'Gurub and the Swamp of Sorrows. He had what he calls a prophecy, in which the return of the Blood God will bring the Atal'ai immortality. Under his direction the Atal'ai and Hakkari sent their most skilled priests to Zul'Gurub, where Hakkar is to be summoned in the flesh.

His undead bodyguard Ogom the Wretched stands beside him. A troll exiled from the Atal'ai for urging caution suspects that the prophecy was only manipulation, and that Jammal'an's trust in false visions has led his people to doom. Jammal'an himself cries out that Hakkar shall live again.
