# Twilight Lord Kelris

A boss of Blackfathom Deeps, number 6 of 8 in Forever's order.

Twilight Lord Kelris is an orc and the leader of the Twilight's Hammer in Blackfathom Deeps, where a great temple of Elune once stood. The corruption of an Old God seeped up beneath the temple and tainted its sacred Moon Well, and the hydra Aku'Mai rose from the waters. The cultists allied with the naga to hold the grounds, and Kelris leads them in sacrificing innocents to Aku'Mai for power.

When the cultists found the strange three-headed creature, Kelris became convinced it was a herald of the Old Gods' return and his ticket to immortality. He oversees its growth in person, even feeding it his own subjects to satisfy its hunger. Wherever evil showed itself in the region, the Argent Dawn found that Kelris had played a part.
