# Gelihast

A boss of Blackfathom Deeps, number 3 of 8 in Forever's order.

Gelihast is a murloc who fights with two swords and strikes with incredible speed. He did not come from the underwater caves of Blackfathom Deeps. The Old Gods called, and he answered, forcing his way into the deeps and slaughtering nearly a dozen surprised Twilight's Hammer cultists with his twin blades.

Impressed by his ferocity and brutality, the cult allowed him to stay. He built a shrine of his own, where he offers sacrifices and receives blessings from the ominous presence in the temple ruins. The Blindlight murlocs of the deeps follow him.
