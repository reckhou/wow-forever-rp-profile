# The Prophet Skeram

A boss of Ahn'Qiraj Temple, number 1 of 9 in Forever's order.

The Prophet Skeram is a religious leader of the qiraji and the author of the Prophecy of C'Thun. He leads the prophets, one of the three great castes of the qiraji alongside the battleguards and the gladiators, and he stands guard at the entrance to the temple of Ahn'Qiraj.

His prophecy tells of a battle between a Titan and a being of unimaginable evil in the time before time. A Titan fell, and an Old God fell as well, or so it was thought. From the silithid the being made the qiraji in its own image, and they named their creator C'Thun. The record follows the war with the night elves in Silithus, and the copy that survives breaks off in Tanaris, where the qiraji face a bronze dragon.
