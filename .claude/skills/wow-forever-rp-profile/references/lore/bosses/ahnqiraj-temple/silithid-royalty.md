# Silithid Royalty

A boss of Ahn'Qiraj Temple, number 2 of 9 in Forever's order.

Lord Kri, Princess Yauj and Vem are silithid colossi of the Vekniss hive. It is the largest hive known, and since it lies behind the gates of Ahn'Qiraj it has grown for thousands of years with no outside interference. The three go by many names, among them the Trinity, the Triumvirate and the Bug Family.

The silithid are insects without a mind of their own. By the prophecy of the qiraji they were born from the magic of the Well of Eternity, and the qiraji were later made from them.
