# Fankriss the Unyielding

A boss of Ahn'Qiraj Temple, number 4 of 9 in Forever's order.

Fankriss the Unyielding is a sand reaver, a silithid that resembles a solifuge, and one of the lords of the Vekniss hive. He waits at the end of a long tunnel filled with Vekniss soldiers and drones, and more hatchlings crawl from the three tunnels around his chamber.
