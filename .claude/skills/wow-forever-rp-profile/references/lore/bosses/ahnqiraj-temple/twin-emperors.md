# Twin Emperors

A boss of Ahn'Qiraj Temple, number 7 of 9 in Forever's order.

Vek'lor and Vek'nilash are twin brothers who rule the qiraji from the sanctuary of their temple. One works with sorcery and the other with a great sword.

Since the Scarab Wall rose, the Emperors have been shut in the temple behind the barrier.
