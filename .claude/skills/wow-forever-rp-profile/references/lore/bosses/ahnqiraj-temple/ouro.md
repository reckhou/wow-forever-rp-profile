# Ouro

A boss of Ahn'Qiraj Temple, number 8 of 9 in Forever's order.

Ouro is an ancient sand worm, a being of legendary power. He is rumored to have been created by the Old God C'Thun himself as a mockery of life. Sand worms burrow through the soil with frightening speed and burst up beneath unwary prey.

His skin is unnaturally thick, nearly impregnable, yet flexible enough to let him move with the speed and grace of a much smaller creature. He lives in the Temple of Ahn'Qiraj.
