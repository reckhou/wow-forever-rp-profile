# Rath'mael

A boss of Ruins of Lordaeron, number 4 of 7 in Forever's order.

Rath'mael is a skeletal lieutenant of the Scourge, a necromancer who rose within its ranks in the Ruins of Lordaeron. Forsaken scouts say he conjures the undead there, and an unholy fog now covers the streets and the graveyard around Lordamere Overlook, giving the Scourge some measure of stealth.

Deathguard Kristof in Brill wants him driven out of Forsaken lands.
