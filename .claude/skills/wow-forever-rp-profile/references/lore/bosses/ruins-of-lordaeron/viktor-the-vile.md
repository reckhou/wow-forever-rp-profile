# Viktor the Vile

A boss of Ruins of Lordaeron, number 6 of 7 in Forever's order.

Viktor the Vile is a large ghoul, one of the Scourge that has held the ruins of Lordaeron since Arthas razed the city. He keeps to the burned shell of a house.
