# Ambassador Flamelash

A boss of Blackrock Depths, number 16 of 20 in Forever's order.

Ambassador Flamelash is a Flamewaker and a servant of Sulfuron Harbinger. His unwavering service to Ragnaros won him the rank of ambassador to the Dark Iron clan. At first this depraved being despised his dwarven allies, but he has come to respect their taste for destruction.

As a show of solidarity he guards the Chamber of Enchantment, a sacred place where the dwarves empower their weapons. It was Flamelash who charged the molten giant Magmus with guarding the emperor's throne room.
