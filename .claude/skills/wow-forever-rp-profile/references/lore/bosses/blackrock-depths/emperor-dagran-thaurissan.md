# Emperor Dagran Thaurissan

A boss of Blackrock Depths, number 20 of 20 in Forever's order.

Emperor Dagran Thaurissan rules the Dark Iron clan. He descends from Sorcerer-Thane Thaurissan and Modgud, and it was the sorcerer-thane who first called Ragnaros the Firelord into Azeroth. Under Ragnaros's control Dagran tore the heart out of Blackrock Mountain and raised Blackrock Spire. He rules not from the city his forebear founded but from the depths near the gates of the Molten Core, with charisma, sorcery and an iron will.

He took the hammer Ironfoe from the captive Marshal Windsor and wields it himself. At his side sits Princess Moira Bronzebeard of the rival Bronzebeard clan. Debate rages over whether he ensorcelled his bride or she joined him willingly. Whatever the truth, so long as he lives the Dark Irons remain pawns of Ragnaros, and a dire threat to kingdoms throughout Azeroth.
