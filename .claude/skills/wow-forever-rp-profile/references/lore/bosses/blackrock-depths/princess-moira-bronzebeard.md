# Princess Moira Bronzebeard

A boss of Blackrock Depths, number 19 of 20 in Forever's order.

Princess Moira Bronzebeard is the daughter of King Magni Bronzebeard of Ironforge, and she sits beside Emperor Dagran Thaurissan in the Imperial Seat. Kharan Mighthammer, the guard Magni assigned to watch over her, failed in his task and now sits in a cell of the Detention Block.

Magni believes that Thaurissan holds his daughter under an evil spell that only the emperor's death can break. Moira tells a different story. She calls Thaurissan a great and honorable dwarf, and she carries his unborn child. If the emperor falls she weeps for him and declares that the heir to Ironforge will be a Dark Iron dwarf, whether her father approves or not.
