# Hurley Blackbreath

A boss of Blackrock Depths, number 12 of 20 in Forever's order.

To call Hurley Blackbreath a regular at the Grim Guzzler is an understatement. In more than a decade the mischievous drunk has left the tavern only once, to steal a top-secret recipe for Thunderbrew Lager. Now and then he sobers up enough to brew it himself, and he claims the lager grants unimaginable strength and courage.

Ragnar Thunderbrew in Kharanos wants his family's recipe back and every keg destroyed. Hurley guards his kegs fiercely and shouts at anyone who goes near them.
