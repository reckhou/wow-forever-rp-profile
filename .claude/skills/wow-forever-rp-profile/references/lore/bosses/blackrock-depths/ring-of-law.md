# Ring of Law

A boss of Blackrock Depths, number 4 of 20 in Forever's order.

The Dark Iron clan has one punishment for crimes committed against it, and that punishment is death. By ancient tradition the guilty pay the blood price in the Ring of the Law, where they must fight savage creatures gathered from across Azeroth.

High Justice Grimstone presides. He appears in the ring to pronounce the sentence and declares that the Sons of Thaurissan will watch the condemned perish. Crowds gather on the upper levels to watch. After the first beasts fall, Grimstone returns to announce that the real punishment lies ahead. To this day nobody knows whether anyone has survived the ring.
