# Fineous Darkvire

A boss of Blackrock Depths, number 8 of 20 in Forever's order.

Fineous Darkvire is chief architect of the Dark Iron dwarves in Shadowforge City, a title he earned by passing off the ideas of more gifted masons as his own. He took the post after Franclorn Forgewright, the builder behind the stonewrought method and much of the Dark Iron stronghold, and with it came Franclorn's great hammer, Ironfel.

Franclorn's will to live gave way once the Spire was finished, but his ghost has no peace. He sees Darkvire as a man of little talent and bad motives, and fears that a craftsman with real skill could one day use Ironfel to build something disastrous. The ghost wants the hammer back before it can be put to ill use.
