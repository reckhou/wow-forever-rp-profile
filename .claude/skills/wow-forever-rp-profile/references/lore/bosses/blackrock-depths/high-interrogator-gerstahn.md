# High Interrogator Gerstahn

A boss of Blackrock Depths, number 1 of 20 in Forever's order.

High Interrogator Gerstahn is a cultist of the Twilight's Hammer who keeps the Detention Block of the Dark Iron clan. She has always indulged in life's finer things, from exotic foods and fine clothes to torture tools set with gems.

Prisoners fill the cells around her, and their cries echo through the halls day and night. Gerstahn calls the noise her symphony of screams and is always eager to add another voice to the chorus. She alone carries the key that opens every cell.
