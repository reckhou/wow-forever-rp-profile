# Lord Incendius

A boss of Blackrock Depths, number 6 of 20 in Forever's order.

Lord Incendius is a fire elemental who learned to harness his destructive powers from Baron Geddon in the Molten Core. The student proved far more efficient than his teacher expected, and Geddon began to fear that Incendius might take his place.

To be rid of the danger, Geddon sent the blazing elemental out of the Molten Core. Incendius now guards the Black Anvil, the fabled anvil of the Dark Iron dwarves in Shadowforge City.
