# Bael'Gar

A boss of Blackrock Depths, number 9 of 20 in Forever's order.

Bael'Gar is a molten giant, a being of pure flame born of the fires of Ragnaros. He lumbers along the Dark Iron Highway, a stunning feat of dwarven engineering carved into Blackrock Depths.

Ragnaros posted him at one end of the highway to guard a massive sealed door. According to legend, only the Firelord and the highest-ranking members of the Dark Iron clan know what lies beyond it.
