# Warder Stilgiss

A boss of Blackrock Depths, number 7 of 20 in Forever's order.

Warder Stilgiss is a Dark Iron mage who spent years in devoted service to Emperor Thaurissan. The emperor's trust brought him the task of protecting the clan's coffers in the Black Vault.

His close friend Houndmaster Grebmar marked the occasion with a gift, one of his vicious dogs, a red hound named Verek. Stilgiss keeps the beast at his side to scare off the vault's would-be thieves.
