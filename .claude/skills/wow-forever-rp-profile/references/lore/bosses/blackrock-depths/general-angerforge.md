# General Angerforge

A boss of Blackrock Depths, number 10 of 20 in Forever's order.

General Angerforge commands the armies of the Dark Iron clan. A lifetime of study made him an expert on dwarven military history and tactics, and he put that learning into a scathing treatise on his clan's failures in past conflicts. The treatise earned him the fury of Emperor Dagran Thaurissan.

Politics aside, Angerforge is Dark Iron to the core and will bleed, and die if need be, to protect his people and his emperor. The Horde knows him as the Butcher of Blackrock, and Warlord Goretooth blames him for the attacks that slaughtered the 109th division of the Kargath Expeditionary Force. Marshal Windsor believes that Angerforge holds part of the information the Dark Irons took from him.
