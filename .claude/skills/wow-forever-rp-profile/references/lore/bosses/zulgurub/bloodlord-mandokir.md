# Bloodlord Mandokir

A boss of Zul'Gurub, number 4 of 10 in Forever's order.

Bloodlord Mandokir is a jungle troll and one of the most powerful lieutenants of Hakkar the Soulflayer. He leads the Gurubashi, the strongest troll tribe, who dwell in the ancient city of Zul'Gurub.

When Hakkar was to be summoned into the mortal world, Mandokir and his people were reluctant and rose up against the plan. Once the blood god entered the world, their resistance ended, and they soon carried out his every command. Mandokir is rarely seen without Ohgan, the raptor he rides, and the two fight side by side.
