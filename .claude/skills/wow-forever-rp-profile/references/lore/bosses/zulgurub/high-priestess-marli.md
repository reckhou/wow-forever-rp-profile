# High Priestess Mar'li

A boss of Zul'Gurub, number 3 of 10 in Forever's order.

High Priestess Mar'li serves Shadra, the spider god, and was one of the five high priests the trolls sent into Zul'Gurub against Hakkar the Soulflayer. Each of them was a powerful champion of a primal god, yet all fell under Hakkar's sway.

Mar'li now feeds the power of Shadra into the blood god. In her lair she hatches broods of spiders, and she changes into the form of a great spider herself.
