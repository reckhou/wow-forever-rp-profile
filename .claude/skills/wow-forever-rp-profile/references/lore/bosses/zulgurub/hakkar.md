# Hakkar

A boss of Zul'Gurub, number 10 of 10 in Forever's order.

Hakkar the Soulflayer is the blood god of the trolls, a vile and bloodthirsty spirit. Some 1,500 years before the Dark Portal opened, famine and terror ruled the broken troll kingdoms. The Gurubashi called on ancient forces for aid, and Hakkar answered. He gave them the secrets of blood and helped them spread their civilization across most of Stranglethorn Vale. But he wanted ever more, and demanded that souls be sacrificed to him every day.

The other trolls turned against him. Gurubashi and Zandalari rose together in a war that shattered the young empire, destroyed Hakkar's avatar and drove his priests, the Atal'ai, from Zul'Gurub. The exiles fled north to the Swamp of Sorrows and raised a temple to him there, until the dragon Aspect Ysera smashed it beneath the marsh.

In time the Atal'ai learned that Hakkar could take physical form only in Zul'Gurub. Their prophet Jammal'an promised them immortality, and their priests succeeded. Now the Soulflayer sits in the heart of the ruins and has enslaved five high priests of the trolls' gods, who feed him their power.
