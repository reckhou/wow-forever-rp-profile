# Darkmaster Gandling

A boss of Scholomance, number 14 of 14 in Forever's order.

Darkmaster Gandling is a human necromancer and the headmaster of the School of Necromancy at Caer Darrow. Kel'Thuzad appointed him personally, and he took the post after Araj the Summoner, who had led the school before him.

Gandling is mortal, unlike Ras Frostwhisper, the lich who rules the site and guards it for the Scourge. The Cult of the Damned uses the school to train its mortal agents, and Gandling runs it. He greets those who intrude on his study by announcing that school is in session.
