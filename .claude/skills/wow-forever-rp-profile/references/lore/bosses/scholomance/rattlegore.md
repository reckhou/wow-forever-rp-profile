# Rattlegore

A boss of Scholomance, number 3 of 14 in Forever's order.

Rattlegore is a golem of bone, one of the bone golems that haunt the halls of Scholomance. He lurks in a chamber directly beneath the Great Ossuary, the room where the plagued dragon whelps are kept, close to the dungeon of Jandice Barov.
