# Lorekeeper Polkelt

A boss of Scholomance, number 10 of 14 in Forever's order.

Lorekeeper Polkelt was once the keeper of knowledge in Scholomance. That knowledge has been lost, and he is now a shambling, unintelligent zombie. He presides over the Hall of Secrets, where a crowd of zombies spreads sickness among all who come near.
