# The Ravenian

A boss of Scholomance, number 11 of 14 in Forever's order.

The Ravenian is a wight, and one of the mightiest agents of Kel'Thuzad. He lives in the recesses of Scholomance, in a vault filled with water, and he is always ready to devour any student who shows a sign of weakness.
