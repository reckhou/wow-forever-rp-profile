# Ragnaros

A boss of Molten Core, number 10 of 10 in Forever's order.

Ragnaros the Firelord is the Elemental Lord of fire. He ruled the Firelands of the Elemental Plane from Sulfuron Keep, which stands in the center of a vast sky of fire, and his lieutenants fought against the titans and betrayed Thunderaan, Prince of Air.

About two hundred and fifty years ago the Dark Iron dwarves renewed the War of the Three Hammers. Sorcerer-Thane Thaurissan, losing the war, gathered seven of his most powerful allies in the Molten Core and called on the ancient powers below the world. Ragnaros burst into Azeroth, and his arrival created the volcanic wastes around Blackrock Mountain. Thaurissan and the seven died, and the surviving Dark Irons were enslaved. Later, Emperor Dagran Thaurissan ruled them for the Firelord and resented it.

Cut off from his own plane, Ragnaros is only a shadow of what he was. He lies at the bottom of Blackrock Mountain, looking for a way home and gathering strength to burn Azeroth. He has learned to make life from stone and plans an army of golems.
