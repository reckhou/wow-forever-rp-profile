# Gehennas

A boss of Molten Core, number 3 of 10 in Forever's order.

Gehennas was pulled out of the Elemental Plane together with the other flamewakers shortly after Thaurissan accidentally summoned Ragnaros. Flamewakers are elementals of flesh and blood, where fire elementals are beings of pure flame, and Gehennas works shadow magic.

He sits near the bottom of the Firelord's hierarchy and covets the power and station of those above him. Nobody who serves beside him trusts him, apart from his two guards, who believe they might gain if their overseer rises. He has a long quarrel with Lucifron that dates from their time in the Elemental Plane. Gehennas guards the Rune of Mohn in the Molten Core with two flamewakers.
