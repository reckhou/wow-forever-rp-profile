# Shazzrah

A boss of Molten Core, number 5 of 10 in Forever's order.

Of all the flamewakers who followed Ragnaros into Azeroth, Shazzrah is the most talented in arcane magic. He knows that Baron Geddon suspects Garr of treachery, and he is glad of the quarrel, because he wants both halves of the Talisman of Elemental Binding for himself. He hopes to find a way to siphon its energies for his own use.

Ragnaros likely knew about his servant's talent for duplicity when he split the talisman and gave the halves to Geddon and Garr. Shazzrah waits and schemes in the Molten Core, in the same part of the halls where Geddon makes his rounds, and guards the Rune of Mazj.
