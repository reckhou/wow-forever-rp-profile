# Sulfuron Harbinger

A boss of Molten Core, number 7 of 10 in Forever's order.

Sulfuron Harbinger is the herald of Ragnaros and one of the most favored of the flamewakers. His true name is not known. From the depths of the Molten Core he commands Ambassador Flamelash and the lesser flamewakers who serve in the outside world, and he answers only to Majordomo Executus.

He guards the Rune of Koro, one of the runes that empower the Firelord's servants, and four of Ragnaros's most fervent priests stand beside him. He watches Shazzrah in particular, certain that Shazzrah means to betray the Firelord. Sulfuron prepares for the day when the lord of fire claims Azeroth as his own.
