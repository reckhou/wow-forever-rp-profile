# Tuten'kash

A boss of Razorfen Downs, number 1 of 6 in Forever's order.

Tuten'kash is a crypt fiend, a large arachnid raised to serve the Scourge, and one of its deadliest commanders in Razorfen Downs. He was blinded before his conversion to undeath, and he makes up for it by hanging fine, almost invisible webs through his lair in the Caller's Chamber. The webs carry every footstep and the lightest touch to him, and he sends out his minions to ambush whoever has wandered in.

Strong vibrations can drive him into a disoriented rage and draw him out of his hiding place deep in the recesses of the Downs.
