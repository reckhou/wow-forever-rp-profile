# Mordresh Fire Eye

A boss of Razorfen Downs, number 3 of 6 in Forever's order.

Mordresh Fire Eye was an orc shaman, sent from Orgrimmar to find out what was happening in Razorfen Downs. He was slain there and remade as an undead servant of the Scourge. The lord of Razorfen Downs judged the elements unreliable and filled him with blazing fel energies to sear the flesh of the living.

In mockery of his old calling as a spiritual leader, Mordresh now uses his power to drive lesser undead into a violent frenzy. He stands on a pile of skulls and bones and calls to the skeletons around him, promising that they will spread across the barren land and enslave the quilboar until the Scourge rules the world.
