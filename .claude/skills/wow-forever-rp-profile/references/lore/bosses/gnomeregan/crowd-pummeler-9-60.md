# Crowd Pummeler 9-60

A boss of Gnomeregan, number 4 of 5 in Forever's order.

In Gnomeregan's heyday, inventors competed for status by pitting their creations against one another. The mekgineer Oglethorpe Obnoticus built an unbeatable robot and earned the respect of the high tinker's court. Sicco Thermaplugg stole the blueprints, built a giant version of the machine and set his forgery loose.

The horrific results drove the ruling council to banish the innocent Obnoticus. The Crowd Pummeler 9-60 is still one of Thermaplugg's favorite stolen inventions.
