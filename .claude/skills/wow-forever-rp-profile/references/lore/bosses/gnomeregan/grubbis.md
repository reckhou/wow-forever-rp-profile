# Grubbis

A boss of Gnomeregan, number 1 of 5 in Forever's order.

When Thermaplugg flooded Gnomeregan with radioactive gas, the plan was meant to anger the troggs and kill the gnomes, and it worked with brutal effect. One trogg reacted oddly to the substance. Grubbis grew, and kept growing. Thermaplugg had not anticipated the side effect, but he never wastes a valuable and dangerous resource.

The giant trogg now patrols the tunnels of Gnomeregan with his loyal basilisk, Chomper. The pair sniff out gas leaks and tasty fungi, and they like intruders even better.
