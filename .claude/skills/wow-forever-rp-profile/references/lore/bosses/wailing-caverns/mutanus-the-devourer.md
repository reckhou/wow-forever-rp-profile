# Mutanus the Devourer

A boss of Wailing Caverns, number 8 of 8 in Forever's order.

Mutanus the Devourer came out of the darkest corners of Naralex's twisted mind. The corruption that plagues the Emerald Dream took the shape of the druid's worst enemy, the murloc, and sent a colossal albino one into the waking world.

Whatever force infected Naralex's dreams has ordered Mutanus to stop anyone from waking him. When the Disciple of Naralex tries the ritual to wake the sleeping druid, Mutanus appears to devour those who come to free him.
