# Verdan the Everliving

A boss of Wailing Caverns, number 7 of 8 in Forever's order.

Verdan the Everliving is a huge elemental that formed long ago from the lush growth of the Wailing Caverns and served as their guardian. Little is known about it from the time before Naralex arrived.

Verdan could not escape the corruption that spread through its home. The gentle giant has become an enormous abomination, allied with the insane druids, that crushes any living creature that comes too near its lair.
