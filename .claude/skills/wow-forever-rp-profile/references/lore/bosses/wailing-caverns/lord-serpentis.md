# Lord Serpentis

A boss of Wailing Caverns, number 6 of 8 in Forever's order.

Lord Serpentis was once the finest student of Naralex. He longed to become a shan'do, an honored teacher, as Naralex was, and that wish led to his downfall. When insanity took root in him, he rallied his brethren to his side and formed the Druids of the Fang.

Once a druid of great power and standing, he is corrupted by Naralex's nightmare and leads the Druids of the Fang in the depths of the caverns, with the thunder lizard Skum as his pet.
