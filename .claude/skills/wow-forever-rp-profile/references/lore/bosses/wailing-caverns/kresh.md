# Kresh

A boss of Wailing Caverns, number 3 of 8 in Forever's order.

Kresh is a giant, ancient turtle who wanders the river and the caves near the entrance of the Wailing Caverns. The turtles that live there were not touched by Naralex's nightmare, and Kresh, whose resilient mind shrugs it off, goes about his life without noticing the evil around him. He has a happy disposition.
