# Lord Pythas

A boss of Wailing Caverns, number 4 of 8 in Forever's order.

Lord Pythas was once Aryn, a childhood friend of Naralex and a seasoned warrior in his personal guard. He took to druidism quickly, but the training did not protect him when the corruption came for his sanity.

He now goes by Pythas and joins his skill in melee fighting to a druid's magic against anyone who intrudes, standing with the Druids of the Fang.
