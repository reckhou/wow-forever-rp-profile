# Overlord Wyrmthalak

A boss of Blackrock Spire, number 9 of 14 in Forever's order.

Wyrmthalak is a black dragonspawn and the overlord and taskmaster of the lower spire, the lesser city of the Blackrock citadel. He holds the Chamber of Battle at its heart, where envoys of the Smolderthorn trolls, the Spirestone ogres and the Blackrock orcs all take his orders. He is the leader of the Bloodaxe Legion, and he answers to General Drakkisath above him.

His loyalty belongs to Nefarian alone. Wyrmthalak has no love for the false Horde or its brutish warriors, and he would sacrifice any number of Blackhand's minions to protect his master. For now he oversees the lower spire's forces as they prepare their assault on the dwarves.
