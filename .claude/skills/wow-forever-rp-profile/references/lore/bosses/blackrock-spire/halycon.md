# Halycon

A boss of Blackrock Spire, number 7 of 14 in Forever's order.

Halycon is a giant red and black worg, the den mother of the Blackrock orcs' war pack. She comes from a long line of great worgs bred for ferocity and loyalty, and over the years she has borne an astonishing number of savage offspring with her mate, Gizrul the Slavener. The orcs take her pups as mounts and hunting beasts, and in return Halycon and her brood are fed captured Dark Iron dwarves, whose gnawed remains litter the floor of her lair.

Rumor says she is a demonic hound that a pit lord of the Burning Legion gave to Rend Blackhand. The truth is plainer and just as frightening. The worgs of Blackrock come from Halycon and her mate, and from no demon.
