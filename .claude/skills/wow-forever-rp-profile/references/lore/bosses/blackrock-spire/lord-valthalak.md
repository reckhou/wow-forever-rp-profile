# Lord Valthalak

A boss of Blackrock Spire, number 14 of 14 in Forever's order.

Lord Valthalak was a drakonid noble and a warlock of immense power. Before General Drakkisath rose to power, Valthalak was General of Blackrock Spire, and he held the chamber in the upper spire where the Beast now prowls. A necromancer as well as a warlock, he kept a spellbook of great value and wore a magical amulet.

A goblin smuggler from Booty Bay, Gremnik Rizzlesprang, hired the Veiled Blade mercenary company to steal that book. The mercenaries killed Valthalak and pried the book from his hands, then fought over the amulet and broke it into pieces. His revenge came after his death. Valthalak's spirit sent spectral assassins to hunt the company down one by one, and it still demands that what was stolen be returned.
