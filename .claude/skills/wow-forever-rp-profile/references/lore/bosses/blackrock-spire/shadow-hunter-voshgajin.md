# Shadow Hunter Vosh'gajin

A boss of Blackrock Spire, number 2 of 14 in Forever's order.

Vosh'gajin is a forest troll of the Smolderthorn tribe and a shadow hunter, schooled in the old troll arts of hexes, curses and healing. Among trolls she is renowned for her beauty and just as well known for a psychotic streak. She holds the halls of Tazz'Alor together with War Master Voone.

Her cruelty has a long memory. The troll weaponsmith Kilram, who now works in Everlook in Winterspring, was once her lover. He still carries the scar she gave him, which she called a love tap, and he wants vengeance. Legend says that one use of her snakestone will keep an axe razor sharp for a thousand years.
