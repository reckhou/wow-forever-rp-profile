# Urok Doomhowl

A boss of Blackrock Spire, number 5 of 14 in Forever's order.

Urok Doomhowl is the real power among the spire's ogres. Highlord Omokk wears the title, but Urok charmed his trusted aide with insidious magic and rules the ogres through him. Any ogre who challenges the highlord is struck down, and Urok has gone to ruthless lengths to keep up the pretense.

He is cruel to those who challenge him directly. Warosh, once a great mage of the Spirestone clan, stood against him. Urok stole Warosh's mojo and cursed him into the shape of a trogg, and the ogre mage still wanders the lower spire in that form.
