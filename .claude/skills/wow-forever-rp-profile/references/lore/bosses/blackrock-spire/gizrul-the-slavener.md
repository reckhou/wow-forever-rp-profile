# Gizrul the Slavener

A boss of Blackrock Spire, number 8 of 14 in Forever's order.

Gizrul the Slavener is the mate of Halycon, and very few of the Dark Horde have ever laid eyes on him. He is a reclusive and unpredictable beast who keeps to a shadowy den and comes out only when Halycon calls to him. Together the two have sired some of the largest and most ferocious worgs in the history of the orcs. Nobody who has met him has lived to describe him.
