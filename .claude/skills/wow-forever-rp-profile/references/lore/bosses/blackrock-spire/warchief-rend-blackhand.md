# Warchief Rend Blackhand

A boss of Blackrock Spire, number 11 of 14 in Forever's order.

Dal'rend Blackhand, called Rend, is the bloodthirsty son of Blackhand the Destroyer, the first Warchief of the Horde. When the Horde fell in the Second War, Rend and his brother Maim led the remnants of their clan into Blackrock Spire and made it their stronghold. There Rend declared himself Warchief and gathered orcs, forest trolls and ogres into the Dark Horde, which calls itself the true Horde. He holds that his bloodline gives him the right to lead the Horde, and he means to overthrow Thrall.

The spire was already held by the Dark Iron dwarves, and Rend and Maim fought them for it in a long war. The dwarves were driven from the upper halls only after Maim had died and the black dragons had come. Rend allied with Nefarian, and the two have worked to create the chromatic dragonflight, which they hope to unleash on the world. Rend rides to war on Gyth, a huge chromatic drake.
