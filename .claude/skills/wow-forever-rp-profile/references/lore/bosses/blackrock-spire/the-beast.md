# The Beast

A boss of Blackrock Spire, number 12 of 14 in Forever's order.

The Beast is a core hound, one of the fiery hounds that serve Ragnaros the Firelord. Nefarian's dragonkin captured him and tortured him for their amusement, as they delight in doing to the Firelord's captured minions. Where others broke, the Beast stayed lucid through the agony, and the dragonkin retrained him as a guard for the upper spire. His ferocity makes him ideal for the work.

He now holds the Furnace, the chamber that once belonged to Lord Valthalak. In the pits at the Molten Span he swallowed a gnome whole. Pip Quickwit, an inventor testing a lava-proof suit, has lived in the Beast's belly for some time.
