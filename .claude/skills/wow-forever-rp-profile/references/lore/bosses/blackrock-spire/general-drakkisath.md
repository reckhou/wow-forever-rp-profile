# General Drakkisath

A boss of Blackrock Spire, number 13 of 14 in Forever's order.

General Drakkisath is a black dragonspawn who rules the dragonspawn armies of Nefarian, son of Deathwing. He holds the pinnacle of Blackrock Spire, in a throne room behind the Halls of Ascension, and he took the rank of General of Blackrock Spire after Lord Valthalak. A cunning and ruthless strategist, he commands Nefarian's armies in their war against Ragnaros.

From his throne he directs the drive to push the Dark Iron dwarves out of the depths of Blackrock Mountain. His forces have already won victories against the dwarves, and they stand ready to clear them from the mountain once and for all.
