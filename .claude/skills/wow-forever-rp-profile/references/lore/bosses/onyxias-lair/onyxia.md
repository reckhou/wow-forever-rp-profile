# Onyxia

A boss of Onyxia's Lair, number 1 of 1 in Forever's order.

Onyxia is a daughter of Deathwing and the twin of Nefarian, the tyrant of Blackrock Spire. She is small for a black dragon of her age, and clever. Her breath melts stone and iron, and her bites and tail lashes are quick and nearly impossible to avoid. Cornered, she takes to the air and burns her enemies from above. She is one of Nefarian's mightiest supporters and has been seen traveling to and from Blackrock Mountain.

Onyxia likes mortal shapes, because they let her steer the affairs of kingdoms in secret. In Stormwind she lives as Lady Katrana Prestor, the noblewoman who stands beside the boy king Anduin Wrynn. With a pendant known as the Dragon's Eye she has bent Highlord Bolvar Fordragon to her will. Marshal Reginald Windsor exposed her in Stormwind Keep and died for it, and she fled.

Her lair is a fiery cave in the Wyrmbog of Dustwallow Marsh. Her brood has overrun the ogre village of Stonemaul, and her eggs are said to lie hidden in the cave. Anyone who nears them will meet her wrath.
