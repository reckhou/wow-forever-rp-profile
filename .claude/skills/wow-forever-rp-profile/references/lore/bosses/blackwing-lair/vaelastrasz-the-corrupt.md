# Vaelastrasz the Corrupt

A boss of Blackwing Lair, number 2 of 8 in Forever's order.

Vaelastrasz, called Vaelan in his visage form, is a dragon of the red dragonflight. He kept his flight's shard of the Scepter of the Shifting Sands for nearly a thousand years, after Fandral Staghelm shattered the scepter in his grief. He saw Nefarian and the black dragonflight as a great danger to Azeroth and swore to hunt them to the end of his days.

He went into Blackwing Lair alone. There Nefarian, in his human guise as Lord Victor Nefarius, drained his strength, bent him to his will and took the shard. Nefarius threatens to destroy it. Vaelastrasz now serves unwillingly and swings between threats and pleas for forgiveness. He cries out that a black fire rages in his heart and begs Alexstrasza to help him resist.
