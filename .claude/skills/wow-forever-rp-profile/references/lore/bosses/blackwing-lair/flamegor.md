# Flamegor

A boss of Blackwing Lair, number 6 of 8 in Forever's order.

Flamegor is the third of the three black drakes that guard the way to Nefarian, and he keeps the upper room of the Crimson Laboratories in Blackwing Lair. He is a drake of the black dragonflight and breathes shadow flame, as his fellow drakes and their master do. Chromaggus waits in the room beyond.
