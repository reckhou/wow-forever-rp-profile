# Nefarian

A boss of Blackwing Lair, number 8 of 8 in Forever's order.

Nefarian is the son of the black Aspect Neltharion, whom the world calls Deathwing, and the brother of Onyxia. He wears a human guise as Victor Nefarius, Lord of Blackrock. The Blackrock clan and several clans of ogres answer to him, and he rules from his lair at the top of Blackrock Spire.

After the Second War he reached out to the Dark Horde in Blackrock Mountain and struck a bargain with Rend Blackhand, then manipulated the orcs into letting the black dragonflight use the mountain as its base. He set up a hidden laboratory in the upper spire, where he works to rebuild his flight and finish what his father failed to do. He experiments with the blood of every dragonflight, much of it taken from living dragons, to breed a chromatic flight of unstoppable warriors and unspeakable hybrids.

The dwarves hold the mountain's volcanic depths under Ragnaros, and Nefarian has vowed to crush them and claim all of Blackrock Mountain. The red dragonflight has marked him, and he has enslaved Vaelastrasz.
