# Razorgore the Untamed

A boss of Blackwing Lair, number 1 of 8 in Forever's order.

Razorgore the Untamed is a dragonspawn of the black dragonflight, the most powerful known to the world, and by his own nature he serves no master. Nefarian found a way around that. He made an Orb of Domination that lets the Blackrock clan bend Razorgore to its will, and Grethok the Controller holds it.

Razorgore guards the clutch of black dragon eggs in the first chamber of Blackwing Lair, which Nefarian values highly. He commands a garrison of orcs and dragonspawn. He will stop at nothing to protect the eggs. When he breaks free of the orb, he cries that the device will never torment him again.
