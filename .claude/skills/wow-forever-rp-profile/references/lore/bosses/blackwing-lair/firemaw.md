# Firemaw

A boss of Blackwing Lair, number 4 of 8 in Forever's order.

Firemaw is a black drake of the black dragonflight and one of three drakes that guard the way to their master, Nefarian. He patrols the length of the first of the Crimson Laboratories, the halls of Blackwing Lair lined with Nefarian's technicians, spellbinders and warlocks. Like Ebonroc, Flamegor and Nefarian himself, he breathes shadow flame.
