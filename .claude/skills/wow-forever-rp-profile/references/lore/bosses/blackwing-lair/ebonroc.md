# Ebonroc

A boss of Blackwing Lair, number 5 of 8 in Forever's order.

Ebonroc is one of three powerful black drakes that guard the way to their master, Nefarian. He is a drake of the black dragonflight and patrols the semicircular ramp that leads to the upper room of the Crimson Laboratories in Blackwing Lair. Like the other two drakes and Nefarian himself, he breathes shadow flame.
