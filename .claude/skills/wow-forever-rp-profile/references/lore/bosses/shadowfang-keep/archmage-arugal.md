# Archmage Arugal

A boss of Shadowfang Keep, number 8 of 8 in Forever's order.

Arugal was a mage of Dalaran who sat among the Kirin Tor and carried the title of archmage. Dalar Dawnweaver, a Kirin Tor mage who has since risen among the Forsaken, remembers him as a charlatan whose spellcraft was so transparent that he was not fit to clean chamber pots in Dalaran, and says he propped up his weak magic with enchanted items.

When the Scourge came, Arugal released the worgen to help the Kirin Tor mages of Dalaran against the undead. The beasts slipped his control and began to spread their curse. They overran Baron Silverlaine's keep above Pyrewood Village and killed the baron, and Arugal, mad with guilt, took the worgen as his children and renamed the place Shadowfang Keep.

From there he spread the curse through Silverpine, to the Moonrage and Shadowfang packs and the people of Pyrewood. The Kirin Tor sent Sorcerer Ashcrombe to check on his work against the undead, and the Forsaken now want his head. In his study he still keeps a book by Ur, a Dalaran mage who studied other worlds.
