# Baron Silverlaine

A boss of Shadowfang Keep, number 3 of 8 in Forever's order.

Baron Silverlaine was the lord of the keep that looks down on Pyrewood Village. It was called Silverlaine Keep then, and the baron lived there with his household and his soldiers until Archmage Arugal's worgen overran the walls and killed him.

Death did not release him. His cursed ghost still walks the dining room, attended by some of his servants, and he meets every stranger who enters his home as a trespasser who must be punished.
