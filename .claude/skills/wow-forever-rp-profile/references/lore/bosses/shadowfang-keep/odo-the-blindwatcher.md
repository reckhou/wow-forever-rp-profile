# Odo the Blindwatcher

A boss of Shadowfang Keep, number 5 of 8 in Forever's order.

Odo the Blindwatcher is a worgen of Arugal's Shadowfang pack. He lives in the rickety wooden structure that hangs off the side of the keep, and he shares it with two pet bats, a Blood Seeker and a Vile Bat.

He lost his sight long ago, which is how he got his name. Over the years he learned to rely on his other senses, as his bats do.
