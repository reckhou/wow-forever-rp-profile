# Fenrus the Devourer

A boss of Shadowfang Keep, number 6 of 8 in Forever's order.

Fenrus is the pet of Archmage Arugal, a worg of unbelievable size that prowls the main study of Arugal's chambers. He may have been shaped by dark magic, whether from Arugal himself or from the worgen who serve him.

Whenever Fenrus senses an intruder he lets out a thundering roar that can be heard from every corner of the keep.
