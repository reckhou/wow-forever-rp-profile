# Razorclaw the Butcher

A boss of Shadowfang Keep, number 2 of 8 in Forever's order.

Razorclaw the Butcher lairs in what was once the kitchen of Shadowfang Keep. When his master, Arugal, brought him into the keep, he slaughtered many innocent humans there. The name he carries comes from that butchery.
