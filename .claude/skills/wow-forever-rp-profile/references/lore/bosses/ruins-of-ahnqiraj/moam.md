# Moam

A boss of Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj, number 3 of 6 in Forever's order.

During the War of the Shifting Sands, the dragons Arygos, Merithra of the Dream and Caelestrasz gave themselves to help make the Scarab Wall. The wall held the qiraji in, yet the empire did not sit idle. The dark master of Ahn'Qiraj ordered Emperor Vek'lor to build an obsidian destroyer greater than the rest. His twin Vek'nilash shaped a body of volcanic rock and Vek'lor strengthened it with magic. Vek'lor named it Moam, and the dark master gave it life.

Moam proved more versatile than earlier destroyers and could summon burning elementals of pure mana. Vek'lor then learned the source of that power. The three dragons had never been granted death. Held alive in Ahn'Qiraj, they had their magic drained and corrupted to feed Moam and the master's other creations.
