# Buru the Gorger

A boss of Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj, number 4 of 6 in Forever's order.

Buru the Gorger is a silithid colossus, a huge creature with four legs, scythe-like arms, gouging horns and a thick armored shell. He lives in the Hatchery of the Hive'Zara hive within the ruins of Ahn'Qiraj, where he guards the eggs of the qiraji. A hatchling of the hive emerges from each egg that breaks. Buru can be weakened only by the destruction of those eggs, and only then can anything break through his immensely thick armor.
