# Highland Horror

A boss of Excavation Site: Wetlands, number 3 of 4 in Forever's order.

Highland Horrors are bog beasts of the Wetlands highlands. Rethiel the Greenwarden senses a corruption seeping through the mountain gully where they live. The creatures there never had the sea's hardening salt, so they react bitterly to the taint and have little strength against such evil.

One of them lurks at the Lost Dig Site. Rethiel judges the matter worse than feared, since an ancient energy is stirring, and wants the excavators told to stop digging for their own safety.
