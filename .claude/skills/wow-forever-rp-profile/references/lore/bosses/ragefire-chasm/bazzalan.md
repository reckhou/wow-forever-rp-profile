# Bazzalan

A boss of Ragefire Chasm, number 4 of 4 in Forever's order.

Bazzalan is a satyr who leads the Searing Blade cultists alongside the orc warlock Jergosh. His motives are unclear. Like all satyrs he is swift and cunning, and he holds a ledge above Jergosh in the cult's hiding place beneath Orgrimmar.
