# Jergosh the Invoker

A boss of Ragefire Chasm, number 3 of 4 in Forever's order.

Jergosh is an orc who rejected the ideals of his people and took up the dark and demonic arts of the warlock. He is one of the leaders of the Searing Blade, a sect of the Shadow Council that has taken refuge in Ragefire Chasm beneath Orgrimmar.

There he schemes, and the plot is dangerous enough to have drawn the attention of Thrall himself. Many of his cultists believe they are the next rising power among warlocks, ready to replace the Shadow Council, but only Jergosh and his fellow leaders are more than expendable.
