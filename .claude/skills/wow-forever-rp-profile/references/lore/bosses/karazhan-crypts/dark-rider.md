# Dark Rider

A boss of Karazhan Crypts, number 3 of 10 in Forever's order.

The Dark Riders haunt Duskwood and Deadwind Pass in search of magical relics. Legend says they are invisible unless they wish to be seen, and they are known as formidable warriors.

Beneath Karazhan one of them waits, bound to three relics, a sword, a scythe and a staff. When all three are laid on the shadowy weapons that mark his altar in the jail, he rises. He speaks of riding once more, of apocalypse, and of waiting until his next summoning.
