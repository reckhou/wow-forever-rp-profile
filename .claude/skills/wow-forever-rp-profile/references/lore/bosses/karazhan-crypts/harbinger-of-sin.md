# Harbinger of Sin

A boss of Karazhan Crypts, number 1 of 10 in Forever's order.

The Harbinger of Sin is a bone wraith that haunts the Slough of Despair, a flooded chamber under Karazhan. The only way in leads through the Upside-down Sinners, humans hung by chains with their heads in the water. The Harbinger speaks of an eternal dark, of a servitude that does not end at the grave, and of a feast of souls. Even in death, it says, it will bathe in the sin of those who trespass.
