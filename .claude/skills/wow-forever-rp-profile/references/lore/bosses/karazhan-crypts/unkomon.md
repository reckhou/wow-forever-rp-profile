# Unk'omon

A boss of Karazhan Crypts, number 5 of 10 in Forever's order.

Unk'omon is a doomguard known as the Winged Sorrow. He is called into the jail beneath Karazhan by Alfwhit Grigdert, a neophyte who hides in a secret room and speaks of a winged master he will summon to claim his dominion.
