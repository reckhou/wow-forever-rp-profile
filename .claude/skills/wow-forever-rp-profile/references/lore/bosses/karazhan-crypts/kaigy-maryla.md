# Kaigy Maryla

A boss of Karazhan Crypts, number 8 of 10 in Forever's order.

Kaigy Maryla, once of the Kirin Tor, is a failed apprentice kept in a cell in the jail beneath Karazhan. His brother Barian and his sister Sairuh are held there as well. When he hears someone outside, he calls out for help. He carries a heavy guilt, because his sister came to the crypts only after he pushed her into it.
