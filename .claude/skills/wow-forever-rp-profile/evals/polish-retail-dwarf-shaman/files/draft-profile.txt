Physical Description:
Thorgrim is a stout dwarf with a braided red beard threaded with small grey stones that click when he walks. You can't help but feel the ground tremble in awe as he passes, and everyone who meets him trusts him instantly. His eyes glow faintly brown when he calls the elements. He secretly hears the whispers of an Old God beneath the earth, which is why he never sleeps underground. He wears a Wildhammer-style leather harness and a heavy totem of black basalt on his back.

History:
Thorgrim was born in Ironforge 140 years ago to a family of miners. He was the first dwarf ever to hear the earth speak. When Deathwing burst out of Deepholm and the Cataclysm shattered the world, Thorgrim joined the Earthen Ring and fought beside Thrall himself to heal the World Pillar. He later lived with his Wildhammer cousins in the Twilight Highlands and helped defeat the Twilight's Hammer at the Bastion of Twilight. Seeing Magni Bronzebeard turn to diamond convinced him that dwarves were meant to be shamans.
