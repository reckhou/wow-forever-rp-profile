EDWIN TALLOW - Human Paladin

Currently: Guarding the road near Goldshire.

OOC: EU evenings, walk-ups welcome.

Physical Description:
A broad man of forty with a greying beard and a dented breastplate. He walks with a slight limp and smells of lamp oil.

Personality:
Dutiful and patient, slow to anger. He talks to his horse more than to people.

History:
Edwin Tallow grew up on a farm in Elwynn Forest and joined the Stormwind Army at seventeen. He served with the Argent Crusade in Northrend before coming home to keep the peace on the roads around Goldshire.
