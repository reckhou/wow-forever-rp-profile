# Hazzas

A boss of Sunken Temple, number 4 of 8 in Forever's order.

Hazzas is one of four young green drakes who followed Eranikus into the Temple of Atal'Hakkar, eager to prove themselves by serving a great and powerful wyrm. Ysera sent them to watch over the ruins, and for a while they kept the Atal'ai in check. One by one they were captured and tainted by the Emerald Nightmare.

Now Hazzas stands with Morphaz in the hall that leads to the shade of Eranikus, and he lashes out at anyone who intrudes upon his waking dream.
