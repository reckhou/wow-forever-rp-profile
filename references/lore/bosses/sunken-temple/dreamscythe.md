# Dreamscythe

A boss of Sunken Temple, number 7 of 8 in Forever's order.

Dreamscythe is a young green drake who came to the Temple of Atal'Hakkar with Eranikus and three others. Ysera sent them to watch over the ruined temple, and the Emerald Nightmare tainted them there.

He still speaks like a warden. He tells intruders to turn back and not to wake the dreamer, and he says that they must be destroyed for their own good.
