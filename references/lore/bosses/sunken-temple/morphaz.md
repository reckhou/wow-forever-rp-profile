# Morphaz

A boss of Sunken Temple, number 5 of 8 in Forever's order.

Morphaz is a young green drake, one of four who came to the Temple of Atal'Hakkar under Eranikus. Ysera sent them to watch over the ruins, and for a time they held the Atal'ai in check. Then the Emerald Nightmare took them.

Years ago Morphaz overwhelmed the archmage Xylem in a bold ambush and swallowed a whole apprentice. Xylem was little troubled by the loss of the young man. The apprentice had been carrying an arcane shard of unsurpassed size and quality, though, and Xylem wants it retrieved.
