# Grimroot

A boss of Demon Fall Canyon, number 1 of 7 in Forever's order.

Grimroot is an Ancient of Lore, one of the giant treant guardians of the forest, and corruption has taken hold of him. In Demon Fall Canyon he is known as the Mourning Guardian. The canyon in southern Ashenvale lies under a fel taint, in the land where the pit lord Mannoroth was slain, and demons still make their homes there.
