# Diathorus the Seeker

A boss of Demon Fall Canyon, number 5 of 7 in Forever's order.

Diathorus the Seeker is a dreadlord of the nathrezim and, with Gorgannon, rules the demonic conclave in Ashenvale. He once kept his lair deep in the barrow den of Demon Fall Ridge, and he now holds the deep ground of Demon Fall Canyon, where Mannoroth fell.

He looks ahead to a greater invasion. When mortals tried a ritual to destroy an echoing orb, he broke it by force and called the pit lord Azgaloth into the world. He told Azgaloth that he would make a fine general in the war to come, and took him to his lair.
