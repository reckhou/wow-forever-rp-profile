# Azgaloth

A boss of Demon Fall Canyon, number 7 of 7 in Forever's order.

Azgaloth is an annihilan, a pit lord of the Burning Legion, who bears the title Lord of the Pit. He was called into the world at Darkwhisper Gorge in Winterspring when the dreadlord Diathorus the Seeker broke a ritual meant to destroy an echoing orb. Diathorus welcomed him to Azeroth and led him away to his lair in Demon Fall Canyon, intending to make him a general in the invasion to come.

Azgaloth demands blood and regards the defenders of this world as an appetizer for the feast ahead.
