# Pyranis

A boss of Demon Fall Canyon, number 4 of 7 in Forever's order.

Pyranis is a night elf druid, corrupted and bound to the demons who hold Demon Fall Canyon. He calls up the twisted growth of the place, summoning dread lashers, fungus and seeds, and wraps the ground in an endless fog. He warns intruders that they cannot stop what has been set in motion, and when he falls he mourns that he and his allies were so close.
