# High Priest Venoxis

A boss of Zul'Gurub, number 2 of 10 in Forever's order.

High Priest Venoxis is the Zandalari priest of Hethiss, the snake god. He was one of five high priests sent into Zul'Gurub to quell Hakkar the Soulflayer, and like the others he fell under the blood god's sway.

Now Venoxis channels the power of his own god into Hakkar, and the serpent god is weakened as the blood god swells. He is attended by Razzashi cobras, and when his rage runs high he takes the shape of a great snake.
