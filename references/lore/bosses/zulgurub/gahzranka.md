# Gahz'ranka

A boss of Zul'Gurub, number 7 of 10 in Forever's order.

Gahz'ranka is a giant hydra that lives in the waters of the ancient Gurubashi capital of Zul'Gurub. The famous fisherman Nat Pagle hunted the creature for a long time and never landed it. He left only a ruined camp at the shore, with shattered lures and tackle, and a measuring tape he lost there.
