# High Priestess Jeklik

A boss of Zul'Gurub, number 1 of 10 in Forever's order.

High Priestess Jeklik serves the bat god Hir'eek, one of the primal gods of the trolls. When Hakkar the Soulflayer was called into Zul'Gurub, the trolls of the land banded together and sent a contingent of high priests into the ancient city to quell him. Each was a powerful champion of a god, but despite their efforts they fell under Hakkar's sway.

Jeklik is one of the five. She takes the shape of a great bat, and she is forced to channel the power of her god into the blood god, who grows stronger while Hir'eek is drained.
