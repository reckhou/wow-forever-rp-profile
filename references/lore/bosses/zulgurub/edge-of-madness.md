# Edge of Madness

A boss of Zul'Gurub, number 5 of 10 in Forever's order.

In a corner of the old city, tablets line the walls around a brazier, and from that place one of four troll spirits can be roused. All four are ghosts, and each carries a different kind of rage.

Gri'lek was a dire troll berserker. Long ago he turned his ear from the spirits and lost his right arm, and he wandered the jungle with his eyes burning, roaring his curses at a sky that no longer listened. Renataki, of the Thousand Blades, lives on pain and fear and hopes to return one day with bloodshed. Hazza'rah, a troll turned ghost, conjures illusions to torment the living. Wushoolay, a jungle troll, wields the lightning.
