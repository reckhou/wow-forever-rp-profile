# High Priest Thekal

A boss of Zul'Gurub, number 6 of 10 in Forever's order.

High Priest Thekal serves Shirvallah, the tiger god. When the blood god Hakkar was called into Zul'Gurub, the trolls of the land sent five high priests to quell him, and Thekal was among them. Each was a champion of a primal god, but Thekal fell under Hakkar's sway like the others.

Now he channels Shirvallah's power into the Soulflayer. Two zealots, Lor'Khan and Zath, stand beside him, and tigers attend him.
