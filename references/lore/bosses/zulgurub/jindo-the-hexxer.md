# Jin'do the Hexxer

A boss of Zul'Gurub, number 9 of 10 in Forever's order.

Jin'do the Hexxer is a jungle troll witch doctor and the leader of the Hakkari. His tribe was exiled or murdered for the evil deeds it did in Hakkar's name, and the survivors resolved to wreak havoc on the world and bring the blood god into it.

Jin'do is one of Hakkar's most powerful lieutenants. In Zul'Gurub he has enslaved several high priests of the Gurubashi to help him summon their dread god, and he and the remaining Hakkari now live inside the walls of the ancient capital.
