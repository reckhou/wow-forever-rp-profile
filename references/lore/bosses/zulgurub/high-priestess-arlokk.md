# High Priestess Arlokk

A boss of Zul'Gurub, number 8 of 10 in Forever's order.

High Priestess Arlokk serves Bethekk, the panther goddess. She was one of five high priests sent into Zul'Gurub to quell Hakkar the Soulflayer, and she, too, fell under his sway.

Arlokk now feeds the power of Bethekk to the blood god. Her temple is full of panthers, and she herself takes the form of a great cat, a hunter that moves unseen until she is ready to strike.
