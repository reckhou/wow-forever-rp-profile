# Electrocutioner 6000

A boss of Gnomeregan, number 3 of 5 in Forever's order.

Mekgineer Thermaplugg has no patience for testing his inventions. He rushes them into the field and moves on to the next project. The Electrocutioner 6000 was his first attempt to use electricity as a weapon, a super-charged mechano-tank.

Only one soldier survived piloting it, Lieutenant Tom "Sizzlepants" Crankle, one of the insane leper gnomes who still follow Thermaplugg. He still drives the machine, and among the leper gnomes he is something of a hero.
