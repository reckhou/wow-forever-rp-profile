# Mekgineer Thermaplugg

A boss of Gnomeregan, number 5 of 5 in Forever's order.

Sicco Thermaplugg was once an ambitious gnomish engineer and the chief advisor of High Tinker Gelbin Mekkatorque. In secret he envied his lord and wanted the title of high tinker for himself. When the troggs overran the lower levels of Gnomeregan, Thermaplugg convinced Mekkatorque to flood the city with radioactive gas. He meant the gas to anger the troggs and kill the gnomes.

The radiation proved far deadlier than he had reckoned. Nearly eighty percent of the gnomes died within days, and many survivors, Thermaplugg among them, were twisted into insane leper gnomes, while Mekkatorque led the rest to safety in Ironforge.

Thermaplugg now calls himself king of the gnomes and rules the radioactive halls of Gnomeregan from the Tinkers' Court. His subjects are numerous, vicious and mad enough to defend him against any intrusion from the upper world, and even the monsters of the city fear him. He has the cunning to destroy everything around him and no thought for his own life. Mekkatorque, who knows of the betrayal now, wants him dead.
