# Viscous Fallout

A boss of Gnomeregan, number 2 of 5 in Forever's order.

The waterways of Gnomeregan were once elegantly complex. Now they are an irradiated mess filled with toxic slime. A lone water elemental that had lived in the gnomes' aquifer became trapped in the contamination, and the pollution corrupted its pure form.

Viscous Fallout wanders the halls of Gnomeregan in anger and agony, a liquid embodiment of the vile poison of Thermaplugg's betrayal.
