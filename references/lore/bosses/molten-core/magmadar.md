# Magmadar

A boss of Molten Core, number 2 of 10 in Forever's order.

Magmadar is a behemoth of igneous rock and roiling magma, and the origin of the core hounds that roam the halls of the Molten Core. He is the alpha male of all of them, and Ragnaros counts him among his favorite pets.

The story goes that while the Firelord was imprisoned in the Elemental Plane, he fed the remains of captured enemies to the waiting twin maws of Magmadar. The hound grew strong on this and loyal to his master, and his savage ferocity has never been matched. Lucifron and packs of hungry core hounds stand guard around him. Magmadar bears a grudge against the molten giant Golemagg, who bullied two of his offspring into serving as pets, though he has not yet acted on it.
