# Majordomo Executus

A boss of Molten Core, number 9 of 10 in Forever's order.

Majordomo Executus stands at the top of the elemental hierarchy, just below Ragnaros. He is a flamewaker who earned that rank by proving close to invincible in the battles that raged in the Elemental Plane. Rumor says he took the place of Baron Geddon, and the two have been rivals ever since, though no one has confirmed it.

Ragnaros does not tolerate failure, so the lesser flamewakers, and Geddon above all, wait for Executus to slip. He is attended by flamewaker healers and elite guards. When his guard has been broken he says that he goes to summon the lord whose house this is, and that anyone who seeks an audience with the Firelord will forfeit their life.
