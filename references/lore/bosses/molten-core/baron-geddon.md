# Baron Geddon

A boss of Molten Core, number 6 of 10 in Forever's order.

Baron Geddon is a fire elemental who followed Ragnaros out of the Firelands when Thaurissan summoned the Firelord. Long ago he fought the titans and helped betray Thunderaan, Prince of Air. Ragnaros devoured the prince's essence but could not consume all of it, so the rest was trapped in a talisman of elemental binding. Ragnaros split the talisman into two equal halves and gave one to Geddon and one to Garr.

Geddon suspects that Garr wants the whole talisman and watches his old comrade closely. Rumor says Majordomo Executus took his place at the Firelord's side, and the two have been rivals since. He also taught Lord Incendius to wield fire, then grew wary of him and sent him away to guard the Dark Iron dwarves' Black Anvil.
