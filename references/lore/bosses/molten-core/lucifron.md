# Lucifron

A boss of Molten Core, number 1 of 10 in Forever's order.

Lucifron is a flamewaker, one of the elementals whose bodies are flesh and blood, unlike the fire elementals, who are pure flame. He and his kind were brought out of the Elemental Plane by Ragnaros after the Dark Iron sorcerer Thaurissan summoned the Firelord into Azeroth.

Among the flamewakers, overseers and their guards compete without pause for rank and for Ragnaros's favor. Lucifron has clashed with Gehennas many times, going back to their long confinement in the Elemental Plane, and that rivalry has followed them into the Molten Core. He now watches over Magmadar, the Firelord's favored pet, with two Flamewaker Protectors at his side. He keeps quiet and waits for any weakness in the other flamewakers.
