# Golemagg the Incinerator

A boss of Molten Core, number 8 of 10 in Forever's order.

The molten giants are the most feared soldiers in the elemental forces of Ragnaros, and none of them can match Golemagg the Incinerator for brutality and efficiency. His power is so complete that he has cowed two core ragers, offspring of the colossal Magmadar, and keeps them as pets.

Magmadar has not forgiven the insult and hates Golemagg for it. The great hound has not yet acted on that rage. Golemagg and his two ragers guard the Rune of Theri deep in the Molten Core.
