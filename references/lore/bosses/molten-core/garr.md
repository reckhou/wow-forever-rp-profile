# Garr

A boss of Molten Core, number 4 of 10 in Forever's order.

Garr is a molten rock elemental and one of the two chief lieutenants of Ragnaros. He came into Azeroth with the Firelord during the War of the Three Hammers. Earlier, he helped betray Thunderaan, the Prince of Air, also called the Windseeker. Ragnaros wanted to consume the prince, and Garr and Baron Geddon ambushed him. Thunderaan was destroyed, but Ragnaros could not swallow all of his essence. He sealed the remainder in a talisman of elemental binding, split it in two and gave one half each to his lieutenants.

After centuries of partnership, Geddon suspects that Garr wants both halves, and relations between them are strained. Garr draws strength from the blazing runes cut into the floor of the Molten Core, and eight Firesworn serve as his guard.
