# Broodlord Lashlayer

A boss of Blackwing Lair, number 3 of 8 in Forever's order.

Broodlord Lashlayer is a large black drakonid in the service of Nefarian. He guards the Halls of Strife in Blackwing Lair and bars the way into his master's inner sanctum. The suppression rooms are his charge, where kidnapped whelps of the other dragonflights are held, corrupted and kept in check.

He does not leave this post easily. When intruders reach him, he tells them that none of their kind should be here, and that they have doomed only themselves.
