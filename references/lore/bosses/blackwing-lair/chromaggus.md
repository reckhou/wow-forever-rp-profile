# Chromaggus

A boss of Blackwing Lair, number 7 of 8 in Forever's order.

Chromaggus is a two-headed beast made by Nefarian and the dutiful protector of Blackwing Lair. He is the only drakeadon known, a four-legged dragonkin with a body like a core hound's, and he carries the powers of all five dragonflights, drawn from Nefarian's experiments with the blood of every flight.

His scales shimmer in shifting colors, and each color is tied to a power he can use. The afflictions that radiate from him have a terrible effect on their victims, who are transformed into drakonid. His name comes from the same root as chromatic, the flight his master hopes to build.
