# Bloodmage Thalnos

A boss of Scarlet Monastery, number 2 of 7 in Forever's order.

Bloodmage Thalnos earned a name in the Scarlet Crusade for the brutal methods he used to purify new recruits. The sadistic mage later caught the plague of undeath and rose again as a fearsome skeleton.

Thalnos still prowls the monastery graveyard. He commands apparitions and zombified creatures to attack anyone who trespasses on his unholy domain, and he says that the angry dead have no rest and that they hunger for vengeance.
