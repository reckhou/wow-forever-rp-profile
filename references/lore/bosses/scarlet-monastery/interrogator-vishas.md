# Interrogator Vishas

A boss of Scarlet Monastery, number 1 of 7 in Forever's order.

James Vishas is the Scarlet Crusade's lead interrogator at the Scarlet Monastery. He enjoys torturing captured undead and takes great pride in his work. He is known for his unrelenting sadism, his cruel expertise and his high pitched voice, and his victims hear him demand that they tell him everything.

For weeks he has tortured Vorrel Sengutz, a Forsaken who lies on a torture table in the graveyard wing. Vishas took Sengutz's wedding ring and gave it to his own wife, Nancy, who lives with Grandpa Vishas in a cottage on the eastern shore of Lordamere Lake in the Alterac Mountains.
