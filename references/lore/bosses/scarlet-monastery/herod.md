# Herod

A boss of Scarlet Monastery, number 5 of 7 in Forever's order.

Herod is the Scarlet Champion, one of the four leaders of the Scarlet Crusade in the Scarlet Monastery, and he holds its armory. He is a brutal fighter who says he has been waiting for a real challenge, and when a battle turns hard he calls on the Light to give him strength.

He has enemies outside the monastery. Raleigh the Devout in Southshore left the Crusade after he saw innocent men and women tortured as supposed plague carriers, and he wants its leaders dead. So does Varimathras in the Undercity, who wishes to end a threat so close to his capital.
