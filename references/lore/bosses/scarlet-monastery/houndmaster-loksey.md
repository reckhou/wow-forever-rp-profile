# Houndmaster Loksey

A boss of Scarlet Monastery, number 3 of 7 in Forever's order.

Houndmaster Loksey is one of the four leaders of the Scarlet Monastery, the captain who trains the hounds the Scarlet Crusade uses to track its enemies. He commands the loyalty of fearsome dogs that can tear a victim apart limb by limb within minutes, and he greets intruders with the cry to release the hounds.

His brother is Huntsman Leopold, the emissary the Crusade sent to Light's Hope Chapel as part of its reluctant alliance with the Argent Dawn. Leopold holds that Loksey cannot even take care of his own hounds, let alone represent the Crusade, which is why Leopold went in his place.
