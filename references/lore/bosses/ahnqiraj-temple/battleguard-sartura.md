# Battleguard Sartura

A boss of Ahn'Qiraj Temple, number 3 of 9 in Forever's order.

Battleguard Sartura is the leader of the qiraji battleguards, one of the great castes that serve the Twin Emperors. Three royal guards fight at her side.

She tells trespassers that they defile sacred ground and that the laws of the Ancients will not be challenged. They will be annihilated, she says, for she serves to the last.
