# C'Thun

A boss of Ahn'Qiraj Temple, number 9 of 9 in Forever's order.

C'Thun is an Old God, a being of unimaginable evil and power. In the time before time, a battle between a Titan and this being raged on the soil of Silithus. A Titan fell, and the Old God fell as well, or so it was thought. For ages C'Thun lay dormant beneath the world and waited for the moment to strike back at those who had harmed it.

It reached out through the silithid and made avatars of them in its own image, the qiraji. They named their creator and spent many thousands of years building the fortress city of Ahn'Qiraj and an army to avenge him.

The night elves and the dragons walled the qiraji in a thousand years ago, but C'Thun has awakened in its lair and is regaining its power. It foresaw the dragons and cast a spell that places them under its control if they come too close. Whispers ride the winds of the Silithus desert.
