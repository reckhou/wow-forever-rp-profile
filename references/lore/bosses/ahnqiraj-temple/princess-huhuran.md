# Princess Huhuran

A boss of Ahn'Qiraj Temple, number 6 of 9 in Forever's order.

Princess Huhuran is a large silithid wasp of the Vekniss hive, the largest hive known, behind the gates of Ahn'Qiraj. Silithid wasps are the flying attack troops of the silithid. They are very aggressive and are often found in swarms.
