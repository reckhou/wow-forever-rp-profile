# Ancient Stone Keeper

A boss of Uldaman, number 5 of 8 in Forever's order.

The Ancient Stone Keeper is one of many golems that staffed and operated the great titan vault of Uldaman. It belonged to a small group that stood between the watchers left in control of the facility and their many servants, and it keeps its post in the Temple Hall.

The corruption of the Obsidian Sentinel has left the Stone Keeper the last of its kind. It goes on protecting the secrets of the titans as best it can until its inevitable shutdown.
