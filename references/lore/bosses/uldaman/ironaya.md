# Ironaya

A boss of Uldaman, number 3 of 8 in Forever's order.

The titans did not build Uldaman directly. They used various servants to raise its megalithic halls, and the Pantheon entrusted the watcher Ironaya with overseeing the construction and upkeep of the complex. As time passed, the once awe-inspiring vault fell to ruin.

Ironaya has spent millennia in a form of stasis in the map chamber, studying the original master plan drawn up by Khaz'goroth and trying to find a way to restore the complex to its first function. She wakes when the Staff of Prehistoria is set in the map, and she cries out that none may steal the secrets of the Makers.
