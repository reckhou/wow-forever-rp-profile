# Grobbulus

A boss of Naxxramas, number 11 of 15 in Forever's order.

Grobbulus is a flesh giant, the first successful one of its kind. The same plague slime that flows through Naxxramas runs inside his hulking body.

He was made to help build an army that could spread the undead plague quickly. His makers meant to inject the living ooze into the bodies of their foes. The recipient of such an injection would usually flee to his allies and bring them down with him.
