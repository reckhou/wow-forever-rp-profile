# Sapphiron

A boss of Naxxramas, number 14 of 15 in Forever's order.

Sapphiron was one of the most ancient dragons of the blue flight and had immense magical power. He lived with his brood in the mountains of Northrend near the entrance to Azjol-Nerub, guarding a hoard of artifacts gathered over the centuries. When Arthas and the nerubian Anub'arak set out through Azjol-Nerub toward Icecrown, they sought that hoard. Sapphiron met them, and found it refreshing that Arthas was so frank about killing him and taking his treasure. The Scourge killed the wyrm and his dragons, and Arthas raised Sapphiron as a frost wyrm.

Years later the frost wyrm stands guard in Naxxramas, over the chamber of its master Kel'Thuzad. His power has been sharpened by his rebirth, and he uses frost to defend the lich at all costs.
