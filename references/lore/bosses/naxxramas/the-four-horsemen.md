# The Four Horsemen

A boss of Naxxramas, number 9 of 15 in Forever's order.

The Four Horsemen are death knights, made by Kel'Thuzad to serve as his royal guard. The most feared soldiers of the Scourge, they are his greatest creation, and they are linked to the lich so closely that only his destruction truly ends them. Thane Korth'azz, a dwarf, wields the power of flame and can call fiery meteors from the sky. Lady Blaumeux is a master of shadow, able to drain the essence of life from those she meets. Sir Zeliek served the Order of the Silver Hand while he lived, and his faith was so strong that the Light still answers him in undeath. His body must obey Kel'Thuzad, yet he has kept his sanity and his morals, and the work he is forced to do is a torment.

Highlord Alexandros Mograine leads them. He was a hero of the Alliance of Lordaeron and the first wielder of the Ashbringer. After his fall Kel'Thuzad took his body, broke his will and set him at the head of the Horsemen. Together they oversee the Military Quarter of Naxxramas.
