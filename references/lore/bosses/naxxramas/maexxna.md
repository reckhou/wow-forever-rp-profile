# Maexxna

A boss of Naxxramas, number 3 of 15 in Forever's order.

Maexxna is a massive spider, taken from the depths of Northrend's mountains many ages ago. She was captured within the dread citadel Naxxramas, and there she gives birth to her brood.

She feasts on those foolish enough to enter the necropolis and feeds her spawn with their corpses. Spider after spider comes of it, and Kel'Thuzad turns them to his own foul ends. Even the poisons of Grand Widow Faerlina are drawn from Maexxna's spiderlings.
