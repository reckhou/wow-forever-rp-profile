# Instructor Razuvious

A boss of Naxxramas, number 7 of 15 in Forever's order.

Instructor Razuvious is the most feared of the death knight trainers of the Scourge. Kel'Thuzad appointed him to train all death knights, and it is said that his technique is so potent that only a disciple of his could hope to withstand him.

In Naxxramas he holds his post in the Military Quarter, and a company of Death Knight Understudies stands with him.
