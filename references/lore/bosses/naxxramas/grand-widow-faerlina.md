# Grand Widow Faerlina

A boss of Naxxramas, number 2 of 15 in Forever's order.

Grand Widow Faerlina was a botanist in life, and later one of the high-ranking members of the Cult of the Damned under Kel'Thuzad. Death did not end her service. In Naxxramas she oversees the Spider Wing, breeding and tending the seemingly endless swarms of arachnids that crawl out of the citadel's depths.

She is a master of poisons and has a keen understanding of what makes mortals suffer. The venoms she brews for the Lich King come from the spiderlings that Maexxna produces.
