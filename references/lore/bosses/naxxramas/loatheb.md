# Loatheb

A boss of Naxxramas, number 6 of 15 in Forever's order.

Loatheb shows what the plague did to the living things of the land. He is a twisted fen creeper, the hideous result of fusing the living plague with the bog beasts of Azeroth.

The plant kingdom can regrow what is cut from it, and in Loatheb that gift is mocked. He summons deadly spores that spread a sickening miasma, and he is said to control the power of healing itself, turning the healing arts against those who wield them.
