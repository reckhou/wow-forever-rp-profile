# Patchwerk

A boss of Naxxramas, number 10 of 15 in Forever's order.

Patchwerk is a titanic abomination, stitched together from the dead, and Kel'Thuzad calls him his Avatar of War. He is one of the most powerful abominations the necromancer has made. Abominations are slow and dull-witted by reputation, which is why the tales of his strength and speed were so hard to believe. Patchwerk is neither slow nor mindless, and he uses his great power to pulverize anyone who stands against him with a flurry of heavy blows.

He has little to say for himself. In Naxxramas he greets intruders with the simple cry that Patchwerk wants to play.
