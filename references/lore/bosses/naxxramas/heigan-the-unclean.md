# Heigan the Unclean

A boss of Naxxramas, number 5 of 15 in Forever's order.

Heigan the Unclean was the mastermind behind the magic of the plague cauldrons, which spread the undead plague through Lordaeron so quickly. The sickness corrupted the plants and animals of the countryside along with the human population. The whole wilderness around Lordaeron is now called the Plaguelands, thanks in no small part to Heigan's handiwork.

Within Naxxramas he is said to have rigged the walls and floors of the citadel with a vast array of traps, which he can spring at will.
