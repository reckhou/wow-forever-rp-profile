# Anub'Rekhan

A boss of Naxxramas, number 1 of 15 in Forever's order.

Anub'Rekhan was one of the finest spider lords of Azjol'Nerub, the underground kingdom of the nerubians. When the Lich King broke that kingdom, the crypt lords were swayed to his side and given the long servitude of undeath. Anub'Rekhan was brought under Scourge control when he and his commander, Anub'arak, were defeated, and it is said that he was the most loyal of Anub'arak's commanders.

Now the crypt lord guards the doors of the Spider Wing in Naxxramas. He greets visitors with a mock-polite welcome to his parlor. He speaks of the hearts he hears beating and of the blood he has gone too long without.
