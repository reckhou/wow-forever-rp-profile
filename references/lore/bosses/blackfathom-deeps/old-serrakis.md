# Old Serra'kis

A boss of Blackfathom Deeps, number 5 of 8 in Forever's order.

Old Serra'kis is a huge threshadon that lives in the Forgotten Pool, a submerged dead-end cavern reached by swimming past the Moonshrine Ruins of Blackfathom Deeps. After the Twilight's Hammer cult took up residence, many of the creatures swimming beneath the ruins were killed for food. The largest thresher proved uncatchable, and those who persisted in hunting it died in the attempt.
