# Lorgus Jett

A boss of Blackfathom Deeps, number 4 of 8 in Forever's order.

Lorgus Jett is a human of the Twilight's Hammer who calls down lightning on his enemies. He keeps to the long tunnels of the Moonshrine Ruins in Blackfathom Deeps, between the chamber of Ghamoo-Ra and the waters of Baron Aquanis.

A crumpled note in his hand shows what he is after. The naga priestesses are to bring him crystals, and the water elemental spirits inside them will aid the cult's aquamancers in their summonings. By controlling the power in the crystals, the cult hopes to appease Aku'Mai and one day gather enough strength to bring the hydra back, so that the world returns to an era of rule by the Old Gods. The Earthen Ring, which found the note, names him as a follower who must be stopped.
