# Ghamoo-ra

A boss of Blackfathom Deeps, number 1 of 8 in Forever's order.

Ghamoo-Ra is a giant turtle, once one of the last peaceful creatures living in the ruined temple of Elune. When the Twilight's Hammer cult arrived, its followers roused the turtle and imprisoned it. They delighted in tormenting the creature for years, flaying its mind and body until it gave way to madness.

The cultists gave the beast its name and trained it to attack outsiders and defend the Old Gods' shrine. It now roams a small island in the Pool of Ask'ar, surrounded by other turtles.
