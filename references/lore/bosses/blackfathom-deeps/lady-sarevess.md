# Lady Sarevess

A boss of Blackfathom Deeps, number 2 of 8 in Forever's order.

Lady Sarevess is a naga and a twisted spellcaster who dabbles deeply in the realm of magic. The Twilight's Hammer granted her a private cove in the Pool of Ask'ar of Blackfathom Deeps and keeps sending her fresh sacrifices.

The cultists want something in return. They hope her rituals and research will result in a magical protection that lets them worship the terrifying hydra at the heart of the deeps without fear of becoming its next meal. Sarevess has no welcome for visitors and orders them slain.
