# Baron Aquanis

A boss of Blackfathom Deeps, number 8 of 8 in Forever's order.

Baron Aquanis is a water elemental that haunts the Moonshrine Ruins of Blackfathom Deeps. Somewhere underwater in the ruins lies a fathom stone, which holds a fathom core, a device that records the history of the elemental activity around it. Whoever lifts the core from the stone wakes the Baron.

The Earthen Ring has long thought of Aquanis as a corrupted elemental power. The Twilight's Hammer has moved into the ruins, and the shamans suspect the cult. A strange water globe, holding a putrid-looking form of water, seems to have fed the creature with energy.
