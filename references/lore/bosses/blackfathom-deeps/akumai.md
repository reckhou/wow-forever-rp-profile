# Aku'mai

A boss of Blackfathom Deeps, number 7 of 8 in Forever's order.

Aku'Mai is a three-headed hydra and an ancient evil that carries a small measure of the Old Gods' power. The creature is feared for its mindless savagery and its insatiable hunger for living flesh.

Blackfathom Deeps was once a great temple of Elune. The corruption of an Old God seeped up from beneath the earth and tainted the sacred Moon Well, and Aku'Mai, a servant of that Old God, rose from the waters. The Twilight's Hammer cult worships the hydra as a divine sign that the Old Gods will soon return, and it sacrifices innocents to the beast.
