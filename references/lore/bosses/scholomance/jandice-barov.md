# Jandice Barov

A boss of Scholomance, number 2 of 14 in Forever's order.

Jandice Barov was the daughter of Lord Alexei and Lady Illucia Barov, and an archmage of great skill. When her family gave its home to Kel'Thuzad and the Cult of the Damned, she was turned into a ghost within the school of necromancy that grew beneath Caer Darrow.

It is said that she carried the blood of innocents from Doctor Krastinov to Kirtonos the Herald. She waits in the lower halls of Scholomance, and when she is threatened she scatters copies of herself through the room, so that anyone who comes for her cannot tell which image is real.
