# Vectus

A boss of Scholomance, number 5 of 14 in Forever's order.

Vectus is a skeletal mage and one of the main teachers of necromancy in Scholomance. He created the plagued dragons that hatch beneath the school and leads a team of Scourge scholars who study them. The eggs come from Tinkee Steamboil, a goblin scholar in the Burning Steppes, who delivers dragon eggs from Blackrock Spire.

Vectus tells his students that the young dragons thrive on terrified flesh, and that their dragonflight will soon be ready to serve the Lich King's plans.
