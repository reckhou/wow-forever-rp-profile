# Kormok

A boss of Scholomance, number 6 of 14 in Forever's order.

Kormok was an ogre with two heads. One was cheerful and a little foolish, and the other was sly and calculating, with an almost dead look in its eyes. He began as a mage and a decent enough member of the Veiled Blade, the mercenary company that stole the spellbook of Lord Valthalak in Blackrock Spire. Kormok grabbed the amulet Valthalak wore, and the quarrel over it broke the company apart.

Then he was exposed to necromancy, and he came to delight in summoning the souls of the banished dead of Purgation Isle. He left the company for Scholomance, but Valthalak's spectral assassins found him there and killed him in front of his master, Ras Frostwhisper.
