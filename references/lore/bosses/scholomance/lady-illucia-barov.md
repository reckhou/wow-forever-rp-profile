# Lady Illucia Barov

A boss of Scholomance, number 13 of 14 in Forever's order.

Lady Illucia Barov was the wife of Lord Alexei and the mother of Jandice, Alexi and Weldon. When the Barovs gave their ground to Kel'Thuzad for his Cult of the Damned, he granted them immortality through undeath in return. Illucia now walks the school that was once her home as a ghost, and she dwells in the Shadow Vault.
