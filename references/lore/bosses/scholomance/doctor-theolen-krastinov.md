# Doctor Theolen Krastinov

A boss of Scholomance, number 9 of 14 in Forever's order.

Doctor Theolen Krastinov, known as the Butcher, is a human torturer and the servant of Kirtonos the Herald. He is the main provider of bodies and body parts for the necromancy experiments of Scholomance.

His first victims came from the household of Caer Darrow, among them the Barov servants Eva and Lucien Sarkhoff. For months he kept them trapped, tortured and maimed, used them in countless experiments to devise a plague, and held them to life by magic so that they could not die. Their ghosts remember him only as the Butcher, and they have sworn to take revenge on him, even beyond the grave.
