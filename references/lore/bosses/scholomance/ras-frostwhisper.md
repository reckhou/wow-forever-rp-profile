# Ras Frostwhisper

A boss of Scholomance, number 7 of 14 in Forever's order.

Ras Frostwhisper was a human mage who spent years among the ruins of Stromgarde before he gave himself to the Scourge. In Stratholme he pledged his undying soul to the Lich King. He stepped inside a pentagram, drew a dagger across his own throat and smiled while he did it. The Lich King stood over the body and with a single gesture raised him as a lich. Leonid Barthalomew, now of the Argent Dawn, watched it happen, and the ground there is bitterly cold to this day.

Ras now rules over Scholomance and guards it in the name of the Scourge, while the mortal necromancer Darkmaster Gandling serves as the school's headmaster.
