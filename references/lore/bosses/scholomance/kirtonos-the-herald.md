# Kirtonos the Herald

A boss of Scholomance, number 1 of 14 in Forever's order.

Kirtonos the Herald is an undead creature that can change shape between a gargoyle and a vampire. He is the master of Doctor Theolen Krastinov, and he fed on the blood of the innocent victims who passed through the doctor's hands. To keep his master content, Krastinov had to bring him more and more blood.

Kirtonos rarely visits Scholomance now. Blood of the innocent is hard to find around Caer Darrow, because most of its people are dead, and only ghosts remain. They spend their time plotting his destruction. A drop of blood laid on the brazier in the Chamber of Summoning still draws him in through the window.
