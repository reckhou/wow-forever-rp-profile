# Lord Alexei Barov

A boss of Scholomance, number 12 of 14 in Forever's order.

Lord Alexei Barov once ruled Caer Darrow, Brill, Tarren Mill and Southshore. He was the husband of Lady Illucia and the father of Alexi, Weldon and Jandice. Not content with their enormous holdings, the Barovs made a deal with the necromancer Kel'Thuzad, leader of the Cult of the Damned, and gave him their home. It became Scholomance, a school of necromancy beneath Caer Darrow, and Alexei was raised as an undead death knight in the service of the Scourge.

He still keeps the Barov Family Vault, the burial place of his line, and he guards the Deed to Caer Darrow, which his sons Alexi and Weldon both want so that they can inherit the family's wealth.
