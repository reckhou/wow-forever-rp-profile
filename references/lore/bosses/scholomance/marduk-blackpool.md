# Marduk Blackpool

A boss of Scholomance, number 4 of 14 in Forever's order.

Marduk Blackpool, also called Marduk the Black, was a brutal death knight and warlord of the Scourge. During the Third War he cut through the human defenders of Lordaeron with a foul, rune-covered sword. At the Battle of Darrowshire he corrupted the spirit of Joseph Redpath, and with the help of Redpath's twisted ghost the Scourge overran the town and killed everyone in it.

Marduk survived the war and kept serving the Scourge. Near Corin's Crossing a dwarf captain broke his sword on an enchanted shield, and Marduk fled the field. The blade sank into the earth and ate out the gorge now known as the Infectis Scar. Afterward he came to Scholomance and became a tutor there, working beside Vectus and his plagued dragons.
