# Instructor Malicia

A boss of Scholomance, number 8 of 14 in Forever's order.

Instructor Malicia is a high elf who teaches in Scholomance, the school of necromancy beneath Caer Darrow. She keeps to the Coven, a chamber deep inside the school where she and a few other warlocks and witches work.
