# Alzzin the Wildshaper

A boss of Dire Maul, number 5 of 19 in Forever's order.

Legends say Alzzin was a gifted arcane illusionist and one of the first students of the satyr Lord Xavius. After the Burning Legion fell at the Battle of Mount Hyjal, he went south to Feralas to continue his masters' mission, the corruption of the forests of Kalimdor. A lone satyr armed with tricks found little success.

He found what he needed in the ruins of Eldre'Thalas, his former home. There he corrupted the Highborne Shrine of Eldretharr and the Fruit of Fertility, a vine that had once protected the city, and used its power to shape illusions of his own. Feared as the Wildshaper, he commands the enslaved ancients and draws the vitality of the forest into his lair. He seeks the total corruption of Feralas.
