# Magister Kalendris

A boss of Dire Maul, number 8 of 19 in Forever's order.

Magister Kalendris was one of the most loyal lieutenants of Prince Tortheldrin. When the prince demanded the slaughter of most of his subjects, Kalendris was tasked with carrying out the order. He murdered many unsuspecting Shen'dralar without hesitation.

In a destructive battle one of the doomed elves mortally wounded him. His death did not end his service. His ghost still roams the Court of the Highborne in Dire Maul West, a defender of Tortheldrin's rule.
