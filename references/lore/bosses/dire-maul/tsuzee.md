# Tsu'zee

A boss of Dire Maul, number 19 of 19 in Forever's order.

Tsu'zee was a night elf rogue of the Shen'dralar. She is now a ghost in Dire Maul West, where she haunts the Court of the Highborne among the wraiths, banshees and ghosts of the Highborne who once lived in the city.
