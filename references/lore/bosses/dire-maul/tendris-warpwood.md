# Tendris Warpwood

A boss of Dire Maul, number 6 of 19 in Forever's order.

Tendris Warpwood is an ancient who stood as a staunch defender of Eldre'Thalas. His heart was bound to the fate of the city. When the Great Sundering shattered the land his resolve never wavered.

Over the next ten thousand years corruption seeped into the ruins, and it infected the ancient's spirit. He now holds the Capital Gardens of Dire Maul. The Ironbark Protectors answer his call, and he keeps an Ancient Equine Spirit imprisoned in spectral bonds. He greets intruders with the cry that they do not belong there, and he calls the ancients to rise against them.
