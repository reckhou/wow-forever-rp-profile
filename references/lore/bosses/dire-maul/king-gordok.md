# King Gordok

A boss of Dire Maul, number 17 of 19 in Forever's order.

To rule the Gordok ogres one must proclaim oneself king and destroy all who might disagree. King Gordok, then merely known as Ulrok, solidified his power by convincing a dozen of his people's strongest warriors to challenge the king at the same time. The wild melee left most of the contestants dead or wounded, and Ulrok's blade easily cut down the survivors and the battle-weary king. Gordok ogres still speak in hushed tones about the Day of Da Blood.

Ulrok took the title and now rules the Gordunni ogres in the Gordok Commons of Dire Maul, with Cho'Rush the Observer as his chief advisor. By tradition, whoever slays the king becomes the next King Gordok.
