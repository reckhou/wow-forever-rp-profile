# Guard Slip'kik

A boss of Dire Maul, number 14 of 19 in Forever's order.

Some might think Slip'kik is merely cautious among the Gordok ogres, but he is also lazy. He does not want to challenge King Gordok directly. Instead he tinkers with traps and old, broken magical devices, hoping one day to deliver the final blow without an ounce of risk or a drop of sweat.
