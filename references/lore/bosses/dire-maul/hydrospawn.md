# Hydrospawn

A boss of Dire Maul, number 2 of 19 in Forever's order.

Hydrospawn is a water elemental in the Shrine of Eldretharr, in the Warpwood Quarter of Dire Maul. When the satyr Zevrim Thornhoof set out to control the elementals of the ruins, he focused on Hydrospawn. While Zevrim performed his profane sacrifices, the elemental grew agitated and restless and struck out at the satyr's demonic minions.

As the years passed, Zevrim used the energy of his victims to whip Hydrospawn into unbridled fury. Little by little he bound the elemental's will to his own commands.
