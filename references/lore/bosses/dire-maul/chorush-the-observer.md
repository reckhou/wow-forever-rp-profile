# Cho'Rush the Observer

A boss of Dire Maul, number 16 of 19 in Forever's order.

Cho'Rush the Observer is a two-headed ogre mage who serves as King Gordok's chief advisor. He does not usually take sides, but when Ulrok was rising to power it was clear that nobody could challenge him. The new king appointed Cho'Rush, believing that twin heads would make him twice as resourceful as the average ogre.

Cho'Rush heals and defends his king with priestly, shamanic and arcane magic. When the king falls, he is ready to turn to whoever struck the blow, and he apologizes for having fought.
