# Pusillin

A boss of Dire Maul, number 4 of 19 in Forever's order.

Pusillin is a mischievous imp who lives in the eastern part of Dire Maul, near the main entrance. In the hurry of his escape from the fallen Prince Tortheldrin, the Highborne elder Azj'Tordin was robbed by him. Pusillin took his book of incantations and the key to the once great halls of Eldre'Thalas.

The imp treats the theft as a game. He skips away from anyone who comes near and sings that the key is theirs if they can catch him. Whoever keeps chasing finds him less amused, and he calls his fiery friends to his side.
