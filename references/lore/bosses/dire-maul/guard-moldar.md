# Guard Mol'dar

A boss of Dire Maul, number 11 of 19 in Forever's order.

Mol'dar is a zealously loyal guard among the Gordok ogres. He is convinced that King Gordok gave him a pair of enchanted gauntlets, and that they made him his people's arm wrestling champion for six years running. In truth the king threw the powerful armor away, and Mol'dar simply found it first.
