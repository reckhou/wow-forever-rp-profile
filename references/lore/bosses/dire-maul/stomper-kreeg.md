# Stomper Kreeg

A boss of Dire Maul, number 12 of 19 in Forever's order.

Stomper Kreeg is a Gordok ogre who is often passed out drunk on duty. His mug of ale is precious to him. More than once a skulking assassin has met his doom after disturbing it by accident, because nothing rouses Kreeg into a berserker fury faster than the sound of his drink splattering on the stone floor.
