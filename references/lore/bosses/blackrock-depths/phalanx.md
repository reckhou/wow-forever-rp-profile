# Phalanx

A boss of Blackrock Depths, number 13 of 20 in Forever's order.

Phalanx is the bouncer of the Grim Guzzler, a tavern in the depths of Blackrock Mountain. Until recently, drunken brawls raged there almost every day. The barkeep, a leper gnome named Plugger Spazzring, cut them down by installing Phalanx, a mighty golem programmed to keep order and to remove raucous patrons with extreme force.

On a peaceful night Phalanx is friendly company. When trouble starts he stands against the crowd and declares that none shall pass.
