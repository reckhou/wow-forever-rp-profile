# Houndmaster Grebmar

A boss of Blackrock Depths, number 3 of 20 in Forever's order.

Grebmar Fleabeard was orphaned long ago, and the hounds he began breeding as companions and guard dogs are the only family he has known. The houndmaster keeps his pack beneath the arena and patrols Blackrock Depths with his loyal beasts.

When he is off duty he takes the dogs to the Grim Guzzler and treats them to the tavern's finest Dark Iron ale. He is close friends with Warder Stilgiss, to whom he once gave one of his vicious dogs, a red hound named Verek.
