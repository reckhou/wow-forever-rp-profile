# Pyromancer Loregrain

A boss of Blackrock Depths, number 5 of 20 in Forever's order.

Pyromancer Loregrain is a tauren shaman who joined the Twilight's Hammer cult. His reasons remain a mystery. He made his way to the depths of Blackrock Mountain and took a sudden interest in Franclorn Forgewright, the famed Dark Iron architect, and he now kneels before the monument raised in the architect's honor.

What he wants from Franclorn is unclear. Loregrain stays loyal to his cult and is an enemy of all who oppose it.
