# The Seven

A boss of Blackrock Depths, number 17 of 20 in Forever's order.

The Seven were the patriarchs of the Dark Iron nation, dwarves of great knowledge and power. When Sorcerer-Thane Thaurissan lost the War of the Three Hammers, they joined him in delving into forbidden lore and accidentally summoned Ragnaros the Firelord at the place now called the Molten Core. The Dark Irons had no idea how much power they had called up. Thaurissan, the Seven and many of their people died in the blaze, which destroyed a great part of the Redridge Mountains and raised the black peak of Blackrock Spire.

Their clan was enslaved to the Firelord, yet the Seven seem to think his coming was a blessing. Their restless spirits, Anger'rel, Doom'rel, Dope'rel, Gloom'rel, Hate'rel, Seeth'rel and Vile'rel, linger in Blackrock Depths, cursed to serve their elemental master and the current ruler of the Dark Irons.
