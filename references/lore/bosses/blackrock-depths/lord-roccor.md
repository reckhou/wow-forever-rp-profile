# Lord Roccor

A boss of Blackrock Depths, number 2 of 20 in Forever's order.

Lord Roccor is a fire elemental who cannot stand his own kind. The elementals of the Molten Core spend their time scheming and backstabbing one another, and Roccor wanted no place among them. He got his wish by angering Ragnaros, the Firelord, on purpose.

The gamble worked. Ragnaros expelled him from the core and set him to guard Blackrock Depths, where Roccor patrols the Halls of the Law with no company at all.
