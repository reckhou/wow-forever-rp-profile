# Magmus

A boss of Blackrock Depths, number 18 of 20 in Forever's order.

Magmus is a molten giant of living fire. Ambassador Flamelash charged him with guarding the throne room of Emperor Dagran Thaurissan, but even that did not settle the emperor's fears. Ever wary of assassins, Thaurissan also gave Magmus control over a row of fire-breathing statues outside his royal chambers.

From the Iron Hall, Magmus warns intruders that the emperor does not wish to be disturbed and tells them to turn back now or face their doom.
