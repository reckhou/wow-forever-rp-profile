# Golem Lord Argelmach

A boss of Blackrock Depths, number 11 of 20 in Forever's order.

Golem Lord Argelmach has spent his life chasing immortality. He forged enchanted constructs in his own image, and rumor says he has turned to a black magic that would let those creations carry his spirit if his flesh-and-blood body were ever destroyed.

The rumor has some support. Before the Third War a gnome scholar named Lotwil Veriatus killed Argelmach, yet the golem lord went on commanding the Manufactory from his private room, guarded by two golems. The Horde wants his head. When it was brought to Lotwil, it proved to be one more Dark Iron creation, and another would rise in time. Marshal Windsor believes Argelmach also holds part of his lost information.
