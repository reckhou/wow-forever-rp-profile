# Oggleflint

A boss of Ragefire Chasm, number 1 of 4 in Forever's order.

Oggleflint is the chief of the Ragefire troggs, who once ruled unchallenged in the depths of Ragefire Chasm beneath Orgrimmar. The Searing Blade cultists moved in, and the troggs proved too weak to drive them out.

Oggleflint and his troggs fell back toward the entrance, where they wait patiently for their unwelcome guests to leave. When Magatha Grimtotem sent diplomats to make peace, the troggs turned on them, and Oggleflint stands guard over the body of one of them, the tauren Maur Grimtotem.
