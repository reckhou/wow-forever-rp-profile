# Taragaman the Hungerer

A boss of Ragefire Chasm, number 2 of 4 in Forever's order.

Taragaman is a felguard, a massive demon who stands in the middle of a lake of lava deep in Ragefire Chasm. Many believe he leads the Searing Blade, and it is said he uses his demonic influence to corrupt the citizens of Orgrimmar.

In truth the cult's leaders are Bazzalan and Jergosh. Taragaman is expendable bait, set out to lure young Horde champions to their deaths.
