# Targorr the Dread

A boss of Stormwind Stockade, number 1 of 5 in Forever's order.

Targorr the Dread was the supreme executioner of Gath'Ilzogg, a warlord of the Blackrock orcs in the Redridge Mountains. He tortured men who had fought bravely to defend the kingdom, and he did it on a whim, with a cruelty that shocked even his own kind.

Captured and sent to the Stormwind Stockade, he was sentenced to die. A noble put a hold on the execution, and the people of Lakeshire suspect foul play. When the prisoners rose against their guards, Targorr fell in with the Defias rioters.
