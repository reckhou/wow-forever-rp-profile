# Bazil Thredd

A boss of Stormwind Stockade, number 5 of 5 in Forever's order.

Bazil Thredd was Edwin VanCleef's second-in-command and most trusted lieutenant in the Defias Brotherhood. He was captured and put in the Stormwind Stockade. For many months he had no visitors, and his jailers took it that the Brotherhood had written him off.

Then, a few months before the Stockade riots, a man calling himself Maelik began to visit him. Maelik carried messages between Thredd, VanCleef and Lord Gregor Lescovar of the House of Nobles, and Thredd and Lescovar planned an uprising in the Stockade. Now Thredd leads the Defias among the prisoners, and Warden Thelwater wants his head.
