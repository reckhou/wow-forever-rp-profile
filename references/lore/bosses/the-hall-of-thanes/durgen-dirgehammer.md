# Durgen Dirgehammer

A boss of The Hall of Thanes, number 4 of 4 in Forever's order.

Durgen Dirgehammer commands a Dark Iron force that has broken into the lower vaults of Old Ironforge, rising from the depths in mole machines. Earthseer Farsen, a Wildhammer from Aerie Peak who was visiting Dun Morogh, found his name in Dark Iron runes on a map of tunnels that all converge on a point beneath Ironforge itself.

Two lesser stone golems stand with him. What he is doing in the depths is unknown. King Magni Bronzebeard's guards mean to seal the breaches and reinforce the lower levels so that no further incursion is possible.
