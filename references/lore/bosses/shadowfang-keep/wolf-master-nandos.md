# Wolf Master Nandos

A boss of Shadowfang Keep, number 7 of 8 in Forever's order.

Wolf Master Nandos is the worgen master of the lesser worgs in Arugal's service. He keeps a pack of his own, and its members fall on intruders before they can reach him.

He is the last of Arugal's henchmen to block the way to the archmage.
