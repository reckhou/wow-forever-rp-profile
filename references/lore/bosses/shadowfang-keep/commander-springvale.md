# Commander Springvale

A boss of Shadowfang Keep, number 4 of 8 in Forever's order.

Commander Springvale was a paladin of the Silver Hand and the commander of the soldiers who served Baron Silverlaine. When the worgen overran Shadowfang Keep he fell with the men under his command.

His ghost haunts the council room, where he is still surrounded by his soldiers. Death has not ended his loyalty, and he keeps the room against everyone who comes in, for the sake of the lord he served.
