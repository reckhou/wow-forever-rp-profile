# Rethilgore

A boss of Shadowfang Keep, number 1 of 8 in Forever's order.

Rethilgore, known as the Cell Keeper, is one of the most vicious worgen in Archmage Arugal's pack. Arugal trusted him to guard the prisoners taken inside Shadowfang Keep.

Two of them are Deathstalker Adamant, a Forsaken scout whom High Executor Hadrec sent to learn what he could about the keep, and Sorcerer Ashcrombe, who came on behalf of the Kirin Tor. Adamant was caught by one of Arugal's magical wards and thrown into a cell, with Rethilgore standing over it.
