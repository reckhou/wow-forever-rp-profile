# Witch Doctor Zum'rah

A boss of Zul'Farrak, number 5 of 8 in Forever's order.

Witch Doctor Zum'rah is a cruel Sandfury witch doctor. The Sandfury learned to raise the dead with voodoo so that every troll could keep working after death, and Zum'rah takes the practice further. Many of the tribe's fallen are offered no protection by a loa after death, and their tortured spirits obey him. He uses his dark magic to force the city's dead into unending service.

He guards his sanctum among the graves of Zul'Farrak, and he greets trespassers with a demand to know how they dare enter, and with a curse that the sands consume them.
