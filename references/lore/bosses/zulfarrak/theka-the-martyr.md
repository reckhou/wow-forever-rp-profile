# Theka the Martyr

A boss of Zul'Farrak, number 4 of 8 in Forever's order.

Theka the Martyr is a historical figure in Sandfury society, a troll who gave his life for his people. Long ago he was slain in a brutal war between the qiraji and the trolls, and Zul'Farrak still stands because of his sacrifice. The dozens of insectoid warriors who killed him were cursed to live as mindless scarabs.

Even in death he keeps a vigil over the city he loved. He is an undead sand troll now, and the scarabs scuttle at his side.
