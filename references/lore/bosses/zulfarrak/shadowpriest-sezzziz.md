# Shadowpriest Sezz'ziz

A boss of Zul'Farrak, number 7 of 8 in Forever's order.

Shadowpriest Sezz'ziz is a priest of the Sandfury who leads a host of some hundred trolls together with Nekrum Gutchewer. The two have gathered sacrifices for the mythic beast Gahz'rilla, who slumbers at the heart of the city. Many troll followers eagerly await the creature's awakening, and they will do anything to see the ritual through.
