# Hydromancer Velratha

A boss of Zul'Farrak, number 1 of 8 in Forever's order.

Hydromancer Velratha is a sand troll of the Sandfury and a worldly member of her tribe. Unlike most Sandfury, she has traveled far, collecting knowledge and artifacts that help her command the sacred waters of Zul'Farrak. She seems to hold a prominent place in the priestly order.

Her journeys have won her many enemies abroad. One is Tabetha, whose beautiful and powerful Tiara of the Deep was stolen by agents Velratha sent to her home while she was away. Velratha sees the great power she commands as justification for her past misdeeds.
