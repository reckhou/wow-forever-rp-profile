# Gahz'rilla

A boss of Zul'Farrak, number 2 of 8 in Forever's order.

Long ago, when trolls held the land that became Tanaris, they built Zul'Farrak around a sacred pool. From that pool they called up the huge hydra Gahz'rilla, a beast so fierce that her scales crackle with energy. The Sandfury have worshiped her as a deity for thousands of years, and her image is a common motif in the city's architecture. Ornaments modeled after her are called Gahz'ridian.

Legend says Gahz'rilla was an adored pet of the Old Gods, or even a demigod in her own right. The trolls guard the secret of her summoning and fear her icy wrath. They rouse her only when they have enough sacrifices to feed her ravenous appetite.
