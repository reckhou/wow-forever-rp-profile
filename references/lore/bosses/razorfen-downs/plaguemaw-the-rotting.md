# Plaguemaw the Rotting

A boss of Razorfen Downs, number 2 of 6 in Forever's order.

Plaguemaw the Rotting is an undead quilboar, a powerful warrior of the Death's Head tribe. Near the entrance of Razorfen Downs stands an idol that serves the quilboar as a grisly oven, where they consume the people they carry off from the Barrens.

Belnistrasz, a prisoner of the Downs who is really a red dragon in human form, means to shut the idol down with a ritual. Plaguemaw comes to stop him.
