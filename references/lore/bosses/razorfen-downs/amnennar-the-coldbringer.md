# Amnennar the Coldbringer

A boss of Razorfen Downs, number 6 of 6 in Forever's order.

Amnennar the Coldbringer is a lich who took part in the Scourge's assault on Mount Hyjal during the Third War. Afterward the Lich King told his servant to stay in Kalimdor and widen the Scourge's reach, and Amnennar began to plan a base from which to strike at Orgrimmar and Theramore.

For that he turned to the quilboar, a displaced and desperate people. He promised them power over death so that they could drive off invaders, and he took control of the Death's Head tribe, turning the thorn maze of Razorfen Downs into a bastion of undead might. Through a direct telepathic link to Ner'zhul, the Lich King, he guides the quilboar with the power of his massive consciousness. He tells intruders that they will never leave alive.
