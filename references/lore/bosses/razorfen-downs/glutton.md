# Glutton

A boss of Razorfen Downs, number 5 of 6 in Forever's order.

Glutton is a massive abomination who patrols the lower tier of the Spiral of Thorns. He has spent much of his time in Razorfen Downs gorging on the spoiled corpses of boars and their quilboar masters. The rancid meat in his gut gives off a cloud of noxious fumes and crippling disease. He snarls that he smells the stench of the living and means to feast on them all.
