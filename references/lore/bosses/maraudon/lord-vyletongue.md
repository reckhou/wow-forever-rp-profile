# Lord Vyletongue

A boss of Maraudon, number 4 of 8 in Forever's order.

Lord Vyletongue leads the Putridus clan of satyrs. He journeyed into the depths of Maraudon after hearing of its corruption. Princess Theradras was wary of the conniving satyr at first, but he proved his worth by creating a host of wretched minions for her, among them Noxxion and Razorlash.

Wherever he goes he spreads his evil through the twisting caves, poisoning the minds of all inside and stunting the growth of the plants.
