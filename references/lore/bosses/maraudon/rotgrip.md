# Rotgrip

A boss of Maraudon, number 7 of 8 in Forever's order.

Rumor has it that Rotgrip has dwelled in the subterranean waters of Maraudon for millennia, a giant crocolisk lurking below Zaetar's grave.

The famed dwarven hunter Hemet Nesingwary once declared that the beast could not be found, and a number of amateur trackers took up the challenge of seeking out the legendary creature. None of them ever returned.
