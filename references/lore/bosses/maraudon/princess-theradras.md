# Princess Theradras

A boss of Maraudon, number 8 of 8 in Forever's order.

Theradras is an enormous earth elemental, a princess. Zaetar, a keeper of the grove and first son of Cenarius, fell in love with her despite the warnings of his brother Remulos. Their children were the first centaur, born misshapen and hateful, and they killed their father.

Grief-stricken, and fearing the wrath of Cenarius, Theradras trapped her lover's spirit in the caverns the centaur named Maraudon and came to treat as holy ground. She bent the place into a den of corruption and suffering, and she guards Zaetar's remains in the crystal caverns of her inner sanctum.

She loves the centaur still. When she feared the Horde might drive them to extinction, she filled them with her power and sent them to war for new lands beyond Desolace.
