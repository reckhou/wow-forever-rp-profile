# Landslide

A boss of Maraudon, number 6 of 8 in Forever's order.

For years the ancient mountain giant Landslide cultivated brilliant crystal gardens in the caverns of Maraudon, before Theradras made her home there.

When the princess took up residence, she enthralled the behemoth. The once-stoic giant now lives only to serve his tyrannical new master.
