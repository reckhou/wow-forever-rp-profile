# Razorlash

A boss of Maraudon, number 2 of 8 in Forever's order.

Razorlash was made to accompany Noxxion through Maraudon, one of the wretched creations of the satyr Lord Vyletongue. In time it became one of the princess Theradras's most cherished servants.

It wanders the caves alongside Noxxion, using thorny tendrils to shred any untainted plant life it can find.
