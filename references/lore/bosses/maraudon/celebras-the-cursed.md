# Celebras the Cursed

A boss of Maraudon, number 5 of 8 in Forever's order.

Celebras is the son of Remulos and a keeper of the grove, like his father. When his uncle Zaetar was killed by his own centaur children and his spirit was held captive in Maraudon, Celebras and his dryad sisters swore to free it.

The keeper was not prepared for the horrors in Maraudon's shadowy corridors. The corruption that had spread through the caverns overcame him and filled his heart with uncontrollable rage. Now he wanders blindly through the caves, cursed, while Vyletongue and Noxxion hold the two halves of his broken scepter.
