# Noxxion

A boss of Maraudon, number 1 of 8 in Forever's order.

Noxxion is a living embodiment of corruption, a putrid elemental. The satyr Lord Vyletongue made him, at the order of Princess Theradras, to be a powerful servant and a loyal pet to ease her loneliness.

Noxxion spreads his toxic essence into the parts of Maraudon that have not yet withered under Theradras's malign influence.
