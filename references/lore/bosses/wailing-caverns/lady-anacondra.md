# Lady Anacondra

A boss of Wailing Caverns, number 1 of 8 in Forever's order.

Lady Anacondra was once Scarletleaf, a young acolyte of the druid Naralex. She was the first to volunteer for his self-appointed mission to the Barrens, where he hoped to use the Wailing Caverns and the Emerald Dream to make the land green again.

When Naralex's dream turned into a nightmare, it shattered her mind. She cast aside her name and now sees a sinister, reptilian future for Azeroth. She is one of the four Fanglords of the Druids of the Fang.
