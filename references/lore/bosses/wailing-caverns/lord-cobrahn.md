# Lord Cobrahn

A boss of Wailing Caverns, number 2 of 8 in Forever's order.

Lord Cobrahn was once Jarlaxla, a druid whose skill at taking on animal forms made him renowned among the druids of the Cenarion Circle. The corruption of the Wailing Caverns twisted that gift.

He has since perfected a deadly serpentine form and taken the name Cobrahn to match his new, vicious strength. He stands much larger than the other druids, with a claw on his right hand and deviate pythons gathered around him, and he is one of the four Fanglords of the Druids of the Fang.
