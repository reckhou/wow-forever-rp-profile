# Skum

A boss of Wailing Caverns, number 5 of 8 in Forever's order.

Skum is a thunder lizard who first came to the Wailing Caverns to escape the harsh environment of the Barrens. Like many of the caverns' other inhabitants, he soon gave in to the corruption. Dark energies now pulse through his veins and have turned him into a mighty, ruthless beast beyond redemption. He is kept in a small paddock in the Winding Chasm as the pet of Lord Serpentis.
