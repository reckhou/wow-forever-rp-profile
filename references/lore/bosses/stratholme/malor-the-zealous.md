# Malor the Zealous

A boss of Stratholme, number 3 of 19 in Forever's order.

Malor is a paladin of the Scarlet Crusade, the zealous order that rose from the Knights of the Silver Hand after the Third War. The Crusade holds the western half of Stratholme from the Scarlet Bastion, once the Bastion of the Silver Hand, and Malor is a crazed member of the order.

He keeps to a chamber off the Hall of Lights, deep inside the Bastion, where he guards the Medallion of Faith. A paladin sheltering in Alonsus Chapel believes the medallion would give him the strength to resist the corruption in his heart and leave.
