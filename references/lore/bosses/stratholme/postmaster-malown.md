# Postmaster Malown

A boss of Stratholme, number 17 of 19 in Forever's order.

Malown was the Postmaster of Stratholme, and he did not hold the post by neglecting the mail. When the Scourge attacked he could not escape their masses. In death he is still dedicated to the stability of the postal system, and he haunts the city to make sure nobody tampers with the mail. He has sworn that not even hail, sleet or snow will stop him, and should the system be disrupted to the point of threatening instability, he may take matters into his own hands.
