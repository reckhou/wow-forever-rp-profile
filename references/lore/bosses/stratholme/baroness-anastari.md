# Baroness Anastari

A boss of Stratholme, number 8 of 19 in Forever's order.

In life Baroness Anastari took what she wanted, no matter the cost. The Scourge murdered her brutally, tore her soul from her body and bound her to the Lich King as a banshee.

The grave changed little about her. She gave up her love of material trinkets for possessions of a far more sinister kind. She guards one of the ziggurats in Stratholme that stand before the Slaughterhouse, and she can seize any living creature she touches, driving out its soul and wearing the body as her own for a time.
