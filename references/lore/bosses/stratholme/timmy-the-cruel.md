# Timmy the Cruel

A boss of Stratholme, number 2 of 19 in Forever's order.

Timmison was a champion infamous for his savagery on the field of battle. His sadism may have grown out of the bullying he suffered as a child.

The Scourge raised him as a ghoul with a shattered mind. The old torment is still with him, and he answers to the name he once loathed, Timmy. He lurks just outside the courtyard of the Scarlet Bastion.
