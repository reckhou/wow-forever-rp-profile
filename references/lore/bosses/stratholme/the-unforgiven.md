# The Unforgiven

A boss of Stratholme, number 7 of 19 in Forever's order.

Lylia was a blade of righteousness among her people. When Arthas commanded his forces to purge Stratholme, she watched countless innocents die by her hand, and the sight tore her sanity apart. She fell on her sword to escape the madness around her.

Dying gave her no peace. She now haunts Market Row as a spectral being, forever denied absolution for what she did.
