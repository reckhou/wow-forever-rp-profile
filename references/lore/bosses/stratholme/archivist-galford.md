# Archivist Galford

A boss of Stratholme, number 5 of 19 in Forever's order.

Galford is the archivist of the Scarlet Crusade in Stratholme and takes great pride in his charge, the Crusade's most valued documents. He keeps them in the Scarlet Archive in the last corridor of the Scarlet Bastion.

The archive holds years of intelligence. Duke Nicholas Zverenhoff of the Argent Dawn sees it as a prize worth burning, certain that its loss would hurt the zealots for weeks to come.
