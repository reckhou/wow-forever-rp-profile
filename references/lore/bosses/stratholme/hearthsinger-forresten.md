# Hearthsinger Forresten

A boss of Stratholme, number 1 of 19 in Forever's order.

Forresten was a traveling singer and piccolo player who was in Stratholme when Prince Arthas ordered the city purged. The Culling claimed countless people whose only crime was being inside its walls, and the musician was one of them.

He still wanders the streets of Stratholme as a ghost, unable to accept what happened to him.
