# Crimson Hammersmith

A boss of Stratholme, number 15 of 19 in Forever's order.

The Crimson Hammersmith is a human smith in the service of the Scarlet Crusade, working inside the Scarlet Bastion in Stratholme. He guards the secrets of the hammersmith's craft with his life and will let no one take them.
