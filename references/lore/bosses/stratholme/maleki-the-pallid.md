# Maleki the Pallid

A boss of Stratholme, number 10 of 19 in Forever's order.

Maleki was among the first to join the Cult of the Damned. A mortal human mage, he showed a tremendous aptitude for the chill of the grave and for frost and shadow magic.

He is driven by an insatiable desire for power and spends his days sharpening his skill in the dark arts, preparing for the moment he will be remade as a lich. He guards one of the ziggurats before the Slaughterhouse in Stratholme.
