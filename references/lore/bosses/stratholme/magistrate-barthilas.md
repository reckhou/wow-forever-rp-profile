# Magistrate Barthilas

A boss of Stratholme, number 11 of 19 in Forever's order.

Barthilas began as an ambitious young paladin at Mardenholde Keep, second to Lord Tirion Fordring. The Second War had taken his parents and left him with a deep hatred of orcs. When Tirion defended the orc Eitrigg, Barthilas moved to capture the prisoner and oversaw his master's prosecution. During Thrall's raid on Stratholme an orc drove a spear into his chest, yet he survived, took over Mardenholde after Tirion's exile, and in time became Magistrate of Stratholme.

Arthas's purge killed him along with the people he governed. The Lich King later raised him from the grave as a wight, a hulking undead brute, and ordered him to guard his ruined home.
