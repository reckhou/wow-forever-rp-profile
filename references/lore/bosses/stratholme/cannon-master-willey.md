# Cannon Master Willey

A boss of Stratholme, number 4 of 19 in Forever's order.

Cannon Master Willey is a ranking member of the Scarlet Crusade, which holds the western half of Stratholme under Grand Crusader Dathrohan. His post is the Hoard, a hall in the Scarlet Bastion where the Crusade keeps its stash of weaponry, and he presides over it. The hall dead-ends in a row of cannons.
