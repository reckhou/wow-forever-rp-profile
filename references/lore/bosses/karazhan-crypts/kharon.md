# Kharon

A boss of Karazhan Crypts, number 4 of 10 in Forever's order.

Kharon is a lich who dwells in the Pit of Criminals, among a heap of skeletons. A book called the Dialogues of the Dead lies there, and whoever reads it calls him forth. He greets the reader with a remark on how much horror they pour out.
