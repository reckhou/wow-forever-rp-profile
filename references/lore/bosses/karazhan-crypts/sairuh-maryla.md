# Sairuh Maryla

A boss of Karazhan Crypts, number 9 of 10 in Forever's order.

Sairuh Maryla is the sister of Kaigy and Barian, three failed apprentices of the Kirin Tor who are held in cells in the jail beneath Karazhan. By her brother Kaigy's account, she was in the crypts only because he pushed her into it.
