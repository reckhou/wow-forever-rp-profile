# Quartermaster Zigris

A boss of Blackrock Spire, number 6 of 14 in Forever's order.

Zigris was an orc hunter in the years after the First War, and he became infamous for tracking down Stormwind refugees and slaughtering them like game. He now serves the Bloodaxe Legion as quartermaster of the spire's storehouse, where the Blackrock orcs keep their weapons, armor and ammunition. There he barks orders at his underlings while he inspects the crates and barrels.

The close, crowded halls of Blackrock Spire sit badly with a hunter. Zigris hates them, and he is certain that a day will come when he will hunt in the open air again.
