# Pyroguard Emberseer

A boss of Blackrock Spire, number 10 of 14 in Forever's order.

Pyroguard Emberseer is a fire elemental who served Ragnaros the Firelord. Long ago, in a great battle in the Burning Steppes, he incinerated dozens of Blackrock orcs. In the end he was beaten and taken prisoner, and his captors carried him into Blackrock Mountain and bound him in the Hall of Binding in the upper spire.

Blackhand Incarcerators keep the prison closed with channeled magic, and the orcs who tend it stay locked in a trance. Emberseer's great energy is drawn off and used to speed up the hatching of Nefarian's black dragon eggs in the chambers beyond.
