# War Master Voone

A boss of Blackrock Spire, number 3 of 14 in Forever's order.

Voone leads the Smolderthorn, a tribe of forest trolls that joined the Dark Horde after the Second War. In that war he led small strike teams on daring raids into the heart of high elf territory. He has never believed in Blackhand's creed. He joined the false Horde because he wanted to be its chief strategist, and a splinter faction fighting against the odds suits him, since he enjoys overcoming a hopeless fight.

Voone rules Tazz'Alor beside the shadow hunter Vosh'gajin, and he is a torturer as well as a commander. Mor Grayhoof, a tauren druid of the Veiled Blade mercenary company, fell into the lower spire and was tortured by Voone's trolls until he was nearly dead. His ghost still haunts Voone's chamber.
