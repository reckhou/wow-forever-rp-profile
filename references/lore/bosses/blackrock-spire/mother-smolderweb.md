# Mother Smolderweb

A boss of Blackrock Spire, number 4 of 14 in Forever's order.

Mother Smolderweb is an enormous crystal spider and the matriarch of the spire spiders. Her lair stayed buried until, centuries ago, Dark Iron dwarves dug into it and let her loose in the halls of Blackrock Spire. She settled in the Skitterweb Tunnels, which climb from the lower reaches of Hordemar City toward the ogres' ledge, and she keeps laying eggs, so her brood grows larger every year.

The Blackrock orcs talk about the lethal venom they call Mother's Milk, which is named after her. Even Blackhand's warriors fear to travel through her domain.
