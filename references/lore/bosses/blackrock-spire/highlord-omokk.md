# Highlord Omokk

A boss of Blackrock Spire, number 1 of 14 in Forever's order.

Highlord Omokk rules the Spirestone ogres from Mok'Doom, a dead-end hall in the lower spire reached by a short stone bridge. The Spirestone clan follows Rend Blackhand and the Dark Horde and makes up a large part of the force that holds Blackrock Spire. Omokk keeps his title with an iron fist. Underlings have challenged him to single combat again and again, hoping to take it, and every one of them has fallen without landing a blow.

The ogres whisper that dark magic protects him, and they are right. Omokk is the face of the clan and Urok Doomhowl is its true master. Urok's boon strikes dead any ogre who lifts a hand against the highlord, so only someone who is not an ogre could ever end his rule.
