# Kurinnaxx

A boss of Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj, number 1 of 6 in Forever's order.

Kurinnaxx is a large sand reaver, a silithid that looks something like a solifuge. He belongs to Hive'Zara, one of the major silithid hives, and serves the qiraji and their master, C'Thun, in the ruins of Ahn'Qiraj.

He is among the first guardians a visitor meets in the ruins. The poisoners of Ravenholdt name him as one of two particularly venomous creatures, and they want his venom sac, along with the one belonging to Venoxis of Zul'Gurub, to brew a new and more effective poison.
