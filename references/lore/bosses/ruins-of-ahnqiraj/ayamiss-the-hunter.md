# Ayamiss the Hunter

A boss of Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj, number 5 of 6 in Forever's order.

Ayamiss the Hunter is a silithid wasp and a powerful flying hunter in the army of the qiraji. Ayamiss and the soldiers of the swarm hover high above the ground in the Comb, a part of the ruins of Ahn'Qiraj between the Hatchery and the Watchers' Terrace. Anyone who confronts the swarm may be taken and sacrificed on an altar, and Ayamiss takes great pleasure in this.
