# Ossirian the Unscarred

A boss of Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj, number 6 of 6 in Forever's order.

In the War of the Shifting Sands, as the bronze dragonflight broke the anubisath constructs, one anubisath found a way to fight back. He hurled his obsidian sword through the wing of the great dragon Grakkarond, who fell and crushed qiraji as he came down. The dragon mauled the anubisath nearly to death before the rest overwhelmed it.

Ossirian was dragged back to Ahn'Qiraj and should have died, but the malevolent power that rules the qiraji spared him. He awoke stronger, his head bound in the visage of a hawk. Emperor Vek'nilash told him he had succeeded where all others, General Rajaxx included, had failed. Ancient crystals now bind his essence and make him nearly invulnerable, though he dies if he strays far from them. His kind revere him as the Unscarred.
