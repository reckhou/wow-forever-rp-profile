# General Rajaxx

A boss of Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj, number 2 of 6 in Forever's order.

General Rajaxx is the chief military commander of the qiraji. In the War of the Shifting Sands he was a towering monstrosity who swung clawed limbs and shouted orders to the qiraji and silithid soldiers. He fought the night elf armies.

Rajaxx now commands the army that gathers once more in Ahn'Qiraj. He holds the General's Terrace with his captains and majors, and he vows that a thousand years of injustice will end and that the dragons themselves will tremble.
