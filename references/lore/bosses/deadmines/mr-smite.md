# Mr. Smite

A boss of Deadmines, number 6 of 8 in Forever's order.

Mr. Smite is a tauren who serves as first mate under Captain Greenskin. It falls to him to keep the crew on its toes, and he is a skilled weaponmaster, trained in all manner of slicing, bashing and the occasional piercing shot.

He likes to play with his victims. He lets them believe they might win, then draws a more dangerous weapon, from a thief's blade to a pair of reavers and finally a mighty hammer.
