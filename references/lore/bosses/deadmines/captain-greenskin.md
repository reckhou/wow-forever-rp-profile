# Captain Greenskin

A boss of Deadmines, number 5 of 8 in Forever's order.

Captain Greenskin is a goblin from Booty Bay, where he once led a fleet of pirate vessels. VanCleef gave him command of the juggernaut taking shape in the Deadmines, and he runs the ship with Mr. Smite as his first mate and Cookie in the galley. VanCleef means to use the juggernaut to lay waste to Stormwind from the sea.
