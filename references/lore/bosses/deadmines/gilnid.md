# Gilnid

A boss of Deadmines, number 4 of 8 in Forever's order.

Gilnid is a goblin of great skill as an engineer and the head smelter of the Defias operation. VanCleef's goblins built a foundry in the mines, and there Gilnid turns the steel and copper hauled in from the captured mines into the anchors and cannonballs the juggernaut needs.

He makes sure the ship is loaded with enough cannon and ammunition, and he tolerates no breaks until the work is done.
