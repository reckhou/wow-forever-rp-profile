# Sneed

A boss of Deadmines, number 2 of 8 in Forever's order.

Sneed is a goblin the Defias Brotherhood hired as lumbermaster for his ruthless efficiency. He runs the Mast Room, where timber felled in Westfall is cut and shaped into the masts and rigging of VanCleef's juggernaut, a giant warship modeled on the orcish ships of the Second War.

His aim is simple. He wants the ship built as cheaply as it can be. To lead the work he built himself a lumber-slicing shredder, and riding it he gets terrifyingly fast results.
