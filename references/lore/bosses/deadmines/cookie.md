# Cookie

A boss of Deadmines, number 7 of 8 in Forever's order.

Cookie is a murloc who serves as the ship's cook aboard the juggernaut, in the service of Captain Greenskin and Edwin VanCleef. No one except VanCleef knows how or why he was given the post. The pirates do not seem to mind, since for them a meal is a meal.
