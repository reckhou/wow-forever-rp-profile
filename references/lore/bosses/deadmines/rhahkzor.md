# Rhahk'Zor

A boss of Deadmines, number 1 of 8 in Forever's order.

Rhahk'Zor is an ogre and the Defias Brotherhood's head foreman in the Deadmines. The mines were abandoned when Stormwind fell in the First War, and now he runs them for Edwin VanCleef and oversees VanCleef's building projects. Ore, timber and metal flow into the caves from the mines and farms the Brotherhood has seized across Westfall and Elwynn.

The laborers answer to Rhahk'Zor, and he keeps them at work by "motivating" them. His methods are unconventional, but they get results.
