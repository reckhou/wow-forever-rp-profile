# The Abandoned

A boss of Ruins of Lordaeron, number 2 of 7 in Forever's order.

The Abandoned is a great ghost that rises from a statue in the Ruins of Lordaeron when the stone stirs. Ever since Arthas destroyed the city, the ruins have been haunted by the dead of Lordaeron, and their ghosts still wander the courtyard.
