# Witherfang

A boss of Ruins of Lordaeron, number 1 of 7 in Forever's order.

A venomous arthropod called Witherfang stalks the ruins above the Undercity. Its toxins are said to eat through even undead flesh.

That claim interests Theodore Griffs of the Apothecarium, who studies the most virulent of Azeroth's exotic specimens. He wants a sample of the creature's blood, so that he can learn how its venom works.
