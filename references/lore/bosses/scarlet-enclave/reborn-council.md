# Reborn Council

A boss of Scarlet Enclave, number 6 of 8 in Forever's order.

The Reborn Council is made of three officers of the Scarlet Monastery. Herod was a champion of the Crusade, a brute who welcomed any real challenge. Interrogator James Vishas had a high, shrill voice and took pride in torturing captured undead for their secrets. Arcanist Doan kept the Monastery's library, held a key that opened Scarlet operations across the Plaguelands, and believed that arcane magic was the only way to rid Lordaeron of the Scourge.

All three fell at the Monastery. All three now walk again, raised into undeath, and they defend the Scarlet Enclave. Vishas, who once tormented the undead, has become one of them.
