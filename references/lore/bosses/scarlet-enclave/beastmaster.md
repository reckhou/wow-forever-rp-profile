# Beastmaster

A boss of Scarlet Enclave, number 3 of 8 in Forever's order.

The Beastmaster, Alexei, carries the title of Beastlord and swears his life to the Scarlet Crusade. He has hunted in the Plaguelands for some time, and he calls those who enter the Enclave the easiest quarry he has met there. His traps are of his own design, and he says he will find a place for the heads of those he catches. He speaks to Sir Dornel as a companion.
