# Solistrasza

A boss of Scarlet Enclave, number 4 of 8 in Forever's order.

Solistrasza is a dragon touched by the Light and held in thrall by it. Grand Crusader Caldoran used the Light to enslave her, and she serves as his pet. Whelps bright with the same power gather around her, and she dwells inside the inner walls of the Scarlet Enclave.

When the Crusade wanted the Argent Dawn drawn into the Enclave, she flew against Light's Hope Chapel and then withdrew behind the walls. The attack was a ruse.
