# Overlord Ramtusk

A boss of Razorfen Kraul, number 4 of 6 in Forever's order.

Overlord Ramtusk commands the military forces of the Death's Head tribe. He is a massive, aging quilboar, and his thick hide carries the scars of many battles against centaurs, harpies, orcs and even tauren.

Age has not slowed him. Ramtusk directs his troops with the swift, confident authority of a battle-forged veteran, and he goes to war crying out for victory and for Agamaggan. Two Razorfen Spearhides defend him with their lives. Striking him down would deal a crippling blow to the Death's Head.
