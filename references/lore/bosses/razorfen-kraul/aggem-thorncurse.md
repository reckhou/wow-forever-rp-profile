# Aggem Thorncurse

A boss of Razorfen Kraul, number 2 of 6 in Forever's order.

Quilboar society has long favored shamanism, and the greatest shaman of a tribe are named prophets of Agamaggan. Calling on the power of their demigod ancestor, these prophets summon boar spirits to fight at their side, much as other shaman call spirit wolves.

Aggem Thorncurse is the Death's Head Prophet. The Death's Head tribe preaches that Agamaggan, the boar god who fell in the War of the Ancients, will rise from the dead, and its preachers are called speakers and prophets. Aggem holds his place among them in Razorfen Kraul.
