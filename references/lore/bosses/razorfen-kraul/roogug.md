# Roogug

A boss of Razorfen Kraul, number 1 of 6 in Forever's order.

Among the quilboar, the reading of stones and crystals is held to be the highest form of shamanism. Roogug served his people for many years and spent them quietly honing the art, until he became the overseer of every geomancer in the Razorfen tribe.

He has gathered a powerful array of crystals and uses them to dispose of weaker foes. The mystics under his watch grow in number, and it is only a matter of time before they overrun the southern Barrens.
