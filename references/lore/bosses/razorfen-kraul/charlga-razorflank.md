# Charlga Razorflank

A boss of Razorfen Kraul, number 6 of 6 in Forever's order.

Charlga Razorflank, the Crone, rules the Razorfen tribes from deep inside Razorfen Kraul. She is a quilboar of unmatched skill in geomancy, and her rise to power was swift. Under her leadership the Razorfen conquered the great thorn mass of the Kraul and drove the tauren out of the southern Barrens.

From the Kraul the quilboar now raid rival tribes and Horde villages, and violence between quilboar tribesmen is growing. Charlga is said to be negotiating with agents of the Scourge, aligning a tribe that does not suspect it with the undead for some purpose of her own. Her people revere Agamaggan, the great boar whose fall in the War of the Ancients is said to have raised the thorns around them.
