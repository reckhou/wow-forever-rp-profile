# Blasted Lands

The Blasted Lands take their name honestly. The red clay underfoot was once tan earth, part of a dark marsh called the Black Morass, before the Orcish Horde tore the Dark Portal open here and poured into Azeroth. What magic leaked through that day scorched the soil red and killed it for good. Nothing grows here now but hardy desert scrub, and hardy desert things: basilisks, scorpions, hyenas.

Archmage Khadgar and the Kirin Tor raised Nethergarde Keep to watch the ruin of the gateway and see that nothing came through it again. Peace held for two years, until the Horde of Draenor reopened the Portal and besieged the keep. When Ner'zhul's ritual began tearing Draenor apart, Khadgar and the other magi used the Skull of Gul'dan to blast the Portal's stone frame to pieces from the far side, severing the bridge between the worlds for good.

Today Nethergarde's mages and paladins still keep their grim watch over the dead Portal, a tight, humorless community that trusts no one and forgives no lapse, the only shelter to be found in a land where the storms off the eastern sea run off the baked ground before they can do any good.

## Dreadmaul Post

Dreadmaul Post is a mound raised by the Dreadmaul ogre clan, who crossed into Azeroth from Draenor and never wandered far from the Dark Portal. They hold the ground still.

## Garrison Armory

In the scarred ground of the Blasted Lands, Alliance miners work the Garrison Armory, wresting minerals from the earth for Nethergarde Keep. What they gather is shipped on to Stormwind, and in return the keep receives the other supplies it needs to endure in that blighted place.

## Nethergarde Keep

Nethergarde Keep stands on a high bluff above the only road into the Blasted Lands, its walls raised to watch a scar in the world that no one wanted opened again.

Archmage Khadgar called for its building, in the years after the Second War, when the first Dark Portal lay broken and the orcish homelands beyond it had withered into ruin. The Alliance of Lordaeron paid for it, some kingdoms more gladly than others, and the Stonemasons Guild raised it under Edwin VanCleef's hand. The Kirin Tor manned it afterward, laboring to hold back the taint that crept from the portal's wound and threatened to spread across the land beyond.

Khadgar remained its master until Ner'zhul's clans reforged the Dark Portal from the Draenor side and besieged the keep. Khadgar and his companions Turalyon, Danath Trollbane, Alleria Windrunner, and Kurdran Wildhammer led the Alliance through the portal to break the Horde there, then stayed behind on the other side to seal it, so that Draenor's ruin would not follow them home. For twenty years after, the portal stood silent.

## Rise of the Defiler

The Rise of the Defiler rears up from the middle of the Blasted Lands, a craggy stretch of broken mountain. Here dwells the great demon Razelikh, its presence enough to give the place its name.

## Serpent's Coil

The Serpent's Coil is a winding cave system in the eastern Blasted Lands, its mouth marked by burnt-out orcish structures left over from the Second War. Long after that war ended, the caves became a hollow for something darker: cultists given over to the worship of Kirith the Damned.

Into that labyrinth came Archmage Allistarj, who set his Shadowsworn to work from within it. Near the entrance a chest holds an amulet bearing his name.

## The Dark Portal

The portal's making was a betrayal from the first stroke. Gul'dan and the human mage Medivh, the latter possessed by the Dark Titan Sargeras, tore the rift together. On Draenor, the orc stonemasons who built its stone frame were murdered by warlocks to prime the final seal, and nearly every draenei prisoner still held by the Horde was brought to its base and drained of life in a single instant to give Gul'dan the power to bridge the two worlds. Through that gateway the Horde poured into Stormwind and began the First War, and in the war that followed it fed endless orcs and ogres into Azeroth, the swamps around it curdling into the blood-red waste later called the Blasted Lands.

At the war's end the archmage Khadgar and his fellow magi shattered the structure on Azeroth's side to bar the Horde's retreat, an explosion so violent it destroyed the frame on Draenor too. Khadgar raised Nethergarde Keep afterward to stand watch over the ruin, though the tear between the worlds was never truly sealed, and demons would come through it again in the years after.

## The Tainted Scar

The Tainted Scar was once part of the Black Morass, before the Dark Portal's corruption bled into it and left the ground dry, treeless, and red as blood. No water runs there now. Demonic beasts prowl soil that no longer remembers what grew on it.

After the battle at Mount Hyjal, the ered'ruin lord Kazzak withdrew what remained of the Burning Legion's forces on Azeroth into the Scar, making it his ground. He set his lieutenant Razelikh the Defiler over it, charging the Defiler to hold the land secure. Kazzak himself remained there afterward, his presence alone enough to mark the Scar as Legion territory still, years after Hyjal was fought and won elsewhere.

An old human warlock, Daio the Decrepit, came to the Scar long after and made his home at its altar. There, among the demons the land already bred, he practiced his rituals undisturbed and took on young recruits, teaching them the infernal arts in a place already given over to the fel.
