## Scholomance

On a rise above the abandoned city of Caer Darrow stands the manor that was once the seat of House Barov, a family whose reach ran from Caer Darrow to Brill, to Southshore, to Tarren Mill. For decades they ruled in comfort, but as the years drew toward war the Barovs grew bitter and grasping, unwilling to let death end what they had built. So they struck a bargain with Kel'Thuzad, master of the Cult of the Damned, to hold their fortune and their land beyond the span of mortal life.

Within six months the keep had rotted into something else entirely. Acolytes of the Cult studied the necromancer's art while the family's own servants were given over as living experiments for a plague of undeath. Before Uther the Lightbringer could pay a scheduled visit to the manor, a sickness bred in its halls was loosed upon Caer Darrow itself. It left no one alive, and bound its victims to wander afterward as ghosts.

The Barovs themselves rose again as undead, still bound to the halls they had sold away. Now the mortal necromancer Darkmaster Gandling serves as headmaster, having succeeded Araj the Summoner, who himself followed Kel'Thuzad.
