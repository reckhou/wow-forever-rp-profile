# Eastern Plaguelands

The ground here was farmland once, the villages of Eastweald scattered around Stratholme, second city of Lordaeron. None of that remains. The soil is brown and sick now, the trees swollen into mushrooms, the air thick with orange mist.

It was at Stratholme that Prince Arthas came, hunting the source of a plague that had already claimed the townsfolk. Lord Uther, master of the Silver Hand, refused to let him purge the city. Arthas stripped him of command, disbanded the Silver Hand on the spot, and put the people of Stratholme to the sword himself rather than let them rise as the Scourge's own. He did not stop there -- he hunted the dreadlord Mal'Ganis into the north, returned a death knight, and killed his own father to end the kingdom of Lordaeron.

Alexandros Mograine raised a Crusade from the Silver Hand's remnant to take the ruin back, seizing Tyr's Hand and half of Stratholme before his death broke his order into the Argent Dawn and the Scarlet Crusade that still holds these fields, warring the Scourge without end and welcoming no one else.

## Blackwood Lake

The waters of Blackwood Lake run foul, infested by corrupted water elementals coiling through the murk. Old annals of Darrowshire claim the lake bed holds the head of Horgus the Ravager, sunk somewhere in that dark.

Before the plague, a town called Blackwood stood here, home to Grand Marshal Othmar Garithos. Nothing remains of it now but the lake bearing its name.

## Browman Mill

Stephen Browman once cut timber at the mill north of Light's Hope Chapel. The Scourge killed him like everyone else the plague touched, then raised him as a ghoul bound to its service. He never left. He stands guard now inside the walls he once owned.

The mill is thick with skeletons, banshees, and Abominations, more garrison than ruin, and nothing about it invites anyone to linger.

## Corin's Crossing

Corin's Crossing sits where the roads to Andorhal, Darrowshire, Stratholme, and Tyr's Hand meet, between the Infectis Scar and Lake Mereldar. Once an ordinary crossroads town, it kept a town hall and a blacksmith's forge.

Now the Scourge fills its streets entirely, and among the dead move things that cannot be seen at all -- Unseen Servants, which see through stealth as if it were nothing. No quiet path exists through Corin's Crossing.

## Crown Guard Tower

Crown Guard Tower stands watch over the Eastern Plaguelands as one of its scout towers. The Scourge invasion of Lordaeron tore through the region, and the tower wears its scars still, a silent marker of the dead lands.

## Darrowshire

Darrowshire once stood as a village in its own right, until the Third War brought the Scourge to its gates. A ghoul lord named Horgus the Ravager led the assault, alongside the death knight Marduk Blackpool. The village's defenders fought back with some success at first, but the tide turned, and in the end every one of them was slaughtered.

Their deaths did not end them. The fallen rose again as ghouls, bound into the service of the very Scourge that killed them, their spirits trapped inside the rotting flesh that once was theirs.

Darrowshire itself now stands silent and broken, empty but for ghosts and memory.

## Eastwall Gate

The Eastwall Gate stands as the side way into Stratholme, opening directly onto Elder's Square, near the Alonsus Chapel where Aurius keeps his place.

## Eastwall Tower

Eastwall Tower stands in the Eastern Plaguelands, a scout tower that once watched over the land for Lordaeron. The Scourge swept through the kingdom, and the tower wears its scars still, watching over dead lands.

## Lake Mereldar

Lake Mereldar's waters lie thick with plague south of Corin's Crossing, corrupted water elementals moving beneath the surface. On its shore stand the ruins of an old house, and a shack and ferry that once served a farm now abandoned.

## Light's Hope Chapel

Beneath the mountains east of the Plaguelands stands a chapel far older than the ruin around it, among the oldest and most revered holy places the Church of the Holy Light ever raised.

Paladins who would not follow the path the Scarlet Crusade was taking withdrew instead into the Eastern Plaguelands and made this chapel their base, calling themselves the Argent Dawn. Lord Raymond George led them first; when he died, Lord Maxwell Tyrosus took his place. Unlike the Scarlet Crusade, they turned no one away for their race.

Though the Argent Dawn stands apart from the Scarlet Crusade, the chapel remains neutral ground where the two still meet to speak with one another.

## Mazra'Alor

At the heart of Zul'Mashar rises Mazra'Alor, a ziggurat of stacked stone standing over the ruined lands around it. The Mossflayer tribe holds it still, their shadow hunters keeping watch from its heights over the troll settlement below.

## Northdale

Northdale is a ruined town north of Light's Hope Chapel, on the edge of Quel'Thalas. Here Taelan Fordring renounced the Order of the Silver Hand for the Scarlet Crusade.

Banshees and unseen watchers haunt the wreckage now. The nearby lake holds corrupted water elementals. Nothing living remains.

## Northpass Tower

Northpass Tower stands farthest north of the watchtowers in the Eastern Plaguelands, marking the road toward Quel'Thalas. The Scourge swept through Lordaeron, and the tower wears its scars still.

## Pestilent Scar

The Pestilent Scar is a deep gorge in the blighted Eastern Plaguelands, likely a lake that dried when the Plague of Undeath struck. It lies mostly dry now, a wound in the earth, its depths home to oozes born of the old corruption.

## Plaguewood

The trees south of Stratholme once stood green and untroubled, a woodland not unlike Elwynn Forest, with a small village nestled near the stone arch at its edge. It was here that Sergeant Morigan, a Lordaeronian soldier born to that village, first noticed the poisoned grain out of Andorhal working its wrongness on the land, when Kel'Thuzad began his work. His warning came too late for the kingdom, and the same road he watched over was the one Prince Arthas later rode down on his way to Stratholme.

What was left afterward was not a forest anymore. The soil itself went spongy underfoot, and mushrooms and toadstools swelled to the size of trees, towering over what remained of the old woodland. They breathe out a dark brown gas not unlike the fumes that rise from the Scourge's plague cauldrons, though no one can say for certain whether the fungus simply grows in poisoned ground or whether the Scourge cultivates it to spread its sickness further. It was for this transformation that the wood took its new name.

## Quel'Lithien Lodge

Quel'Lithien Lodge stood as a Farstrider camp near Quel'Thalas, a place of warmth and welcome. The lodge once housed a number of elven documents, some pertaining to the royal family and the Ranger-General of Silvermoon. By the time of the Second War, Renthar Hawkspear led the hunters there.

## Scarlet Base Camp

On the eastern shore of Lake Mereldar, not far from the religious bastion of Tyr's Hand, the Scarlet Base Camp stands behind its barricades, its defenses reinforced against whatever might come.

## Stratholme

Stratholme's ruin began with grain. Shipments from Andorhal, secretly infected by the Cult of the Damned, were baked into bread and eaten by a populace who never knew they were dying. Prince Arthas Menethil arrived with Uther the Lightbringer and Jaina Proudmoore to find the plague already taking hold. Believing the only mercy left was a clean death rather than a slow turning into the walking dead, Arthas ordered the city put to the torch. Uther and Jaina refused and withdrew their men. Arthas did not. Fire swept the streets, and almost every soul in Stratholme was killed before the dreadlord Mal'Ganis, whom Arthas had come to kill, slipped away laughing, leaving the prince to burn what remained.

It was not enough. The dead rose anyway, and the city became the Scourge's capital in Lordaeron, ruled in turn by the lich Kel'Thuzad and later the death knight Baron Rivendare.

Stratholme still burns.

## Terrordale

Terrordale sits at the edge of the Plaguewood, its streets emptied and given over to the Scourge and plagued animals. Only the merchant Augustus the Touched and the ghost hunter Egan still linger among the ruin.

The town is haunted. Spirits walk its lanes, and in certain places Terrordale itself seems to falter, the physical world buckling under the weight of the realm beyond it, as though the ground remembers some old torment it cannot release.

It was this unrest that drew Lord Grayson Shadowbreaker, who saw in the spirits a fitting trial for paladins seeking to prove themselves worthy of judgment and sacrifice, a step toward earning the chargers reserved for the truly deserving. What the spirits had been in life goes unrecorded, only that their torment was real enough to warp the town around them.

## Terrorweb Tunnel

The tunnel bores through the mountain northwest of Terrordale, running south to where it meets the Thondroril River. Nerubian crypt fiends choke the passage entire, and among them stalks something worse: a Nerubian Overseer, far deadlier than its kin.

At the tunnel's mouth by the river, Tirion Fordring keeps watch, facing the dark of the passage from the light outside it.

## The Fungal Vale

North of the Marris Stead and the Crown Guard Tower lies the Fungal Vale, ground gone dark purple beneath a murky haze. Here the Plague of Undeath has taken its deepest hold, and the Scourge and the Cult of the Damned move freely through the corruption, holding the hollow as entirely their own.

## The Infectis Scar

Near Corin's Crossing, the death knight Marduk Blackpool's runesword shattered against a dwarf captain's shield. He kept the hilt and cast the ruined blade aside as worthless. But it sank into the earth, and the ground opened beneath it, filling with dark water.

What remains is unnatural through and through, unlike the nearby Pestilent Scar, a mere natural gorge.

## The Marris Stead

The Marris Stead sits on a low ridge above the region's main road, a farmstead that once belonged to Nathanos Marris. His cousin Stephon lived here for a time, and Sylvanas Windrunner was known to visit Nathanos beneath its roof.

Freed by the Banshee Queen after she raised the Forsaken in the Undercity, Nathanos returned to the home he had lost and made it his own again on her order. From here he drew Horde soldiers into the fight against the Scourge and the Scarlet Crusade still infesting the Plaguelands, and he gathered relics from the battle of Darrowshire, keeping them within the stead for reasons he told no one.

## The Noxious Glade

The Noxious Glade sits up a hill north of Light's Hope Chapel, more tainted by the blight than the rest of the Plaguelands around it. The air hangs thick and sickly green, and the ground has been stained a dark purple where nothing green can grow.

The Scourge has made the glade its own: a plague cauldron, a slaughterhouse, and a few ziggurats stand among the poisoned ground, and Scourge creatures move through it in numbers.

## The Scarlet Basilica

The Scarlet Basilica stands within Tyr's Hand, the high seat of the Scarlet Crusade's faith, bound to the abbey nearby and to the distant Scarlet Monastery in Tirisfal Glades. Crusader Lord Valdelmar holds it, his devotion to the Crusade's cause absolute.

## The Undercroft

The Undercroft is a crypt in the southwestern Eastern Plaguelands, home to undead trolls led by Zaeldarr the Outcast, a risen dire troll. From here he raids the Argent Dawn's grave pit at Light's Hope Chapel, stealing bodies for his own.

## Thondroril River

The Thondroril River divides the Western and Eastern Plaguelands. It rises far north near the Terrorweb Tunnel and runs south until it empties into Darrowmere Lake, continuing beyond as the Darrowmere River.

Its banks belong to the blight. Maggots and vampire bats crowd the wet ground, plaguebears and spiders stalk the shallows, and snapjaws lurk where the current slows.

## Tyr's Hand

Tyr's Hand is a fortified human city, one of the last holdouts of the living against the Scourge in the region. Its surroundings are in far better condition than the rest of the Plaguelands, though the trees and flora still decay.

The city is among the oldest and most revered holy sites of the Church of the Light, built almost entirely of churches, crowned by the Scarlet Basilica and the fortified abbey.

When the Scourge came and the Eastweald became the Plaguelands, Tyr's Hand did not fall. Under Lord Valdelmar it withstood daily assault, sheltered first by the Knights of the Silver Hand and later by the Scarlet Crusade, sending supplies and reinforcements onward to other Scarlet holdings, with daily reports carried in from Stratholme by couriers who had to fight their way through the plague-thick roads.

## Zul'Mashar

Zul'Mashar was once the capital of the Mossflayer tribe, forest trolls of the northeastern Eastern Plaguelands. Long wars against the humans of Lordaeron left the tribe's lands in disarray, and the plague of undeath that followed brought ruin deeper still. The city that once ruled here stands now as a shadow of its former glory.

Its ziggurat, Mazra'Alor, still stands at the heart of the city, held now by Warlord Thresh'jin and Zul'Brin Warpbranch. But the Mossflayers' own people are being covertly transformed by the plague, and Zul'Mashar is in danger of becoming nothing more than a bastion for the undead.
