# Feralas

Feralas rises strange among its neighbors, a rainforest of ancient trees where the wastes around it promise only dust. Scattered through its green stand the ruins of the elves, half swallowed by root and vine, greatest among them the buried city of Dire Maul.

The Gordunni ogres hold Feralas now, and they have made it theirs by breaking it. They have torn down whole stretches of forest, and their taint has settled over the land like a sickness. Beyond the walls of Dire Maul their raiding parties burn villages and murder travelers through the southern woods, a corruption spreading through the wilds that traces back to the ruins beneath.

Grimtotem tauren live scattered through the region still, hunted by those who remember what the tribe has done.

## Camp Mojache

Camp Mojache sits where a swift stream feeds into Wildwind Lake. The tauren who hold this ground are known for the thickness of their hides and the courage of their braves, who guard travelers against the dangers of Feralas.

## Dire Maul

Twelve thousand years ago, a covert sect of night elf sorcerers raised Eldre'Thalas to guard Queen Azshara's most prized arcane secrets. Among its Highborne, the arcanists known as the Shen'dralar lived in quiet splendor, and one of them, Millicent Serene, wrought the Fruit of Fertility, a vine whose aura of growth and protection let the whole city flourish.

Cut off from the Well of Eternity after the Great Sundering, the Highborne bound a demon, Immol'thar, and drew their power from his imprisonment. It sustained them for thousands of years, until the cost of holding him grew greater than what he gave. Their leader, Prince Tortheldrin, answered this by turning on his own people, slaughtering many of them to free enough power to keep the rest alive. The survivors retreated deeper into the ruins they once ruled, abandoning the rest of the city to whatever would come to dwell there.

The stone of Eldre'Thalas still stands, arcane energy bound within it by a craft none now remember, holding the shape of a city that fed itself on its own people to survive.

## Dream Bough

A great tree rises from a small island in the middle of Jademir Lake, its roots and branches holding one of the portals into the Emerald Dream. The Green dragonflight keeps watch over it, guarding the threshold against any who would tamper with the dream below.

## Feathermoon Stronghold

Off the western coast of Feralas, on Sardor Isle, stands a fortress raised in the sea by General Shandris Feathermoon, who for ten thousand years has stood at Tyrande Whisperwind's side as her right hand among the Sentinels. She gave the stronghold her name, and it holds this stretch of Kalimdor's coast for the night elves still.

Only the sea joins it to the mainland, a boat crossing from the Forgotten Coast to the island dock.

## Feral Scar Vale

In the High Wilderness of western Feralas lies Feral Scar Vale, claimed by the Feral Scar yeti who give it their name.

## Frayfeather Highlands

The Frayfeather Highlands belong to the Frayfeather hippogryphs, who nest in the high places there and give the land their name. Among the crags their eggs lie hidden in scattered nests, guarded by the wheeling flocks that call the highlands home.

## Gordunni Outpost

In the mountains north of Lariss Pavilion, the ground bears the mark of the Gordunni. Where Feralas is elsewhere green, the outpost lies withered and brown under the ogres' presence.

Two caves mark the hills, a small one near the entrance and a great mound at the back, both held by the Gordunni who claim this blighted ground.

## Grimtotem Compound

The Grimtotem Compound, east of Dire Maul, is a home the Grimtotem tauren have made for themselves. In its southern section stands a large cage, holding captive sprite darters. East of the northern section, gnolls have made their own camps.

## High Wilderness

The High Wilderness spreads across western Feralas, from the Forgotten Coast to the Verdantis River. Bears, wolves, and hippogryphs roam its ancient trees, treants stand among the deeper groves, and yetis and ogres claim the wilder reaches beyond.

## Isle of Dread

The Isle of Dread lies off the coast of Feralas, south of Sardor Isle, its central valley walled in on every side by unyielding cliffs save for a single approach in the southeast. Within that hidden basin the chimaerok make their home, arcane-touched beasts and devourers alike, ruled over by Lord Lakmaeran. His flesh has been sought for stranger purposes than survival: rendered down and reshaped, it became a monstrous five-hundred-pound fowl devoured whole by the gnome wizard Narain Soothfancy.

At the northern end of the isle, a cave sinks into the rock unlike anything else known there, deep and strange in its making. A secret passage beneath the water leads through to an inner chamber that serves no purpose anyone has found beyond sheltering Lady Szallah in its depths. The cave and its naga are commanded by Lord Shalzaru, whose presence drew the attention of Feathermoon Stronghold, wary of the naga so close to their own shores.

## Jademir Lake

Jademir Lake in northern Feralas holds a small island, Dream Bough, at its center. A portal into the Emerald Dream stands there, guarded by Lethlas and a number of green dragonspawn. Infected green whelps linger along the lake's northwestern shore.

## Lariss Pavilion

The Lariss Pavilion, night elf in its stonework, stands north of Camp Mojache in Feralas. Its dryads were once peaceful, but the Emerald Nightmare has corrupted them, and now they attack any who draw near.

Beneath the Pavilion lies a hidden way down, known to Azj'Tordin, a Highborne who broke with Prince Tortheldrin and fled Dire Maul through it. He escaped with little, for an imp named Pusillin stole his book of incantations before he could vanish below, and he has kept to that passage ever since.

## Lower Wilds

The Lower Wilds fills the eastern reach of Feralas, where treants move among the trees and corrupted dryads walk, their gifts turned strange.

## Oneiros

South of Jademir Lake in northern Feralas lie the ruins of Oneiros.

## Rage Scar Hold

Rage Scar Hold is a dug-in cave in Feralas, holding the fiercest yetis in the area. Scattered within are books and strange symbols, hinting at arcane study, or perhaps the remains of some unfortunate magus who met his end there.

## Ruins of Isildien

The Ruins of Isildien are what remains of a great night elven city, broken ten thousand years ago. Gordunni ogres now nest among its fallen colonnades, ruling over stones raised by hands far older than their own.

## Ruins of Ravenwind

Northern Feralas holds the broken stones of Ravenwind, raised long ago by the Highborne of the kaldorei, much as Dire Maul was raised elsewhere. Little of that glory remains standing now. Northspring harpies have taken the ruins for their own, nesting among the fallen columns and empty halls.

## Ruins of Solarsal

Southwest of Feathermoon Stronghold, on Sardor Isle, the old stones of Solarsal still stand among the trees, night elven work long since abandoned to the wild. The Hatecrest naga hold the ruins now, coiling through the broken columns where the elves once walked.

## Sardor Isle

Sardor Isle rises off the coast of Feralas, a small refuge held by the night elves under Shandris Feathermoon. Naga have crept onto its shores, and wolves range through what wild places remain, so the stronghold's watch is never idle.

## Shalzaru's Lair

South of Feathermoon Stronghold's island, at the northern end of the Isle of Dread, a twisting cave holds two chambers joined by a waterway. Hatecrest sorceresses and serpent guards fill its length. Deep within dwells Shalzaru, master of the Hatecrest.

## Thalanaar

Thalanaar stands quiet, a small outpost with little to draw travelers beyond its purpose. Caryssia Moonhunter keeps her craft here, working hides and leather in the old tribal way, one of the few reasons anyone has cause to linger.

## The Forgotten Coast

The Forgotten Coast runs the whole western edge of Feralas, where the land meets the Veiled Sea. Water elementals rise from the surf here, and sea giants stalk the shoreline.

A murloc fishing village clings to this shore. Birds have been known to swoop in and carry off tadpoles, and the village has suffered raids by humans and gnolls in turn. Worst of all are the Gordunni ogres, who have preyed upon it for years, dragging murlocs off into captivity within the ruins of Dire Maul. A dozen or more have been taken this way, among them two named Murrl and Murrgly.

## The Twin Colossals

The Twin Colossals rise on the western edge of Feralas, two vertical mountains so high they are the tallest point in the region, with the road to Desolace threading the gap between them.

At the top of the eastern peak lies a small, deep pond, its water calm enough to fish, where Gregan Brewspewer can be found. From the summit the whole region opens out below.

## The Writhing Deep

The Writhing Deep, in southeastern Feralas, is given over to Hive Zukk'ash. The silithid there have poisoned the ground around them, driving nearby gnolls to frenzy and north against Camp Mojache.

Warriors from the camp cut down the silithid on the surface, but the hive's heart beneath the earth, packed with eggs, was never touched. Rexxar warns it is only a matter of time before the next generation hatches.

## Verdantis River

The Verdantis River winds through central Feralas, southeast of the ruins of Dire Maul, cutting a quiet green thread through the tangled forest. Its waters hold Feralas ahi, found nowhere else in the world.

## Wildwind Lake

Wildwind Lake lies near Camp Mojache in Feralas, its waters quiet beneath the surrounding wilds, home to nothing more remarkable than what swims beneath its surface and the stillness that hangs over the camp nearby.

## Woodpaw Den

In the heart of Feralas lies Woodpaw Den, warren to the Woodpaw gnolls. Here the Woodpaw Alphas hold sway over their kin, thick upon the ground in numbers few outsiders care to test. South of the den stand the Darkmist Ruins, silent neighbors to the gnolls' clamor.

## Woodpaw Hills

The Woodpaw Hills lie thick with forest, home to the gnolls of the Woodpaw clan. Their hold on the land is slipping, for in the southeasternmost reaches the ground has split open into what is now called the Writhing Deep, and the swarming things that burst up from beneath the earth have been driving the gnolls from their own territory. Where Woodpaw Alphas once led their tribe from the camps, they now hold only in the Woodpaw Den.
