# Dun Morogh

Snow lies deep across Dun Morogh, walled in on every side by the Khaz Modan mountains, its passes watched always by dwarven troops. Before the mountains belonged to anyone else, they belonged to the Frostmane ice trolls, who had settled the region after breaking away from the Drakkari Empire in ages past.

That changed when the first gnomes climbed out of Uldaman and turned their wits toward surviving the cold and the trolls alike, digging fortified dwellings deep into the peaks. Five hundred years later the dwarves followed them up out of Uldaman, drawn by the same mountains they had glimpsed on the horizon, and raised Ironforge beneath the tallest of them. Finding the gnomes already settled nearby, the two peoples struck a friendship that has never broken: the dwarves lending their strength to raise Gnomeregan, the gnomes sharing the secrets of their machines. Together their armies broke the Frostmane trolls and drove the survivors back into the hills, ending the trolls' hold on the land for good.

Gnomeregan is gone now, ruined, and its people crowd into Ironforge for shelter.

## Amberstill Ranch

Amberstill Ranch sits off the road to Loch Modan, north of Gol'Bolar Quarry, where the dwarves of the Amberstill family raise their herds against the cold of Dun Morogh. Rudra Amberstill breeds prized rams here, while young Yarlyn keeps her snowshoe rabbits underfoot.

The herds have not gone untroubled. A wendigo named Vagash has taken to stalking the ranch's animals, and the Amberstills have had to defend what they raise against its hunger.

## Anvilmar

In Coldridge Valley stands Anvilmar, a hall built around a great forge. It was the first true settlement the dwarves raised in these northern reaches, a gathering place before any others took shape. The forge at its heart burns still, though no smiths teach their craft there now.

## Brewnall Village

Brewnall Village sits in Chill Breeze Valley, near Gnomeregan, home to the Barleybrew family. The Barleybrews have given themselves to one pursuit: perfecting the dwarven art of brewing. They send their young out from the village to guard it from wild animals and to seek rare ingredients for the ultimate ale.

## Chill Breeze Valley

Chill Breeze Valley runs east to west between Gnomeregan and Kharanos, its path worn familiar by young dwarves and gnomes passing through. But the valley bends north and south as well, and there the snow tells a different story: bloodstains spread wide across the white, and torn animal carcasses lie abandoned. Few travel this branch willingly. Those who do go hunting the beasts responsible, hoping to end their threat to the dwarven lands, and many do not return. Deep in one of the caves lairs a wendigo, the creature most responsible for the blood in the snow.

## Coldridge Pass

Coldridge Pass is the narrow way out of Coldridge Valley, the last stretch a dwarf must cross before reaching Kharanos and the wider roads of Dun Morogh. Troggs have overrun the pass, and travelers are left with only two choices: run through as fast as their legs allow, or cut a bloody path through the invaders.

Fendrig Redbeard once led twelve other miners into the pass to investigate the infestation. Deep inside, he was pinned beneath a fall of stone. The others turned back to dig him free, but the effort brought the pass down around them. Twelve miners died. Only Fendrig, pulled from the rubble, survived.

## Coldridge Valley

Snow lies thick over Coldridge Valley, walled in on every side, its only way out the narrow tunnel of Coldridge Pass to the northeast. That passage is infested with trogg raiders, brutish things that have long made the crossing to Kharanos and Ironforge dangerous for anyone travelling it alone.

Beyond the pass the road climbs east, then north through Kharanos, before winding up through the mountains to the gates of Ironforge itself.

## Frostmane Hold

Frostmane Hold stands on the western edge of Dun Morogh's cold mountains, south of Gnomeregan, a scatter of buildings knit to a cave system driven into the mountainside. It is the base camp of the Frostmane ice trolls, who hold the ground with headhunters and hideskinners guarding every approach.

## Gates of Ironforge

The Gates of Ironforge rise where the great ramp of Dun Morogh climbs to meet the mountain, visible for miles across the snows before a traveler ever reaches them. They stand as the threshold between the open dwarven lands and Ironforge proper.

Passing through, one comes under the gaze of a huge statue raised to High King Modimus Anvilmar, set to keep watch just inside the entrance, so that none enter the city without passing beneath his eyes first.

## Gnomeregan

Gnomeregan is the subterranean capital of the gnomish kingdom of the same name, wrought-iron evidence of gnomish brilliance, ambition, and ingenuity.

During the Third War, a trogg invasion brought about its downfall. The troggs had been pulled from their sleep by the upheaval of titan sites, and swarmed up into the city from below. In a desperate attempt to kill the invaders, High Tinker Gelbin Mekkatorque, on the advice of his advisor Sicco Thermaplugg, ordered his people to open the pressure valves of their giant, grinding machines and release toxic radiation throughout Gnomeregan. Though the gas killed some of the troggs, it only empowered others, and nearly eighty percent of gnomekind died within days. The survivors fled to the protection of Ironforge. Others were twisted into horrific, mindless, evil-doing leper gnomes, who still haunt the ruined tunnels.

Driven mad by the radiation himself, Thermaplugg declared himself "King of Gnomeregan" and seized control of the poisoned city, leading an army of troggs and leper gnomes through its wrecked halls and workshops.

## Gol'Bolar Quarry

Gol'Bolar Quarry lies dug into southeastern Dun Morogh, a mining camp cut short by what came crawling up from beneath it. The dwarves opened the excavation seeking what the mountain held, but the digging woke troggs, who swarmed up from the depths of the mine and overran the pits and tunnels the miners had carved.

The camp that remains is a foothold, not a victory: dwarves hold the surface while the mine below stays lost to the troggs.

## Helm's Bed Lake

Helm's Bed Lake lies frozen for most of the year, its surface locked in ice thick enough to walk across in safety. Only a single hole is kept open, cut so that fresh water can still be drawn from beneath the frost.

The safety ends at the shore. Wolves and other wild creatures range the banks, and stray troggs haunt the surrounding rock. South of the lake stands an abandoned building claimed by Dark Iron dwarves, who strike at anyone who strays too near it.

## Iceflow Lake

Iceflow Lake freezes hard in the cold of Dun Morogh, but the dwarves of Brewnall Village keep one hole cut through the ice, drawing fresh water and fish from beneath the frozen surface.

Out toward the lake's center rise a pair of islands, best left alone. A pack of ravenous wolves has claimed them, running under a worg named Timber, and their presence keeps the islands empty of all but the beasts that hold them.

## Ironband's Compound

South of Helm's Bed Lake, Ironband's Compound stands as a foothold for Dark Iron dwarves, who strike at anyone who strays too near. The place once belonged to Sturgy Ironband, before it fell into Dark Iron hands.

## Kharanos

Kharanos sits among the mountain peaks of Dun Morogh, its streets ringed by wintry wilds that never let its folk grow soft. A forge burns at its heart, a gnomish workshop clatters away, and the Thunderbrew Distillery pours some of the finest ale to be found anywhere, but the town has never been able to rest easy in its charm. To the west lies a cavern thick with wendigo, and to the northwest the Frostmane trolls hold Shimmer Ridge, watching the town below.

That watching once turned to war, when a giant Frostmane troll fell upon Kharanos and very nearly wiped out every dwarf living there. Where the town's defenders faltered, one dwarf did not. Lazlo Grindwidget stood against the troll and brought it down alone, and for that the town named him a hero, a title that has followed him ever since through streets that remember how close they came to silence.

## Misty Pine Refuge

Misty Pine Refuge lies quiet in central Dun Morogh, a sheltered clearing among the snow-heavy pines.

## North Gate Outpost

North Gate Outpost sits astride North Gate Pass, the narrow way through the mountains that joins Dun Morogh to Loch Modan, the northeasternmost of the dwarven holds in these highlands.

The mountaineers garrisoned here spend their days pushing back the wildlife pressing in from the surrounding peaks. Some who went hunting the more dangerous beasts have not come back, and search has been made of the mountains for word of them, with little enough to show for it.

## North Gate Pass

The pass cuts through the high mountains, dropping from Dun Morogh down toward Loch Modan. It is the shortest road between the two, and travelers bound for the lands beyond the Loch pass through here more than any other way.

The North Gate Outpost stands guard here, holding the passage.

## Shimmer Ridge

Shimmer Ridge rises in northern Dun Morogh, held by Frostmane trolls who keep two camps joined by a bridge, tending shimmerweed and taming boars among the rocks.

## South Gate Outpost

South Gate Outpost sits where the pass narrows between Dun Morogh and Loch Modan, a chokepoint of stone held by dwarven mountaineers. Theirs is quieter work than that of their kin at North Gate: they have driven back the wildlife that once pressed on the pass, and the road through it stays open and safe for those making their way to or from Thelsamar.

## South Gate Pass

South Gate Pass cuts through the mountains, joining the high snows of Dun Morogh to the lower valleys of Loch Modan. It is the shortest way down to Thelsamar, worn by travelers between the two lands.

Here stands South Gate Outpost, a guard station set to watch the pass.

## Steelgrill's Depot

Steelgrill's Depot sits east of Kharanos, a small camp of miners and engineers working the frozen ground of Dun Morogh.

## The Grizzled Den

A large cave system opens southwest of Kharanos, cutting deep into the ice of Dun Morogh. Wendigos have made their den here, shaggy frost-breathing beasts that flock together against any who trespass. Their manes are prized for seat covers, though few who go in after them come out without a fight, for the wendigos do not scatter but close ranks and drive intruders back out with claw and frozen breath.

## The Tundrid Hills

The Tundrid Hills lie south of Gol'Bolar Quarry, a stretch of wind-scoured ground where few travel without a blade in hand. Wolves and other wild things prowl the slopes, and those who cross the hills to cut between Coldridge Valley and eastern Dun Morogh do so knowing the road through Kharanos would have been safer.

The Frostmane trolls have raised a camp in the valley here, holding this ground within sight of the dwarves' quarry.

## Thunderbrew Distillery

The Thunderbrew Distillery stands in Kharanos, its name known far beyond the dwarven town for the quality of its ale. Innkeeper Belm keeps the place, offering rest and drink to those who pass through.
