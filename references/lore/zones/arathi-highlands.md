# Arathi Highlands

King Thoradin came to this arid country because a dream told him his people would prosper here, and he raised Strom upon it, the first capital of a united Arathor. The dry ground served him as a wall of thorns, denying the forest trolls of Amani the cover for their ambushes, and he raised a second wall besides. For a thousand years Strom stood at the heart of the empire, until its noble houses drifted away to richer soil and Thoradin's last descendants left to found Stormwind. What remained became Stromgarde, held ever after by the Trollbane line.

Stromgarde has since fallen, overrun by ogres and the Syndicate, its survivors driven from the keep to a canyon they now call Refuge Pointe. Nearby stands Hammerfall, once a camp where humanity caged its orcish prisoners, now held by the orcs themselves and renamed for Orgrim Doomhammer.

Thoradin's great wall has collapsed into a gaping ruin, and his keep lies broken and contested, fought over still by the ogres, the Syndicate, and the last soldiers of a kingdom trying to reclaim it.

## Boulder'gor

North of Stromgarde Keep, the hill of Boulder'gor rises broken and rough. Boulderfist ogres hold their mound here, and Witherbark forest trolls linger on its slopes alongside them. On the eastern face stands the Circle of Elements, and just south of the hill lie a pair of farms.

## Boulderfist Hall

Boulderfist Hall stands in southern Arathi Highlands, near Thandol Span and Witherbark Village, a mound of stone fortified by ogre hands. Within the larger cave at its heart rules Kor'gresh Coldrage, chief of the Boulderfist clan.

The ogres have since pushed outward into the surrounding hills, strengthening their defenses further.

## Circle of East Binding

The Stone of East Binding stands watch over the eastern reaches of Arathi Highlands, near Hammerfall, marking one of the Circles of Binding scattered across the region. Water elementals guard the stone still, bound to the place as though holding some old task unfinished.

## Circle of Inner Binding

The Circle of Inner Binding stands in southern Arathi Highlands near Stromgarde Keep, guarded by earth elementals. Here rests the Stone of Inner Binding, strongest of the stones.

Through its shard, Myzrael found a way to speak with mortals.

## Circle of Outer Binding

The Circle of Outer Binding lies near Refuge Pointe in the Arathi Highlands. At its center stands the Stone of Outer Binding, guarded always by elementals of air.

## Circle of West Binding

The Circle of West Binding stands in western Arathi Highlands, near Thoradin's Wall. There the Stone of West Binding rests, guarded by fire elementals that never leave it unwatched.

## Dabyrie's Farmstead

In the northern reaches of the Arathi Highlands stands Dabyrie's Farmstead, held by Kenata Dabyrie and her children Fardel and Marcel, citizens of Stromgarde and loyal to the Alliance. A militia keeps watch over the fields, for the family has enemies.

## Drywhisker Gorge

Drywhisker Gorge cuts through the mountains of eastern Arathi Highlands, northeast of Hammerfall. At its end lies a cave, home to the kobolds of Drywhisker, allied to the giants that share these highlands.

Deep within, past the warrens, rests a stone shard in which a piece of the essence of Princess Myzrael lies trapped. The kobolds guard it fiercely, along with the motes gathered in the cave's depths, though none among them seem to know what the shard truly holds.

## Faldir's Cove

Faldir's Cove takes its name from Faldir, who is said to have marked this spot when he and his followers set out.

Long after, the Blackwater Raiders came seeking an elven treasure said to lie in the waters nearby. Captains Steelgut and O'Breen led three ships into the cove, but a strange tide rose against them, stirred by naga guarding sunken ruins beneath the reef, and two of the three ships went down. Only the Harbinger reached the shore, and her crew found themselves trapped in the bay, unable to leave until the naga were driven off and the navigation logs recovered from the wrecks below.

Later, two gnomish engineers, Doctor Draxlegauge and Professor Phizzlethorpe, built a device to hunt for elven gems said to be calcified within the stones of the Drowned Reef.

The cove still bears the scars of that flooded history: reef, wreck, and ruin, all telling of ships that never should have sunk, and a treasure the sea has not given up.

## Go'Shek Farm

Go'Shek Farm lies in the eastern Arathi Highlands, near Witherbark Village. After the Third War, Forsaken and orcs out of Hammerfall took the farm for their own purposes, the orcs drawing supplies from it, the Forsaken turning its fields and outbuildings into a testing ground for alchemy of a darker sort.

The Alliance agents Quae and Kinelory came to look into what was happening here. With help, they cut down a Forsaken courier bound for Tarren Mill before he could deliver his message, and searched the farmstead itself, where they turned up the notes of an Apothecary Jorell. His research pointed to something new and terrible taking shape: another plague in the making.

Hammerfall Grunts hold the farm now.

## Hammerfall

The ground at Hammerfall remembers what it once was. After the Second War the Alliance of Lordaeron built one of its largest internment camps here, penning captured orcs behind its walls. It did not stay a prison. Warchief Thrall and Orgrim Doomhammer led the Horde against the camp to free their people, and in the chaos of that liberation, as Thrall covered the retreat, a charging knight drove a lance into Orgrim's back with such force that it pierced his armor front and back. Doomhammer died there, on the ground he had come to free.

After the Third War the Horde returned to the ruins and raised a forward base among them, naming it Hammerfall in honor of the Warchief who had fallen taking it. It became more than an outpost -- a refuge for those who felt they had no place left in the world, its spirit lodges, stables, forge, and great hall standing where the internment camp's walls once stood.

## Northfold Manor

After the fall of Stromgarde, Northfold Manor passed into the hands of the Syndicate, who made it an outpost in Arathi Highlands.

Here the troll shaman Tor'gan came, drawn by a wrongness in the air. Tracing it to the manor, he found the residual traces of demonic summoning still clinging to the place, and as he drew close his own blood began to boil with a rage not fully his own. On the ground he found an amulet of bloodstone, used to call up demons, though this one was spent of its power. Suspecting the Syndicate's dealings ran deeper than banditry, he sought out other such amulets to learn the truth of their intentions.

## Refuge Pointe

When Stromgarde's capital fell in ruins during the Third War, most of its people fled south into the Arathi Highlands. But the remnants of the Stromgarde army would not go with them. They stayed, determined to reclaim what had been lost, and it was here, at Refuge Pointe, that they made their stand.

The outpost became the seat of the Alliance's war in the Highlands, its soldiers bent on retaking Stromgarde Keep and securing what resources they could for the Alliance's cause. That singleness of purpose left little room for anything else. When Thandol Span was struck hard by a Dark Iron assault and its dwarves sent word asking for aid to reclaim Dun Modr and the span itself, Captain Nials could not spare a soul. Stromgarde had fallen, she said, and until it was retaken every sword and every hand at Refuge Pointe belonged to that cause alone. Honor and duty bound them to the Highlands, she told the messenger, and Longbraid the Grim would have to manage without them.

## Stromgarde Keep

Stromgarde was once Strom, cradle of the first human kingdom, capital of the Arathorian Empire. When the Orcish Horde came to the Eastern Kingdoms, it still stood as a power among the Alliance of Lordaeron, its name unbowed even after the Second War.

The city was sacked after the Third War and left broken. It now lies split into three: the inner keep held by the Syndicate, the ruined mage district overrun by Boulderfist ogres aided by Witherbark trolls, and the last quarter still held by the Stromgarde military, from which Prince Galen Trollbane commands what remains of his people.

## Thandol Span

Two great stone arches span the gap between the Wetlands and the Arathi Highlands, raised by dwarven hands and guarded for three generations by the Anvilmar line. They were built after the War of the Three Hammers, when Ironforge sought to keep faith with its Wildhammer kin in Aerie Peak, and the bridges became both road and symbol between the two dwarven kingdoms, carrying trade that enriched them both.

The bridges were capable of bearing the weight of whole armies, and once served as the main road of trade between dwarves and humans. It was here that Anduin Lothar drove the last of the Old Horde out of Lordaeron and back into Khaz Modan, forcing them across the Span in defeat.

Now the Span stands scarred. A Dark Iron plot, laid by Kam Deepfury and Balgaras the Foul, has done heavy damage to the bridges, and dwarven forces fight to hold what remains and retake the nearby town of Dun Modr. Many dwarves have died in the fighting, among them Sully Balloo, Ebenezer Rustlocke, and the brother of Longbraid the Grim.

## The Drowned Reef

Beneath the bay off the southern shore of Arathi Highlands lies a sunken elven city, ancient and ruined. It is no longer empty: the Daggerspine naga have claimed it, ruled by Prince Nazjak.

The reef above has taken many ships, among them the Maiden's Folly and the Spirit of Silverpine. It is said the Daggerspine can turn the very tides of the bay, luring crews who think they have found safe harbor in Faldir's Cove onto the stones instead.

## The Tower of Arathor

The Tower of Arathor stands in the mage district of Stromgarde Keep, named for the old Empire of Arathor from which the kingdom took its lineage.

Archmage Trelane once studied within its walls, and there he built a defense woven into the tower itself, dormant until woken by an enchanted gem. When the Syndicate and the Boulderfist ogres closed in on Stromgarde, Trelane tried to hold the tower with his creation, but it was not enough. He fled the city, and the tower fell with it. The Boulderfist ogres took the district for their own, and the ogre mage Ruul Onestone came to dwell within its halls.

Trelane never stopped thinking of what he had left behind there -- his phylactery, his orb, an ember agate worked with his own hand. He turned to his former apprentice, Archmage Malin, to see them recovered from the ogres who now held the tower. Word came back to Malin that the old master's possessions had been found again, hidden away in chests within the halls he once called his own.

## Thoradin's Wall

King Thoradin raised this wall to hold back the Amani trolls, in the days when Strom stood young and Arathor had only just claimed the land for humanity. For generations it stood a stalwart sentinel, a line meant to shield mankind from the trolls' incursions.

Centuries on, the wall still stands, but heavily damaged, its stones broken and its long watch reduced to ruin.

## Witherbark Village

Witherbark Village sits around a small lake in the southeast of the Arathi Highlands, home to the trolls of the Witherbark tribe. Behind the village lies a large cavern where the tribe's leaders are most often found.

The Witherbark have made common cause with the Boulderfist ogres nearby, an alliance turned against Stromgarde and Hammerfall. Together, troll and ogre hold a section of Stromgarde itself, wrested from its former masters.

That strength has made the Witherbark a marked people, hunted by both the Alliance and the Horde, who wished to see them ended. Among those who stood against that reckoning, Nimar the Slayer was named the most dangerous, a fighter who in death took many of his enemies with him.
