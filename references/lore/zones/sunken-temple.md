# Sunken Temple

This is a dungeon or raid. Its bosses each have a file under `bosses/sunken-temple`.

## The Temple of Atal'Hakkar

More than a thousand years ago, the Gurubashi Empire tore itself apart in civil war. At its heart were the Atal'ai, troll priests who sought to bring the blood god Hakkar the Soulflayer back into the world. Defeated and exiled, they fled north into the Swamp of Sorrows and raised a temple there to prepare for his return.

The dragon Aspect Ysera learned of their plan and sank the temple beneath the marsh, meaning to bury their work forever. It did not happen that way. Undisturbed in the drowned dark, shielded from any eye that might have stopped them, the Atal'ai carried on their rituals in peace. The green dragons Ysera set to guard the ruins have kept watch ever since, holding closed a door that was never truly sealed.

Still the temple sits sunken and half-flooded, its altars to Hakkar unbroken, and those who enter risk worse than death. The Loa of Blood is owed souls as well as flesh, and every one it is given feeds the day it does not fail.
