# Mulgore

For hundreds of years the tauren had no land of their own, driven across Kalimdor by centaur raiders who scattered their tribes into wandering camps. When Thrall and his orcs landed on these shores, Cairne Bloodhoof looked upon them and saw kindred spirits, honorable and strong. With the orcs' escort and their strength at his back, Cairne led his people into the sheltered valley walled in by mountains on every side, and there his Bloodhoof tribe drove the centaur from the grasslands and claimed them at last.

Upon the mesa that rose above the plain, Cairne built Thunder Bluff, a refuge open to tauren of every tribe, and in time the scattered peoples united under his rule.

The pastures remain green, the mesas still cast their long shadows over Bloodhoof Village, and every tauren who comes of age must still walk into the wild plains to prove themselves in the Great Hunt, as their ancestors did.

## Bael'dun Digsite

The Bael'dun expedition cut its quarry into the western edge of Mulgore, tearing pickaxes into the mountainside. Their digging was named an affront to the Earth Mother, and when words failed to turn them back, the Bloodhoof tribe seized and broke the invaders' tools to drive them off.

The dwarves dug on regardless, heedless of the warnings given them, their reckless work stirring the anger of the spirits of the land. The quarry remains, a scar cut into Mulgore's earth.

## Bloodhoof Village

Bloodhoof Village sits at the waters of Stonebull Lake in the plains of Mulgore, named for the tribe that calls it home. Here the wild kodo beasts of the plains are broken and trained, until they stand calm enough to bear a rider, and the village is known for this work as much as for anything else.

Baine Bloodhoof keeps watch over the village, seeing to its protection. Zarlman Two-Moons tends to the spiritual life of the Bloodhoof tribe, the elder who speaks for the ancestors and the earth mother to those who live and pass through.

## Brambleblade Ravine

Brambleblade Ravine cuts into the northeastern edge of Red Cloud Mesa, home to the Bristleback quilboars. At the back of the ravine, in a large hut, dwells their chief, Sharptusk Thornmantle, whose leadership has drawn tauren attacks meant to break the quilboar raids at their source.

## Camp Narache

Camp Narache sits at the northern edge of the Red Cloud Mesa, a small circle of tents belonging to the Hawkwind Tribe, in southern Mulgore. Wounded braves rest in the main tent, and around the center of the village the tribe's teachers keep to a quiet circle, paying their respects to Greatmother Hawkwind.

## Kodo Rock

Kodo Rock rises quietly between Camp Narache and Brambleblade Ravine, easy to pass without noticing unless one is watching closely, for it can only be seen from close by.

## Palemane Rock

Palemane Rock is a cave cut deep into the mountainside of western Mulgore, home to the gnolls of the Palemane tribe. They keep both the cave and a camp outside it, and they are quick to violence, falling upon any who stray too near their ground.

## Ravaged Caravan

On the northeastern shore of Stonebull Lake smolders the wreck of a Venture Company caravan, still spewing black smoke into the sky over Mulgore. It had been carrying supplies to Supervisor Fizsprocket's mining operation when the tauren, weary of goblins moving through their land, fell upon it.

The survivors never abandoned the wreckage. They still hold the smoking mound and its scorched cargo, fighting off any who come near.

## Red Cloud Mesa

Red Cloud Mesa is a stretch of rolling plains atop the high cliffs of southern Mulgore, known for its quiet, open beauty. It is here that young tauren prove themselves, taking up arms against the Bristleback quilboar, who hold Brambleblade Ravine to the northeast.

Camp Narache, set in the northwest of the mesa, stands as the last safe ground on the mesa. From the graveyard west of the camp, a path climbs up to the top of Fargaze Mesa.

## Red Rocks

The red sandstone of Red Rocks rises in the northeast of Mulgore, a place where the world of Thunder Bluff falls away and something quieter takes its place. Here the tauren send their honored dead to the Earth Mother and Sky Father by cleansing flame. Not every fallen tauren is brought here; only the most valiant are given this rest, the warriors who founded and defended Thunder Bluff, those who gave their lives for their tribes and chieftains. It is also where young braves come to complete their passage into adulthood, standing among the stones to listen for the voices of their ancestors on the wind.

The sacredness of the place has not kept it safe. A band of Bristleback quilboar forced their way into Red Rocks and fouled the holy ground with their presence, an act that stirred deep anger among the tauren of Thunder Bluff who hold this place dear.

## Stonebull Lake

Stonebull Lake curls around Bloodhoof Village on three sides, a long horseshoe of cool water. Its waters run thick with fish, and lines are cast into it through many quiet hours. Where the lake narrows, a bridge spans the gap, carrying the road that links Bloodhoof Village to Thunder Bluff.

## The Golden Plains

The Golden Plains, a basin of shifting green grass southeast of Thunder Bluff, are treasured among the tauren homeland and prized as hunting ground. Great herds of kodo gather here to graze, and somewhere in the grass, it is said, the famous Arra'chea makes her home.

## The Rolling Plains

The Rolling Plains open out south of Bloodhoof Village, running toward the border of the Barrens. Wolves and cougars move through the grass alongside kodo and tallstriders.

Unlike the rest of Mulgore, the plains are not sealed off: centaur outriders have found a way through here, ranging where no other centaur tread. In the eastern reaches, a nest of Windfury harpies has taken hold.

## The Venture Co. Mine

The mine cuts deep into the eastern cliffs of Mulgore, three ways in: the main gate on foot, a cliff face, and a small lumber camp beyond it. It does not belong there. The tauren refused the Venture Company mining rights in their homeland, and the company simply began digging anyway, with Supervisor Fizsprocket set over the operation and Mogul Razdunk's advisory board pressing him to expand further still, into lumber, sending new shredders and raw materials for a mill to feed on Mulgore's timber.

The tauren did not let the intrusion pass. A caravan of mining supplies moving under guard along the northern shore of Stonebull Lake was found by the Outrunners, who fell on it and burned what they could, leaving the wreckage behind on the assumption that fire had ruined anything worth taking. It had not. Venture Co. salvagers were later seen picking through the ashes, gathering up what the flames had spared.

That discovery marked the mine for retaliation, a warning struck at Fizsprocket's operation for the company's refusal to leave tauren land alone.

## Thunderhorn Water Well

The well sits in the open grass of Mulgore, along the north road between Thunder Bluff and Bloodhoof Village. The Venture Company once seized it, hoping to choke off the tauren's water, but the tauren drove them out and reclaimed it.

## Wildmane Water Well

The Wildmane Water Well rises north of Thunder Bluff, northernmost of the tauren's sacred wells. Windfury harpies have taken the ground around it, and the well lies fouled, awaiting cleansing.

## Windfury Ridge

Windfury Ridge takes its name from the harpies nesting in its heights, fierce creatures that swoop upon any who stray too close, killing or carrying off the unwary. Cougars and wolves prowl the surrounding hills as well.

Yet the harpies' brilliant feathers are prized by the tauren of Thunder Bluff, who take them for weddings and feasts.

## Winterhoof Water Well

The Winterhoof Water Well lies at the edge of the Rolling Plains in southern Mulgore. Years after the Third War, the Venture Company seized it and poisoned its waters to choke off the tauren's supply. Mull Thunderhorn crafts totems to cleanse the taint, but the well is not yet pure.
