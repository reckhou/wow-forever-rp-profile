## Dalaran

Dalaran rose as a city of magi, founded after the Troll Wars by wizards of the Arathorian Empire, and became a nation unto itself. Its people answered to no crown but the Kirin Tor, that elite circle of the world's mightiest spellcasters, governed by the Council of Six. At its heart stood the Violet Citadel, sanctum and haven to those who studied the arcane, and no city outside the elven lands of Quel'Thalas held so great a concentration of magical power. Even the high elven mages of Quel'Thalas, in their pride, bowed to the sovereignty of the Kirin Tor.

That sovereignty was broken in fire. When the Scourge came against Dalaran, its people were driven from their own kingdom, and Archimonde himself brought the city to ruin, scattering the Kirin Tor's surviving members across Azeroth.

The survivors did not let their city stay dead. They returned to the wreckage and set about rebuilding it, raising a vast dome of magic to shelter its broken heart from further harm.
