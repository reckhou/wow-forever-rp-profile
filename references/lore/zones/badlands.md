# Badlands

The Badlands is a cracked desert of dry earth, scattered boulders, and winding canyons, hemmed in by mountains on every side. Little survives here but coyotes, cougars, and buzzards.

In the northern reaches lie the ruins of Uldaman, workings of the ancient earthen. When it was unearthed, it offered the first clues to the dwarves' lofty origins, and Ironforge prospectors flooded in to search the ruin for Titan artifacts. But the Stonevault troggs, roused after millennia of slumber, slaughtered the explorers and now defend their stolen city with savage purpose. A large Dark Iron fortress stands just south of Uldaman, its dwarves warring with the ogres of the Dustbelcher tribe and the drakes that nest among the black dragon spawn in the east.

Later, Kargath was founded here as the only Horde outpost in Khaz Modan, raised so orcs might watch their renegade Blackrock kin. Warchief Thrall himself sent the Kargath Expeditionary Force to hunt down Rend Blackhand.

## Agmond's End

The dig site in the southern central Badlands bears the name of the dwarf who died there. Agmond came to break stone and found the troggs that had already claimed the ground; they finished him where he stood, and the site was abandoned to them.

## Angor Fortress

The Dark Iron dwarves hold Angor Fortress as their seat in the Badlands, directing their digging toward Uldaman.

## Apocryphan's Rest

The bones of Apocryphan's Rest sprawl across the Badlands south of Kargath, a dragon's skeleton fallen and left to bleach in the dry heat. Buzzards nest and hunt among the ribs now, the only ones who claim the great carcass as home.

## Camp Boff

Camp Boff is the southeastern camp of the Dustbelcher ogres, one of four alongside Camp Cagg, Kosh, and Wurg. Few ogres guard it.

## Camp Cagg

Camp Cagg is one of the homes of the Dustbelcher ogres, sharing that name with three other camps scattered nearby.

## Camp Kosh

Camp Kosh sits at the northern edge of Dustbelcher territory, watching the barren roads that wind toward Kargath and down into the Searing Gorge. Caravans crossing the Badlands dread this stretch, for the ogres of Kosh fall upon them for whatever they carry.

## Camp Wurg

Camp Wurg is one of the homes of the Dustbelcher ogres, kin to the ogres of Camp Cagg, Camp Boff, and Camp Kosh.

## Dustbelch Grotto

In the southwestern Badlands rises a great mound of earth and stone, hollowed out and claimed by the Dustbelcher ogres. Dustbelch Grotto is their home, a warren of tunnels and chambers where the clan has made its den amid the cracked, sun-scorched land around it.

## Dustwind Gulch

The winds scour Dustwind Gulch until nothing soft is left standing, only stone and the ravine's dry bones. It lies west of Lethlor Ravine, a cut of barren ground in the Badlands where the dust never settles for long.

## Hammertoe's Digsite

Hammertoe's Digsite sits in the northern Badlands, its excavation trenches now silent of the miners who once worked them. Hammertoe himself has abandoned the site, and in his place a gang of Dark Irons has moved in, likely the same force that has pushed into Uldaman.

## Kargath

Kargath stands alone in Khaz Modan, the only outpost the Horde holds in that land, named for Kargath Bladefist, chieftain of the Shattered Hand clan.

On the order of Warchief Thrall himself, the Kargath Expeditionary Force rides out again and again into the harsh Badlands. Its true purpose is to cut a route through the Searing Gorge to Blackrock Mountain, so the Horde might strike at Rend Blackhand, Warchief of the Dark Horde, in his own stronghold. That road runs through ground the Dark Iron dwarves call theirs, and so the Horde has found itself at war with them again, not by plan but by the simple fact of the land it needed to cross.

Kargath's other worth is plainer to see: it is the hinge between north and south, the point where the road from Hammerfall and the Undercity meets the road down to Grom'Gol, Booty Bay, and Stonard.

## Lethlor Ravine

Lethlor Ravine cuts wide and craggy through the eastern Badlands, its floor haunted by whelps and drakes of the black dragonflight. Scattered through the ravine stand crystalline obelisks, strange and deliberate amid the rock and dragon-fire.

## Mirage Flats

The Mirage Flats lies east of Dustbelch Grotto in the Badlands. Little remains of it but a stretch of no-man's land, claimed only by giant buzzards, ridge stalker patriarchs, and rabid crag coyotes.

## The Dustbowl

The Dustbowl lies between Kargath and the valley of fangs, a stretch of cracked, arid ground in the northwest Badlands. Coyotes and buzzards range across it, and rock elementals stir among the stones. Little grows here but cactus and a few stubborn trees. Camp Wurg stands within this waste.

## The Maker's Terrace

Outside the entrance to Uldaman, the earth has been peeled back in wide, deliberate cuts, a scar of excavation left open to the sky. Dark Iron dwarves hold the Terrace now, watching the diggings and the road that leads down into the buried places below.

## Uldaman

Deep within the mountains of Khaz Modan lies Uldaman, a vast and ancient vault raised by the titan keepers in ages long past. Dwarves out of Ironforge once delved into it, cutting away at its buried chambers before the Third War, but the vault has since fallen to troggs and Dark Irons, who now dig through its stone for whatever earthly riches it still holds.

Uldaman remains rich with the leavings of that titan-forged age, and those willing to brave its depths still find artifacts buried within it, slowly uncovered by archaeologists and adventurers of the Explorers' League who continue to pick through the ruin.

## Valley of Fangs

The Valley of Fangs cuts narrow between two walls of stone in the Badlands, claimed by wolves and cougars whose teeth give the place its name.

Within it camps Martek the Exiled, cast out from wherever he once belonged, alongside Rigglefuzz and Jazzrik.
