## Zul'Farrak

Zul'Farrak was once a city of jungle, when Tanaris was still forest under the Gurubashi Empire. When the Great Sundering tore the ancient world apart, the jungle burned away into desert, and the Farraki trolls who had built their city here found themselves stranded amid the sand. They endured, hardening into the Sandfury, a tribe known even among trolls for its ruthlessness and its dark mysticism.

Within the sun-blasted walls, troll legend speaks of Sul'thraze the Lasher, a blade said to instill fear and weakness in any foe it touched. Long ago the sword was broken in two, and it is whispered that both halves still lie somewhere inside the city, lost among its courts and temples.

At the heart of Zul'Farrak lies a sacred pool, and beneath its still water the Sandfury are said to keep something far older than themselves sleeping - a demigod whose waking would bring ruin down upon the city and all within it.
