# Stonetalon Mountains

Stonetalon Peak has always belonged to the spirits before it belonged to any people. The night elves keep a barrow and a glade at its crown, communing with nature, and the tauren hold it sacred too, for it was here the immortal Oracle once watched the strands of fate as the Earth Mother wove them. Legend says the first tauren to settle these mountains carried flaming totems up to the highest crags, lighting bonfires to An'she to burn back the darkness clinging to the stone.

Lower on the mountain, Elune long ago blessed a fountain with the gift of health. Harpies later took the peaks nearby, capturing the wyverns that nested there and fouling the fountain's waters. Thrall came against them, with Cairne Bloodhoof and his tauren at his side, breaking a band of centaur to win a Glyph of Purification and cleanse what the harpies had corrupted. The freed wyverns carried them up to the Peak itself.

That old peace with the land is gone in the south. The Venture Company has stripped Windshear Crag bare, felling its trees, choking the air with soot, and fouling the water with oil and waste, until the mountain spirits cried out loud enough for shaman as far as Orgrimmar to hear it.

## Blackwolf River

The Blackwolf River flows from Cragpool Lake through Windshear Crag in the Stonetalon Mountains. Its waters run foul, poisoned by the Venture Company's deforestation of the valley.

## Boulderslide Cavern

At the end of Boulderslide Ravine, Boulderslide Cavern holds the nest of the Boulderslide kobolds. They mine a rare purple crystal called Resonite, touched with earthen magic, and guard every vein of it with their lives.

## Boulderslide Ravine

Boulderslide Ravine lies quiet along Webwinder Path, untouched by the wars troubling the lands around it. Rams pick their way along its slopes, and cliff stormers wheel overhead.

Opposite the ravine's cavern stands an abandoned cave, its barrels still stacked, its crude torches still standing though no one remains to light them. On its walls, tribal art endures, marked in a style that looks to be tauren work.

## Camp Aparaje

Camp Aparaje was a tauren village in the eastern Stonetalon Mountains, attacked by Grimtotem forces from Grimtotem Post under Grundig Darkcloud. Makaba Flathoof had lived there with his daughter Kaya before the attack.

## Cragpool Lake

Cragpool Lake sits beneath the great wooden structure the Venture Company raised over Windshear Crag. Gerenzo Wrenchwhistle watches over the operation from this spot.

The lake itself is off limits, claimed by Cragjaw, a fish grown monstrous in its still water.

## Greatwood Vale

The Greatwood Vale lies wooded in the foothills of the Stonetalon Mountains, reached by the Webwinder Path, with Malaka'jin to its south.

The vale's quiet broke when the Grimtotem tribe, under Grundig Darkcloud, fell upon Camp Aparaje and slaughtered nearly everyone there. Makaba Flathoof survived, and called for the Grimtotem in the vale to be hunted down in answer.

## Grimtotem Post

Grimtotem Post is the tribe's foothold in Stonetalon Mountains, its brutes, ruffians, sorcerers, and mercenaries patrolling the slopes around it. Grundig Darkcloud commands them here, holding the post for the Grimtotem's own purposes.

## Malaka'jin

Malaka'jin sits in the Stonetalon Mountains, south of the Grimtotem Post. It is a modest village, home to troll hunters who make their living selling their catches to those of the Horde who pass through, a foothold within sight of Grimtotem ground.

## Mirkfallon Lake

Mirkfallon Lake sits at the heart of Stonetalon Mountains, a wild basin thick with wyverns, slimes, and thunder lizards moving through its waters and shores. A hill rises from the lake's center, and above it, at Windshear Heights, the area's remaining wyverns still nest.

## Sishir Canyon

The canyon leads from the main road in the Stonetalon Mountains toward the Ruins of Eldre'thar, but no one passes through it easily. Sishir Canyon is given over entirely to the Deepmoss spiders, a monstrous brood whose eggs and webbing choke every ledge and hollow of the stone. Deep within, the greatest of them, Besseleth, waits among her kin.

## Stonetalon Peak

Stonetalon Peak lies in a green valley at the far northwestern reach of the mountains that share its name, tended by the druids of nearby Talon Den. The mountain is held sacred, and magic runs thick through its stone.

During the Third War, Jaina Proudmoore and her expedition came here and set a base near the entrance to the caverns beneath the peak. Thrall and his Horde followed after, seeking the Oracle.

It has since become a hub for the night elves and the Alliance, its druids keeping watch over the valley.

## Sun Rock Retreat

Sun Rock Retreat sits hidden among the peaks south of Mirkfallon Lake in the Stonetalon Mountains, a place where the spirit world presses close against the living one. Long before the Horde raised its banners here, tauren made pilgrimage to this stone to perform their rites of crossing into the spirit realm. It is said that An'she, the sun, once kissed the mountain where the retreat now stands.

Now orcs and tauren hold Sun Rock Retreat together, the busiest gathering of Horde strength in these mountains, a hub of trade and labor, mending what the Venture Company and other malignant forces have torn from the land.

## The Charred Vale

The Charred Vale was once green, a valley in the southwest of Stonetalon where Bloodfury harpies made their nests and treants walked the forest as its guardians.

Then the goblins of the Venture Company came to strip the vale for its wood and ore. The treants rose against them and drove the machines back, but the vale's spirits would not be calmed. The digging had woken something beneath the earth, and fire and elementals poured up through the broken ground where the forest had stood. The treants who turned the goblins away did not come back whole; whatever the goblins' weapons touched them with, it stayed, and they are counted among the vale's dangers now rather than its guardians.

What remains is a valley of flame and lava, black trees standing in ash where green ones stood before. The druids of Sun Rock Retreat have taken up the work the treants once did, coaxing new growth from the burned ground in the hope that the vale will be a forest again rather than a wound in the mountains.

## The Talon Den

High on Stonetalon Peak, the earth opens into the Talon Den, a barrow home to the Mirkfallon dryads and the keepers who share their name, watched over by night elf Cenarion Caretakers.

## The Talondeep Path

The Talondeep Path is a winding tunnel joining Ashenvale to the Stonetalon Mountains, its passage uneven with small dead ends and strange hollows worn into the rock. It opens west of Mystral Lake on one end, and in the northwestern reach of Windshear Crag on the other. Nothing dwells there; it is simply a way through the mountains.

## Webwinder Path

The Webwinder Path runs between Malaka'jin and Mirkfallon Lake, through land that has sunk low on either side. West of Malaka'jin, the Horde built a barricade across the road to hold the Alliance from pressing further east.

## Windshear Crag

Windshear Crag was once old-growth forest, thick and green in its valley among the Stonetalon Mountains. It is not that now. What stands here is stumps, fouled water, and machinery that never quite stops creaking.

The ruin has a name behind it: Gerenzo Wrenchwhistle, a leper gnome given the whole logging expedition to run under the Venture Company's banner. He built the Super Reaper 6000, a machine that can reduce the crag's oldest trees to sawdust, and he watches his work from a platform raised above Cragpool Lake, lord of everything the machine has cleared. His mastery of the place has made him expendable to those above him: Director Riddlevox, of the Undermine's Tinkers' Union, wants him dead, and the goblin Ziz Fizziks lurks in the crag, waiting to see the order carried out.

In the southeast corner of the crag, Windshear kobolds have taken the Windshear Mine for their own, tunneling after smoky iron ore.

## Windshear Mine

Windshear Mine lies at the end of Blackwolf River, within Windshear Crag. The Venture Company once worked its tin seams here, setting kobolds to the digging.
