# Silithus

The desert lies at the far southwestern edge of Kalimdor, a waste of broken rock and sand reached by the wide path out of Un'Goro. Beneath its surface lies the remnant of the Aqiri empire, Ahn'Qiraj, whose central temple the night elves sealed away in an effort to keep something terrible from waking. The ruins scattered across Silithus, and the silithid hives now riddling the sand, are proof enough that the sealing did not hold.

Long ago the Qiraji themselves were driven behind the Scarab Wall, imprisoned there in the aftermath of the War of the Shifting Sands. Their servants, the silithid, were left free, and over the centuries they multiplied and spread across all of southern Kalimdor until the night elves judged the threat too great to watch any longer, and turned instead to reopening the Gates of Ahn'Qiraj to finish what the Wall had only delayed.

The Cenarion Circle keeps its watch from Cenarion Hold, tracking the silithid swarms and weighing whether they are a danger too great for any one people to face alone.

## Bones of Grakkarond

In central Silithus lie the huge bones of the dragon Grakkarond, who fell there in the War of the Shifting Sands.

## Bronzebeard Encampment

The Bronzebeard Encampment stands in southern Silithus, east of the Scarab Wall.

## Cenarion Hold

Cenarion Hold rose in Silithus after the War of the Shifting Sands, built by the Cenarion Circle atop an ancient night elven settlement. It stands as one of the great druid strongholds of Kalimdor, rivaled in size only by Moonglade itself, and its purpose is singular: to hold the line against the silithid swarms and stand watch over the buried thing beneath the sands, the imprisoned Old God C'Thun, lest its armies rise again.

It is a hard, embattled place to live. The silithid press constantly against its walls, and the region around it grows ever more hostile. Even so, the merchants of the Hold keep their stalls open, trading with whoever makes the long climb up from Un'Goro Crater, for trade is scarce that far from anywhere else, and every coin matters.

The path to it winds northwest out of Un'Goro, past the Mossy Pile, climbing a steep cliff face that switches back on itself before opening onto the burning wastes of Silithus.

## Hive'Ashi

Hive'Ashi lies in northern Silithus, its silithid brood spreading out to infest the Swarming Pillar beyond its borders.

## Hive'Regal

In southern Silithus, the mounds of Hive'Regal rise from the sand, home to the silithid, tunnels and chitin thick amid the dust.

## Hive'Zora

Hive'Zora is a silithid nest burrowed into the sands of western Silithus.

## Ortell's Hideout

In southeastern Silithus, a small cave opens onto the slopes, home to a hermit named Ortell.

## Ravaged Twilight Camp

In the Crystal Vale, northwestern Silithus, stands a Twilight's Hammer camp, claimed by Highlord Demitrian.

Here Demitrian remains, holding this ground for the cult.

## Southwind Village

Southwind Village once stood as a night elven outpost in Silithus. It did not survive the War of the Shifting Sands.

When the qiraji rose in their swarming multitudes, Valstann Staghelm, son of Fandral Staghelm, led a detachment of the night elven army to hold the village. His troops were slaughtered where they stood, and Valstann himself was taken alive by General Rajaxx while the village he had been sent to defend was torn apart behind him.

What the qiraji left behind has not stayed empty. The spirits of druids and Sentinels who fell in the slaughter linger among the wreckage, wandering without purpose, bound to the place of their deaths, following the Shade of Ambermoon. Southwind Village stands on not as quiet ruin but as haunted ground, its dead still unfinished with it.

## Staghelm Point

A night elven tower once crowned a peak in northeastern Silithus. Here Ralo'shan the Eternal Watcher keeps her post, a bronze drake of Nozdormu's brood wheeling in the sky above her. Down the road from the tower lies Twilight's Run, a cave sheltering Twilight cultists, with a small camp at its mouth, a threat the watcher must contend with.

## The Crystal Vale

In the far northwestern corner of Silithus lies the Crystal Vale, a valley given over to earth and air elementals. Thorium runs thick beneath its stone, but the ground belongs first to the spirits that stir there, restless and old. Highlord Demitrian holds a camp within the vale.

It is here that Thunderaan, the Windseeker, can be called forth and bound anew into the blade that bears his fury, a working of storm and will that few dare attempt and fewer still complete.

## The Scarab Dais

The Scarab Gong stands at the Scarab Dais, northwest of the Scarab Wall outside Ahn'Qiraj, in southern Silithus.

## The Scarab Wall

The Scarab Wall runs along the southern edge of Silithus, sealing off the desert from the ruined city of Ahn'Qiraj beyond it. It was raised from the sand itself at the end of the War of the Shifting Sands, its sandstone bulk climbing at least a hundred feet into the sky. Above that height, where stone could rise no further, the bronze dragonflight wove their magic into a barrier many times taller still.

Those who made it, night elves and bronze dragons together, named it Irana'ethil, Shield against the Shadow. The name says plainly what it was for: C'Thun and its silithid broods lay sealed within Ahn'Qiraj, and the wall stood between that horror and the rest of the world.

## The Swarming Pillar

The Swarming Pillar rises south of Cenarion Hold, a peak riddled through with silithid tunnels, its slopes never still. A haze of small silithid circles the stone at all hours, a living cloud that gave the pillar its name, and beneath the sand at its foot lies one of the hive's chambers, the largest of the many such nests honeycombing the ground of Silithus.

## Twilight Base Camp

Twilight Base Camp stands as one of four camps the Twilight's Hammer cult has raised across the wastes of Silithus, a foothold for those who serve the coming of the Old Gods amid the dust and ruin of that scoured land.

## Twilight Outpost

The Twilight Outpost stands in the far southwestern corner of Silithus, near the Scarab Wall, west of the Bronzebeard Encampment. It is held by the Twilight's Hammer cult.

## Twilight Post

Twilight Post stands in the northwest reaches of Silithus, west of Cenarion Hold and northwest of Twilight Base Camp, a foothold claimed by the Twilight's Hammer in that scoured, silt-blown land.

## Twilight's Run

Twilight's Run lies hidden in the rock at the base of Staghelm Point, in northern Silithus. Its cave mouth gives no hint of what fills the dark beyond: chamber after chamber given over to the Twilight's Hammer Cult, who have made this hollow in the mountain their own, deep and unseen, while the desert wind scours the stone above them.

## Valor's Rest

Valor's Rest is a small camp at the edge of Silithus, reached by the path climbing from the northwest corner of Un'Goro Crater -- the first ground a traveler crosses coming up into the sands.

The camp is tended by members of the Cenarion Circle, who share the ground with an insect infestation that feels unsettlingly familiar. Alliance and Horde alike shelter here in passing, and graves stand quiet at its edge.
