# Darnassus

The night elves built Darnassus after the Third War, when their long immortality had failed them. Many could not bear the sudden weight of aging and frailty. A number of grieving druids resolved to plant a new World Tree to restore their bond with the eternal world. With Malfurion Stormrage gone missing, Fandral Staghelm took up the mantle of Archdruid and led the effort. The tree they raised, Teldrassil, rose off the coast until its crown broke above the clouds, and among its boughs the night elves founded their city as a testament to their people's survival.

Darnassus stands open to the sky, unlike the close, walled cities of the humans and dwarves. It is built around a broad lake, its shores joined by graceful bridges, its pathways carpeted in fallen leaves. The Temple of the Moon rises above the trees as the seat of High Priestess Tyrande Whisperwind and her Sisters of Elune, beside the colonnaded Hall of Justice where the Sentinels keep their watch. The city remains what it was built to be: a tranquil refuge, and a monument to a people learning to live with mortality.

## Cenarion Enclave

The Cenarion Enclave lies in the northwest of Darnassus, a circular grove where oaks and ashes stand taller than the halls of stone elsewhere in the city. Every tree here bears runes shaped not carved but grown from the living bark, and the gathering-places of the grove are themselves formed from wood and stone worked together, as though raised rather than built.

This has always been the seat of the city's druids, just as the Temple of the Moon is the seat of its priests. Archdruid Fandral Staghelm leads them from the boughs of the Enclave, and the largest of its living structures, standing at the heart of the grove, is his own residence.

The grove still serves the order that gathers beneath it: druids come here to be taught the old ways, hunters train in its easternmost tree, and at the foot of the great Enclave tree one may learn to ride the nightsabers that carry Darnassus's riders through the wilds. Below the ground, hidden within the westernmost bough, a secretive guild of rogues keeps its quarters, known only to those who already know where to look.

## Craftsmen's Terrace

The Craftsmen's Terrace lies in the north of Darnassus, given over to trade rather than war or worship. Here the city's artisans keep their shops, and weary travelers may shelter in one of Darnassus's inns. An embassy of the Argent Dawn also stands here, a foreign presence granted a place within the elven city.

The terrace borders the Warrior's Terrace to the south, and the Cenarion Enclave to the west.

## The Temple Gardens

At the heart of Darnassus lie the Temple Gardens, a wide expanse of water, walkways, and small islands taking their name from the great Temple of the Moon that rises just to the south. Bridges span outward in every direction, joining the Cenarion Enclave to the north, the Craftsmen's, Warrior's, and Tradesmen's Terraces to the east, and the Temple of the Moon to the south, so that all roads through the city seem to gather here.

The largest island holds the city's bank, and a portal at the western edge of the gardens offers passage down to Rut'theran Village at the base of the World Tree, making this quiet center of water and stone also the threshold between Darnassus and the world beyond.

## Tradesmen's Terrace

The Tradesmen's Terrace sits in the southern reaches of Darnassus, where shopkeepers keep their stalls and traders gather to bargain, making it the heart of commerce for the whole city. From the north it opens onto the Warrior's Terrace, and to the west the paths lead toward the Temple of the Moon and the Temple Gardens.

Its buildings are grown of wood, in the manner of the night elves, light and graceful things.

## Warrior's Terrace

The Warrior's Terrace holds the eastern gate of Darnassus, where the city opens onto the wilds of Teldrassil. Here the Sentinels keep their ground, and any night elf who would take up arms in service of the city comes to this terrace to do it, working the target ranges and dueling grounds until their skill is fit for war.

Riders on nightsabers keep watch along the gates, and the terrace stands as the city's shield against whatever violence might come up the road from the forest.

It is bordered by the Craftsmen's Terrace to the north, the Tradesmen's Terrace to the south, and the Temple Gardens to the west, so that a warrior training here stands within call of the city's artisans, its merchants, and its priests alike.
