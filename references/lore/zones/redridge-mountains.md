# Redridge Mountains

The Redridge Mountains lie east of Elwynn Forest, northeast of Duskwood, and south of the Burning Steppes.

An idyllic region of rushing rivers, towering elms, and rising elevation, Redridge stands under Stormwind's protection, though since the Second War it has been technically independent. It remains one of the last peaceful regions of Azeroth, its people calm and content, supplying Stormwind with timber, fish, and crops. Yet the peace is imperfect: a force of Blackrock orcs from the Burning Steppes holds Stonewatch Keep, though so far they keep to themselves. Compared to the ruin of Westfall, Redridge still holds together.

On the northern bank of Lake Everstill stands the town of Lakeshire, besieged by gnolls and Blackrock orcs out of the surrounding hills. To its south, the crossroads known as the Three Corners links Lakeshire to Elwynn and Duskwood, and by the Lakeridge Highway to Stonewatch Keep, a road held by dragon whelps, gnolls, murlocs, and wild beasts.

The lake's surface looks calm enough, but murlocs lurk beneath it, and rumors persist of a sea monster in its depths.

## Alther's Mill

The mill northeast of Lakeshire stands empty, its wheel long stilled. It was little more than a waypoint on the road to Stonewatch Keep, its ruins home to spiders, buzzards, and the odd stray orc.

## Galardell Valley

The Galardell Valley, in the northeastern Redridge Mountains, swarms with gnolls under Lieutenant Fangore, whose howl calls the packs down on any who face him unprepared.

Above the valley stands the Tower of Ilgalar, dwelling place of the evil Morganth, whose name alone marks the place as one to fear.

## Lake Everstill

Lake Everstill lies at the heart of the Redridge Mountains, its waters wide and blue enough to give the sleepy town of Lakeshire its whole reason for being. The lake drains eastward, its river running on toward the dark stones of Stonewatch Keep.

It was once a fisherman's lake, calm and dependable, but that has changed. Tribes of murlocs have overrun the shallows and depths alike, and somewhere in the deep water a thresher still patrols, old and vast enough that even seasoned hunters of the murloc tribes give it a wide berth. The Everstill Bridge still carries travelers across the water, above the murloc camps and the thing that watches from below.

## Lakeridge Highway

Lakeridge Highway once carried steady traffic along the southern shore of Lake Everstill, before orcs seized Stonewatch Keep and the road fell out of use. Gnolls now range along most of its length, and camouflaged Blackrock hunters lie in wait further down. Travel along it is not something anyone would advise.

## Lakeshire

Lake Everstill lies still and cold beneath the Redridge peaks, and the town on its shore has bled for that ground more than once. When Warchief Blackhand first led orcs into these mountains, Sir Anduin Lothar and the Knights of Stormwind ambushed them on the slopes and nearly broke them, until two Shadow Council warlocks turned the battle and forced Lothar back. Blackhand returned weeks later with a greater host and took Lakeshire outright. Only after the Second War did the Alliance of Lordaeron reclaim it, Supreme Commander Turalyon overseeing the return of its scattered people.

## Redridge Canyons

The canyons cut through the northwestern reaches of Redridge, their walls hiding the den of Yowler, who leads the gnolls that hold this ground.

## Render's Camp

The Blackrock orcs hold a camp in the northwestern Redridge Mountains, close by the way into the Burning Steppes. From here they keep watch over the pass, a foothold of the Blackrock clan pressed against the mountains that lead to their master's domain.

## Render's Rock

Render's Rock is a wide cave in the northwestern Redridge Mountains, near the Burning Steppes. Blackrock orcs hold it in force.

Here Corporal Keeshan lies wounded, cut off, needing to reach Lakeshire alive.

## Render's Valley

Render's Valley lies in the southeastern Redridge Mountains, a Blackrock orc outpost grown into a stronghold, ringed with towers and held by elite warriors.

## Rethban Caverns

The Rethban Caverns cut deep into the northwestern Redridge Mountains. Gnolls hold the tunnels, led by the cunning Ardo Dirtpaw, hoarding Rethban ore wrested from the stone. The caverns may take their name from a man called Bart Rethban, though little else is remembered of him.

## Stonewatch

Stonewatch once belonged to the humans of Lakeshire, a stronghold set against the cliffs where the falls cascade down below the keep, watching over Redridge against the orcish menace of the Burning Steppes.

That watch was broken by betrayal. The warlock Morganth struck an alliance with Gath'Ilzogg and his Blackrock orcs, and together their combined force fell upon Stonewatch Keep with a savagery that broke the garrison. The orcs took the keep, but their triumph was short-lived in trust, for Morganth turned on the allies who had won it for him. He raised the Tower of Ilgalar for himself and bent a warband of Shadowhide gnolls, under Lieutenant Fangore, to his own purposes. Since that turning, both orc and man who cross the gnolls' path have paid for it.

Abandoned by the warlock who had used them, the Blackrock orcs held the keep on their own, Gath'Ilzogg ruling it with his lieutenant Tharil'zun at his side.

## Stonewatch Falls

Below Stonewatch, the eastern river of Lake Everstill drops in a long fall down the hillside, gathering into a deep lake in the nook of the Redridge Mountains. A small tribe of murlocs has settled its banks, fishing the water and raising crude shelters beneath the falls.

## Stonewatch Keep

Stonewatch Keep once belonged to the humans of Lakeshire, set to watch over Redridge. It fell when the warlock Morganth struck an alliance with the Blackrock orc warlord Gath'Ilzogg, and together their combined forces threw themselves at the keep with a savagery fed by Morganth's dark magic. The defenders broke, and the keep was taken.

The alliance did not last. No sooner had Stonewatch fallen than Morganth turned on the orcs who had bled for it beside him. He raised the Tower of Ilgalar nearby and filled it with Shadowhide gnolls under Lieutenant Fangore, creatures bent to his will alone. Orc and man alike have since fallen to the brutes he set loose.

Abandoned by their warlock ally, the Blackrock orcs held the keep on their own, Gath'Ilzogg and his lieutenant Tharil'zun ruling what remained of it. During the battle they had taken Corporal Keeshan of the Stormwind Army prisoner and dragged him off north to their camps. Magistrate Solomon set a bounty on Gath'Ilzogg's head for it, and Marshal Marris of Lakeshire swore Tharil'zun would answer for his crimes as well.

## Stonewatch Tower

Stonewatch Tower once stood beside Stonewatch Keep, both held by the humans of Lakeshire, until the Blackrock clan seized the stronghold. The tower serves as a scout post under Gath'Ilzogg's underlings, orcs of the Blackrock clan watching over the stronghold their kin took from Lakeshire's men.

## Three Corners

At Three Corners the road forks three ways: west to Goldshire in Elwynn Forest, east to Lakeshire, south to Darkshire in Duskwood. Standing here, a traveler stands at the seam of three lands, with only the crossroads to mark the passage between them. Gnolls press in from the hills nearby.

## Tower of Ilgalar

The Tower of Ilgalar rises in the eastern Redridge Mountains. Morganth rules it, a sorcerer exiled from Stormwind's Mage Quarter for his dark studies, who took the tower as refuge and bent the Shadowhide gnoll clan to his will.
