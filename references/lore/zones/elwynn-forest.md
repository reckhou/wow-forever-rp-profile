# Elwynn Forest

When the orcs came in the First War, thousands marched through Elwynn to surround Stormwind, cutting it off from everything but the sea. Knight Champion Anduin Lothar led the Knights of Stormwind in a charge through the forest itself, striking the orcish rearguard by surprise and scattering them. It bought the city time, but not enough. Warchief Blackhand soon took Goldshire and Moonbrook, the last towns standing between the Horde and the king, and Stormwind Keep was sacked. Lothar led the survivors to the sea and away to Lordaeron, and the ruins of the city belonged for a time to the Stormreaver clan. Scattered orcish catapults still lie broken and rotting across the region, the only reminders left of that occupation.

Northshire Vineyards, east of the city, were once the pride of Stormwind's farmers. When the Stonemasons Guild went unpaid for the years they spent rebuilding the ruined capital, and the nobles disbanded them rather than settle the debt, Guildmaster Edwin VanCleef's fury spread into riots that killed Queen Tiffin Ellerian Wrynn and birthed the Defias Brotherhood. The Brotherhood drove the farmers from Northshire and occupied its fields.

## Brackwell Pumpkin Patch

In southeast Elwynn Forest stands a farmstead that once belonged to the Brackwell family. What became of them, no one can say. The Defias Brotherhood took the place for their own, and the Brackwells simply vanished from its fields and fences.

Of them, only one memory lingers: a prize sow named Princess, grown enormous on vegetables she stole by creeping over to the neighboring Stonefield Farm night after night, long before the bandits ever came.

## Crystal Lake

Crystal Lake lies east of Goldshire, its waters thick with murlocs that make the shore a poor place for the unwary. A human homestead watches over the water from the southern shore.

## Eastvale Logging Camp

The mill camps of Eastvale stand scattered through eastern Elwynn, their living quarters strung between the sawpits, and the work never truly stops. Orders for lumber come in faster than the deadlines allow, and the lumberjacks labor under a constant press of buyers waiting on Stormwind's timber.

Marshal Haggard, who once led the Stormwind Guard, retired from that command a few years back and chose to spend his remaining years in a manor within the camp rather than in the city he once defended. Ceya Northbrooke was raised here as well.

The forest presses close on every side, and the wildness of it is no small thing. Wolves and bears have harried the loggers, scattering work crews and threatening to bury the camp's deadlines entirely. The trouble grew severe enough that the Stormwind Guard was forced to call for outside help in culling the beasts.

## Echo Ridge Mine

Echo Ridge Mine's tunnels lie in northwestern Northshire Valley. Kobolds swarmed the shafts and made them their own, much to the frustration of the humans living nearby, and Marshal McBride could do no more than send men to cull their numbers again and again.

## Fargodeep Mine

Fargodeep Mine was once worked for Stormwind gold, its shafts sunk deep into the earth of Elwynn Forest. It stands abandoned now, its tunnels claimed by a clan of kobolds. Lower Mines, the deeper reaches beneath the main works, run further still into the dark.

Marshal Dughan, watching over Elwynn's safety, saw the surrounding patrols increased once the kobold presence within was confirmed. Among the kobolds skulks Goldtooth, who holds a necklace stolen from Auntie Bernice Stonefield, a keepsake she has not given up hope of seeing again.

## Forest's Edge

Forest's Edge lies southwest of Stormwind, a tongue of woodland between Mirror Lake Orchard, the Nazferiti river, and a smaller stream toward Westfall. Boars root through its underbrush, and copper veins run along the banks. The forest belongs to a band of gnolls, and among them stands one who is no mere pack beast. Hogger ranges the woods among the lesser gnolls, no common cur among them.

## Goldshire

Goldshire sits in the heart of Elwynn Forest, its fields within sight of Northshire, its roads reaching toward Stormwind, Westfall, and Redridge. It grew up in the years after Stormwind's founding, when the kingdom's people spread from the capital into the surrounding country.

The town's worst hour came during the First War, when Warchief Blackhand turned the Horde against the lifeline running between Stormwind Keep and its people. Goldshire and Moonbrook were the last two towns standing between the orcs and the capital, and when they fell, the road to the Keep lay open. Goldshire did not rise again until after the Second War, when the Alliance of Lordaeron rebuilt it, Supreme Commander Turalyon himself seeing to the return of Stormwind's scattered citizens.

What remains now is quieter: the Lion's Pride Inn stands at the center of town, its hearth and ale drawing travelers from every road that meets here. Marshal Dughan of the Stormwind Guard keeps watch over the town still, mindful of what it once cost to lose it.

## Heroes' Vigil

A ring of standing stones circles the small island in Stone Cairn Lake, centered on the Stone of Remembrance. It honors the dead of the First War, when orcs sacked Goldshire and the surrounding lands and put them to fire.

Rogue wizards hold the island now. Among the stones lies the body of Henze Faulk, left where he fell.

## Jasperlode Mine

Jasperlode Mine once fed Stormwind's forges, one of the sites in Elwynn Forest that supplied the raw ore the city needed for its wars. That work is done now. Kobolds have overrun the tunnels, and Stormwind's army, stretched thin by graver threats, has no men to spare to root them out.

The kobolds are not the only danger below. In the deepest recesses of the mine a colony of mine spiders has taken hold, and to them the kobolds are not invaders to fear but prey to hunt. Their queen, Mother Fang, broods in the dark and suffers no intrusion into her nest.

## Jerod's Landing

Jerod's Landing is a boat dock on the Elwynn Forest bank of the Nazferiti river. The Defias Brotherhood holds it, and the Dockmaster and his men use it to smuggle stolen goods from the Redridge Mountains to Klaven's Tower in Westfall.

## Mirror Lake

Mirror Lake lies small and quiet in western Elwynn Forest, its waterfall spilling bright beside a nearby orchard, the kind of place that looks made for swimming or fishing. It is not so simple anymore. Defias bandits have taken to the woods around it, and what should be a place of rest has become one to cross carefully.

## Mirror Lake Orchard

Mirror Lake Orchard lies on the southern banks of Mirror Lake, its rows of trees and a lone cottage standing among the fields of western Elwynn Forest. Defias bandits once held the orchard and the cottage beside it, using the place as their own, and whether the orchard's true owners were driven off by them, no one can say for certain.

## Northshire Abbey

Northshire Abbey rests in the valley that bears its name, in the foothills of Elwynn Forest, its main hall, hall of arms, and library wing standing where clerics from the Church of the Holy Light first settled after venturing south from Lordaeron. From here the Holy Order of Northshire Clerics gave Stormwind its comfort and its wisdom, and it was within these walls that Medivh, the Guardian of Tirisfal, was tended through the long years of his coma.

The abbey did not survive the First War unscathed. Its Abbot lent the Clerics of Northshire to Stormwind's defense, and not long after the half-orc Garona was brought here as a captive, the orcs overran Northshire and burned it to ruin. Refugees fled toward Lordaeron, but not before pulling sacred tomes from the wreckage, texts that would later teach the mages of Dalaran the art of invisibility.

The ruined grounds did not stay empty. Cho'gall's Twilight's Hammer clan made the site its headquarters during the Second War, until the Alliance retook Elwynn Forest and Archbishop Alonsus Faol rebuilt the abbey stone by stone, founding the Brotherhood of Northshire in its halls.

## Northshire Valley

Northshire Valley lies closed in on every side but one, its mountains impenetrable, its only pass to the south sealed behind thick stone walls and a watched gate. A river cuts through the quiet of it, winding down toward Crystal Lake, past the abbey and the vineyard rows that give the place its air of peace.

That peace was not always so certain. During the Second War, Northshire Abbey stood as the headquarters of Cho'gall's Twilight's Hammer clan, sworn to Gul'dan's cause, and its walls sheltered orcish war-councils rather than novices and farmers.

Now Marshal McBride commands the abbey's garrison and answers for the whole of Northshire's safety, though the valley is not wholly settled even under his watch. Wolves press in from Duskwood in numbers no one can account for, kobolds burrow through Echo Ridge Mine.

## Northshire Vineyards

The vineyards stretch out east of the abbey, once tended for their harvest before the Defias Brotherhood claimed the land. Garrick Padfoot led the agents who held it, using the rows as a foothold for the Brotherhood's minions, who took the stolen property of Milly Osworth with them.

## Ridgepoint Tower

Ridgepoint Tower watches the border of Elwynn Forest, one of the Stormwind Guard's scout towers. Its sentries turn their eyes toward Redridge, where gnolls and orcs gather, toward Duskwood, where the dead do not stay buried, and toward the nearby Defias camps. The garrison holds well defended against all three.

## Stone Cairn Lake

Stone Cairn Lake takes its name from Heroes' Vigil, the cairn raised on the island at its heart to remember the dead of the First War against the orcs, when so many fell that no single grave could hold their memory. It was once a place of quiet pilgrimage.

That peace is gone. Rogue Wizards have taken the island, and the cairn meant for mourning now stands behind them. Along the southern banks, a tribe of murlocs has raised its own crude dwellings, indifferent to the monument across the water.

## The Maclure Vineyards

The Maclure Vineyards lie in southern Elwynn Forest, tended by the Maclure family across long rows of vine and soil. The Maclures hold a bitter rivalry with the neighboring Stonefield family, trading dislike as freely as they trade nothing else. Yet a child of the Maclures and a child of the Stonefields have fallen for one another, in defiance of everything their families stand for, and some hope such a love might mend what generations of bad blood could not.

## The Stonefield Farm

Stonefield Farm lies in southern Elwynn Forest, worked by the Stonefield family, who share a bitter rivalry with the nearby Maclures. Yet a child of each house has fallen in love, defying the old grudge between their families. Some hope that romance might succeed where years of quarreling never could, and mend what the feud has broken.

## Thunder Falls

The path up from Mirror Lake's waterfall climbs steep and narrow into the mountains before opening onto Thunder Falls, a ravine unlike others in Elwynn Forest for having falling water at both ends. A third waterfall tumbles down from the northern cliffs, feeding the ravine from above. Below the rise stands a small rural cottage, its anvil and fire kept lit, with a little pier and boat resting on the river beside the house. Bandits linger near the cottage. The river spills onward through a second waterfall near the edge of Westfall. The violet perch that swim in these waters are said to be found nowhere as readily as here.

## Tower of Azora

The Tower of Azora rises amid the fields of central Elwynn Forest, home to a company of gnomish servants who answer to the Archmage Theocritus.

Far off in the Redridge Mountains stands its rival, the Tower of Ilgalar, ruled by the sorcerer Morganth. The two towers have never made war outright, but they watch each other constantly through magical means, each waiting to catch the other in some misstep.

The tower's own magic spills into the fields around it, and anything that lingers too near is liable to be touched by it, turned into something stranger than it was. The servants tend their tower against this small, constant trouble, one eye always turned toward Redridge.

## Westbrook Garrison

Westbrook Garrison stands at the western edge of Elwynn Forest, where the trees give way to the poorer country of Westfall, and it has always been a lonely posting for the Stormwind Guard.

Its hardest years came while Lady Katrana Prestor's corruption sat heavy over the kingdom and the bulk of the Stormwind Army was committed elsewhere. With too few soldiers to spare, gnolls and thieves out of Westfall pressed harder on the woods each season, and the garrison barely kept the roads safe.

Among the gnolls, the Riverpaw chieftain Hogger became a name of dread. He overpowered every attempt made to capture him, and wanted postings still hang at the garrison gate naming him, unfulfilled.
