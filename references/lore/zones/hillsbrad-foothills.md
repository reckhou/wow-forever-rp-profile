# Hillsbrad Foothills

Hillsbrad's soil is rich and its hills green, and travelers say it is what all of Lordaeron looked like before the Plague came. That is its cruelty as much as its comfort: the land remembers what was lost everywhere else, while the people who hold it fight the same war that ruined the rest.

At the outset of the Second War, orcish raiders struck its coastal towns, and Orgrim Doomhammer himself rode against Durnholde Keep to free Zul'jin from its cells. Decades later it was Thrall who came through these hills, gathering the scattered orcish clans before slipping into Southshore by night to steal its ships and carry the new Horde across the sea to Kalimdor.

What the orcs began, the Forsaken have continued. They claimed Tarren Mill as their southernmost holding and turned it into a staging ground against the humans of Southshore and Hillsbrad Fields, who held fast for the Alliance.

Pine woods dot the farmland now, and roads still cross the pastures toward the gloomy Forsaken settlements to the north, as if the land itself were still waiting to see which side keeps it.

## Azurelode Mine

Azurelode Mine lies in the southwestern reach of Hillsbrad Foothills, its hill once counted part of Gilneas before the Greymane Wall rose and cut the kingdom off from the world.

The mine belongs to the Alliance, worked by miners under watch of guards, its iron feeding the Alliance's armories.

## Chillwind Point

Chillwind Point takes its name from the chill wind pouring down from the Western Plaguelands, funneling through the ravine below. The rise is crowned by an ancient troll ruin, all that remains of those who raised it. The pass once belonged to the kingdom of Lordaeron. The jungle troll Bath'rah the Windwatcher keeps to the ruin now.

## Corrahn's Dagger

Corrahn's Dagger is the second of the four fingers of Hillsbrad. The Syndicate holds this hill.

## Crushridge Hold

Crushridge Hold rises near the Ruins of Alterac, a cave hollowed into an earthen mound. Around it the Crushridge ogres have raised their encampments, thick in number and brutish in temper, holding the slopes above the ruined city below.

## Darrow Hill

Darrow Hill lies in the northern Hillsbrad Foothills, beneath the Headland. Mountain lions and stags range its open fields, and within the Foothill Caverns yetis have made their den, among veins of Alterac granite that run through the rock.

## Dun Garok

Dun Garok stands in the hills of the Hillsbrad Foothills, a dwarven keep under the command of Captain Ironhill. His garrison guards the humans of Hillsbrad and watches over the Azurelode Mine, a presence the Forsaken cannot abide, who have offered gold to any who would slip into the keep and put its dwarves to the sword.

## Durnholde Keep

Durnholde Keep stands broken east of Tarren Mill, its barracks and mage tower fallen in on themselves, its arena silent. Once it was the seat of the orc internment system raised after the Second War, ruled by Lieutenant General Aedelas Blackmoore, a self-absorbed drunkard who used the camp for his own advancement.

It was Blackmoore who found an orc infant among the bodies of murdered parents and brought him back to the keep, naming him Thrall. The boy was raised and taught there, wet-nursed by a human woman whose daughter, Taretha Foxton, grew close to him. Blackmoore beat and fought the boy in the ring for years, trading on his strength to climb in rank. Taretha wrote to him in secret, and he wrote back, until at last he told her he meant to escape. She created the diversion that let him slip free, met him beyond the walls, and gave him provisions before he left.

He did return, years later, at the head of the free orcs he had gathered. Thrall laid siege to Durnholde, killed Blackmoore in single combat, and called upon the Spirit of the Earth to level the fortress entirely. What is left has since drawn the Syndicate, who claim these ruins as their own.

## Eastern Strand

The Eastern Strand runs along the southeastern coast of the Hillsbrad Foothills, its counterpart lying further west along the same shore. This stretch of sand belongs to the Daggerspine naga, who hold the beaches and the shallow water beyond them.

## Gallows' Corner

At Gallows' Corner, where the roads meet in the Alterac Mountains near the ruined halls of Alterac, a scout tower stands broken and empty. Crushridge ogres now hold the crossing, prowling the stones around the tower's remains, and nothing else stirs there.

## Gavin's Naze

Gavin's Naze is the last of Hillsbrad's four hills, and the most guarded.

Mountain lions range this hill, among them Araga, marked by her uncommon coloring.

## Growless Cave

In the Alterac Mountains, a cave shelters mountain yeti, with Skhowl often found just outside its mouth. Deep within stands the Flame of Uzel, a pyre raised by the human Helcular for the ritual that would make him a lich. Meat burned upon it draws the mighty yeti Frostmaw to the entrance.

## Hillsbrad Fields

Hillsbrad Fields lie fertile and cultivated across the western Hillsbrad Foothills, feeding Southshore and the town of Hillsbrad besides. Apple orchards, pumpkin patches, and grazing cattle cover the land, and the Azurelode Mine still sees work from the people of the town.

Early in the Second War, Doomhammer turned his armies against Hillsbrad and had it destroyed in a gruesome display meant to serve as a warning to the human kingdoms. It was rebuilt after the war, and stands populated still, one of the few corners of former Lordaeron that was not left in ruin nor swallowed by the Scarlet Crusade; its guards yet wear the tabard of their kingdom, and Magistrate Burnside governs from the town hall with the Council of Hillsbrad and the clerk Horrace Whitesteed at his side.

Now the fields lie under threat again, from High Executor Darthalia's forces at Tarren Mill. Peasants and farmers bear the raids, but many strike back at the undead in ambushes of their own, armed by the blacksmith Verringtan and his apprentices.

## Misty Shore

The Misty Shore runs along Lordamere Lake, between Dandred's Fold and Dalaran. Turtles move slowly through its shallows.

Two of the Scarlet Crusade hold this shore, falling on any who come too near, as though the ground itself needed guarding.

Across the water, the Dawning Isles can be seen, with Fenris Keep brooding faintly through the mist.

## Nethander Stead

Nethander Stead lies just east of Southshore, its fields long abandoned and overrun by Mudsnout gnolls, the only place their tribe is found. Giant white mushrooms called Mudsnout Blossoms grow wild there, once sought by Apothecary Lydon for his deadly Elixir of Agony.

## Purgation Isle

A spiral of stone rises south of the Hillsbrad shore, crowned with a cemetery and the broken remains of a scout tower. Before the First War, Purgation Isle belonged to Kul Tiras.

What came after is uncertain, but the dead do not rest. Dozens of spirits walk the isle, human and dwarf, paladins and priests, monks and mages, condemned souls bound to its stones.

## Ravenholdt Manor

Ravenholdt Manor sits apart from the world by design. Reached only by a narrow, twisting path that climbs from a plateau northwest of Durnholde Keep, winding through the mountains and a small cave before it opens onto the grounds, the manor is not a place one stumbles upon. Outsiders are not welcome here, and those who serve Lord Jorach Ravenholdt keep it that way.

The manor is his property, orange-roofed in the old fashion of Alterac, whose kingdom once held this land before the First War.

In the years since, the Syndicate has wandered this part of the highlands, and Ravenholdt has stood against that affliction from behind its hidden path and guarded gates.

## Slaughter Hollow

Deep in the Alterac Mountains, the Crushridge ogres have made their home in Slaughter Hollow, a mound sunk into the hills. Little more is known of the place than the ogres who hold it, but the name alone tells what waits for anyone who wanders too close.

## Sofera's Naze

Sofera's Naze rises as one of the four fingers of Hillsbrad. The Syndicate holds the hill, watching over the ground below.

## Southpoint Tower

Southpoint Tower stands at the far western edge of Hillsbrad Foothills, just south of the road that runs on toward Silverpine Forest. The stonework is old and cracked, a scout post left to ruin, though the tower itself was never brought down in the fighting that passed through here.

## Strahnbrad

Strahnbrad sits on the northeastern foothills of the Alterac Mountains, its town hall and blacksmith standing empty now. Once it belonged to the Kingdom of Alterac, and after the Second War it passed into the hands of Lordaeron.

The town's ruin began shortly before the Third War, when Blackrock orcs descended on it to take slaves for sacrifice to the Burning Legion. Strahnbrad had only a handful of footmen to defend it, but word reached the Knights of the Silver Hand in time. Uther the Lightbringer rode ahead to try to head off the attack, while Prince Arthas Menethil was sent to hold the town itself. Arthas did not arrive in time. Dean, Joe, Mike, Scott, and many others were killed before he reached Strahnbrad, and what defenders remained joined him in routing the orcs and killing the Slave Master who had led the raid. The Alliance carried the field, but Strahnbrad's people were already gone.

The Syndicate later took the ruined town for their own, using it as a stronghold to claim the Alterac Mountains, and held the orc Gol'dir prisoner in one of its cells, working him as a slave to haul and dig.

## Tarren Mill

Tarren Mill was once a simple Lordaeronian town among the grasslands below the Alterac Mountains. In the early days of the Second War, high elven archers from Silvermoon, sent south to see the orcish threat for themselves, were ambushed near the town and dragged off to a nearby prison camp. King Terenas sent Alliance soldiers to free them, and the rescue succeeded. It was that success which led the Council of Silvermoon to join the armies of Quel'Thalas to the Alliance of Lordaeron.

Tarren Mill did not remain in human hands. The Forsaken took it in time, and left it as they left their other holdings: a church, a scatter of houses, orchards gone to rot, everything standing but nothing whole. From here the Forsaken stage their war against the Alliance forces of Southshore and Dun Garok, and the ground between Tarren Mill and Southshore has grown infamous for skirmish after skirmish between the two towns, neither able to break the other.

## The Headland

The Headland is one of the fingers of land reaching out across Hillsbrad.

On the cliffside here, Araga rests among her cubs.

## Thoradin's Wall

King Thoradin raised this wall to hold back the Amani trolls, in the days when Strom stood young and Arathor had only just claimed the land for humanity. For generations it stood a stalwart sentinel, a line no troll horde could cross.

Centuries on, it still stands, but heavily damaged, its stones broken and its long watch reduced to ruin.

## Western Strand

The Western Strand lies on the southwest coast of Hillsbrad Foothills. Torn Fin murlocs have raised small camps along its shore, and Andy Murgole patrols these waters, keeping watch over the coastline where they dwell.
