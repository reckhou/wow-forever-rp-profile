# Desolace

Theradras, daughter of Therazane the Stonemother, once lay slumbering beneath a fertile grassland. Tauren shaman, believing the Earth Mother herself dwelled below, called out to her for decades until at last something answered -- not the Earth Mother, but Theradras, roused in fury from her sleep. She fed on the land to restore her weakened form, and the meadows died in an instant. The tauren named what remained Desolace.

Zaetar, son of the forestlord Cenarius, came to bind her, but loved her instead, and she loved him back. Of their union the centaur were born, and their first act was to murder their own father. They multiplied swiftly and drove the tauren from the land, beginning centuries of war between the two races.

Theradras dwells still in Maraudon, beneath the western canyons, and it is there that the centaur trace the birth of their race.

## Bolgan's Hole

In Gelkis Village, the cave called Bolgan's Hole shelters the clan's most prominent members, its khan Shaka among them.

Deep within grow blue crystals, the tears of Theradras, hoarded by the Gelkis shaman, who believe the stones carry the princess's protection.

## Ethel Rethor

Off the northwest coast of Desolace, the sea has swallowed most of Ethel Rethor. Only a lone tower breaks the surface, joined to shore by a narrow bridge to a doorway that stays shut. Naga have taken the drowned ruin for their own.

## Gelkis Village

Gelkis Village sits ringed around a wide ravine in southwestern Desolace, seat of the Gelkis clan. Bridges strung between the cliffs and bluffs of the ravine lead down to Bolgan's Hole, a cave cut into the southern mountain face, where the Gelkis Khan makes his residence.

The village lies hard against Desolace's main north-south road, so close that the road itself has become a danger to those who travel it. The Gelkis have pushed new huts out to the east side of the road leading toward Feralas, along the lake, spreading beyond the ravine that first sheltered them.

## Ghost Walker Post

Ghost Walker Post stands beside the Kodo Graveyard in central Desolace, and the dead there shape everything about the place. Tauren shamans keep watch over the bones, easing the spirits of fallen kodo into their next lives and, when the need arises, speaking with those who have already passed. Gurda Wildmane leads them, holding this exposed post with little more than the bare necessities to sustain it.

Unlike Shadowprey Village, secure elsewhere in the region, Ghost Walker Post has no such safety. It is a forward camp built for war, not comfort, with no inn and nowhere to rest. It exists for one purpose: to carry the Horde's fight against the Burning Blade cultists and the centaur tribes ranging across Desolace.

## Kodo Graveyard

The kodo of Desolace come here to die, their bones piled and bleached across the cracked earth. Disturb them and the dead do not stay quiet: apparitions of kodo rise from the ground to guard what remains of their kind.

## Kolkar Village

The village on the eastern margin of Desolace is the seat of the Kolkar clan, the most prolific of the centaur clans across Kalimdor, with outposts as far off as the Barrens and Durotar. The rival Magram press them constantly, for the feud between the clans never rests.

## Kormek's Hut

The hut stands weathered in north-central Desolace. Inside dwells Willow, a member of the Twilight's Hammer, tending the place with unlikely warmth for one of that cult. Outside, in a pen scattered with kodo bones, Bibbly F'utzbuckle dances among the remains, delighted at the profit such bones might fetch.

## Magram Village

Magram Village rises from a scatter of islands amid polluted yellow pools in southeastern Desolace, its huts linked by crude bridges thrown across the water. Here the Magram clan makes its home, one of the centaur peoples locked in war with the Gelkis clan for dominance of these barren lands.

## Mannoroc Coven

Among the ruins of the night elves that lie broken across Desolace, one place bears a name no traveler speaks lightly. Mannoroc Coven takes its name from Mannoroth, the dreaded Pit Lord, and the ground here remembers him. The earth was dead long before the demons came, but fel magic has blasted it further still, until the very air hangs thick with corruption and a deadly pall settles over anyone who crosses into it.

The Burning Blade cultists opened the way, tearing a number of portals into the world to summon through them the servants of the Burning Legion: infernals, succubi, felhounds and felguards that now prowl the ruins. Lord Azrethoc commands these demons, while the orc warlock Jugkar Grim'rod stands among them as the Burning Blade's own voice in the coven. Together they have built the largest gathering of demons anywhere in central Kalimdor, proof that the cultists' scheme has succeeded far beyond a warning.

## Nijel's Point

Nijel's Point stands watch in the northern mountains of Desolace, an outpost held by the Alliance and the Cenarion Circle.

Keeper Marandis came here with his dryad sisters, using the outpost as their base while they pursued Princess Theradras. Their purpose was to retrieve the body of Zaetar from the depths of Maraudon and carry it home to Stonetalon Mountains.

At the same time, the Stormwind Army stationed the Nijel's Point Guard here under Captain Pentigast, set against the threat of the centaur clans that held the region. Pentigast watched the rivalry between the Gelkis and Magram with a soldier's wariness, certain that whichever clan won out would grow bold enough to turn on the Alliance holdings nearby, and so sought to learn how the centaurs might be beaten by weighing one clan against the other.

## Ranazjar Isle

Ranazjar Isle is a small, rocky island off the northwest coast of Desolace, home to the serpentine naga. Among the stones stand night elven ruins, part of the same ancient city as Ethel Rethor, now silent above the naga that dwell there.

## Sar'theris Strand

The coast where Desolace meets the sea is unlike the land behind it. While the interior lies grey and desolate, Sar'theris Strand is sunny, its grasses green. Sea giants wade its shallows, naga coil through its waters, and makrura scuttle along its sands.

Below Ethel Rethor, a small boat lies smashed on the beach, its wood splintered, whatever crew it carried gone.

## Sargeron

The city that stood here once bore a night elven name, lost since the War of the Ancients tore the world apart. What remains is ruin, now called Sargeron by the Hatefury satyr who dwell there, in honor of their demon master Sargeras.

The ruins once held three holy sites of Elune: her cathedral, her temple, her sanctuary. Relics of that older faith still lie among the stones: Elune's Brazier, her Handmaiden, her Cup, and the Ancient Vortex Runestone.

## Scrabblescrew's Camp

Near the Kodo Graveyard in Desolace stands a small goblin camp. It belongs to Smeed Scrabblescrew, an enterprising goblin who maintains a kodo caravan, driving the great beasts across the wastes for whatever profit the land allows.

## Shadowbreak Ravine

Shadowbreak Ravine lies hidden in the mountains southeast of Shok'Thokar, a fold of Desolace few would stumble upon by chance. Giant mushrooms rise among its rocks, and at its heart sits a summoning circle bound in massive chains, built by the Burning Blade cultists who made the ravine their haven.

## Shadowprey Village

Shadowprey Village sits where the Sar'theris Strand meets the southern coast of Desolace, a fishing settlement of the trolls. Its people make their living from the sea, and their catches are what keep the Ghost Walker Post fed further inland.

## Tethris Aran

Night elven ruins lie scattered in northern Desolace, north of Thunder Axe Fortress, the remains of a settlement thought destroyed in the Great Sundering. Nothing has been rebuilt. Only Desolace's wild creatures now move among the broken stones.

## Thunder Axe Fortress

Thunder Axe Fortress stands in northwestern Desolace, given over to the Burning Blade cult. Behind its walls of stronghold, barracks, cave, and watch towers, the cultists under Kohor work toward summoning the demon Nazargen in his full strength, wary of calling him too soon, when he would be weak enough to be destroyed.

The fortress once held the Sceptre of Light, an artifact of terrible potency stolen by the Burning Blade from the naga. Azore Aldamort, serving the Argent Dawn, set himself to recovering it, and with aid succeeded in wresting it back from the cult's keeping.

Within these walls also dwelt Tyranis Malem, a name spoken with contempt among his own people, for he turned traitor to them and gave himself to the study of demonic magic, making Thunder Axe Fortress his refuge for such dark pursuits.

## Valley of Bones

The Valley of Bones is a crag on Desolace's southern edge, haunted by skeletons, centaur necromancers, and the ghosts of the slain Magram clan. Against the cliff walls rest a pair of massive skeletons, the Dead Goliaths, their origin unknown.

## Valley of Spears

The Valley of Spears, in western Desolace, is home to the Maraudine clan of centaur, reachable on foot only through narrow mountain passes to the south and east. At its southwestern end lies the entrance to Maraudon. Among the centaur, held captive, is Melizza Brimbuzzle.
