## Shadowfang Keep

Shadowfang Keep stands on a high bluff above Pyrewood Village. It belonged once to Baron Silverlaine, a lord of Gilneas, until the Scourge came down upon Lordaeron and the Royal Archmage Arugal sought a way to stop them. Drawing on the research of the mage Ur, and with King Genn Greymane's blessing but against the counsel of the Kirin Tor, Arugal summoned the worgen. They turned back the undead tide, then turned on Gilneas itself, savaging the Greymane Wall before running loose through Silverpine. They fell upon the keep and killed Baron Silverlaine and his household.

Arugal, broken by what he had unleashed, did not flee it. He named the worgen his children, took the keep for his own, and renamed it Shadowfang. Communing with a master he served in the Wolf Cult, he spread the curse through the forest, until Pyrewood Village itself was turned, its people human by day and beasts by night, ruled over by a council that answered to him alone.
