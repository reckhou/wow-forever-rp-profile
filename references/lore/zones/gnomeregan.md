## Gnomeregan

Gnomeregan is the subterranean capital of the gnomish kingdom of the same name, wrought-iron evidence of gnomish brilliance, ambition, and ingenuity.

During the Third War, a trogg invasion brought about its downfall. The troggs had been pulled from their sleep by the upheaval of titan sites, and swarmed up into the city from below. In a desperate attempt to kill the invaders, High Tinker Gelbin Mekkatorque, on the advice of his advisor Sicco Thermaplugg, ordered his people to open the pressure valves of their giant, grinding machines and release toxic radiation throughout Gnomeregan. Though the gas killed some of the troggs, it only empowered others, and nearly eighty percent of gnomekind died within days. The survivors fled to the protection of Ironforge. Others were twisted into horrific, mindless, evil-doing leper gnomes, who still haunt the ruined tunnels.

Driven mad by the radiation himself, Thermaplugg declared himself "King of Gnomeregan" and seized control of the poisoned city, leading an army of troggs and leper gnomes through its wrecked halls and workshops.
