## Uldaman

Deep within the mountains of Khaz Modan lies Uldaman, a vast and ancient vault raised by the titan keepers in ages long past. Dwarves out of Ironforge once delved into it, cutting away at its buried chambers before the Third War, but the vault has since fallen to troggs and Dark Irons, who now dig through its stone for whatever earthly riches it still holds.

Uldaman remains rich with the leavings of that titan-forged age, and those willing to brave its depths still find artifacts buried within it, slowly uncovered by archaeologists and adventurers of the Explorers' League who continue to pick through the ruin.
