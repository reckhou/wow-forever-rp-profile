# Thousand Needles

The canyon runs deep and dry, its floor lost among towering mesas, until in the east it opens into the Shimmering Flats, a great salt pan under a wide sky. The sunrise from Freewind Post or from the heights around Darkcloud Pinnacle is a spectacular thing to see over such barren country. The druid Quentin has wondered whether the harsh dryness here, set so close against the lush jungle of neighboring Feralas, is not a natural thing at all, but the mark of some strange magic worked upon the land.

The high mesas belong to the tauren, and Freewind Post stands as their hold among them. They did not keep this ground unchallenged. The local centaur pressed them, and worse, their own kin of the Grimtotem tribe turned against them, so that the tauren of Freewind Post were forced to fight tribe against tribe for the high country. Only with the aid of the Horde did they beat the centaur and the Grimtotem back.

## Camp E'thok

Camp E'thok is a Galak centaur camp in northwestern Thousand Needles. Its centaurs raided a caravan carrying the goblin Wizlo Bearingshiner, but could not steal the gadget he prized most, kept safe around the neck of his ferocious pet panther.

## Darkcloud Pinnacle

Darkcloud Pinnacle rises on three small mesas east of the Great Lift, their tops laced together with rope bridges strung over open air. Only two paths lead up, each well guarded: a natural slope across a fourth mesa, or a bridge reaching over from Razorfen Downs.

The Grimtotem tauren hold these heights, led by Arnak Grimtotem and Harb Foulmountain. Behind the guarded bridges, the two leaders carry on a secret correspondence with some dark power whose identity is not known. What passes between them is not spoken of openly, but the care taken to seal off the mesas shows how closely that secret is kept.

## Freewind Post

Freewind Post sits high on a mesa in Thousand Needles, raised out of the reach of the centaur that roam below. Wooden lifts carry its people up from the canyon floor, and rope bridges string between the neighboring pinnacles, climbing pinnacle by pinnacle until they reach the village. Below, the Galak centaur press their warbands, and harpies wheel and shriek over the flats, but the height itself is the post's real defense.

The deeper trouble came from the northwest, from Darkcloud Pinnacle, where the Grimtotem tribe held their own high ground. Freewind Post grew wary of them long before open war came: tauren went missing, agents of unknown and dark origin were seen visiting the pinnacle, and murder was laid at the Grimtotems' door. Chieftain Arnak Grimtotem bore the worst of the blame. When Lakota Windsong of Freewind Post refused his advances, he had her taken by force, and when her brother came to defend her, the Grimtotem killed him.

## Highperch

Highperch rises above the Thousand Needles, home to wyverns and their nests. Among the crags the tauren Pao'ka Swiftmountain lies stranded, hoping to slip past the wyverns and make it home.

## Ironstone Camp

Ironstone Camp sits at the edge of the Shimmering Flats.

## Mirage Raceway

A goblin named Pozzik and a gnome named Fizzle Brassbolts each believed their own people the cleverer engineers, and neither would yield the point. So they settled it as tinkerers do: they built a track, and they built rocket cars to run on it.

The rivalry draws crowds far beyond the two of them. Horde and Alliance alike come to cheer their favorites as gnome and goblin drag cars race daily, each team scrambling afterward to rebuild their engines with some new edge. The real contest is always the same one: which race builds the better machine, and it is never settled.

## Razorfen Downs

Deep within the brambles of the Thousand Needles lies a maze of thorn and bone, its entrance said to be shaped from Agamaggan's own maw, an arched gap in the outer dome so hidden that only those who already know its shape can find it. This is Razorfen Downs, the ancestral burial ground and traditional capital of the quilboar, crafted from the same mighty vines as Razorfen Kraul. Charlga Razorflank shaped most of the great thorn dome that covers it.

For generations the Downs stood as a place of quilboar dead, tended by the high priests of the Death's Head tribe. But after the Third War, the lich Amnennar the Coldbringer turned his agents against the quilboar and seized their holy city, binding it into a bastion of the Scourge. The quilboar fought bitterly to win back what was theirs before Amnennar's plague could spread further into the Barrens, and in that struggle the living were dragged down and caged within the thorns alongside the dead.

Amnennar remains within the labyrinth still, ruling over the quilboar he has bent to his will, his hold on the Downs unbroken.

## Roguefeather Den

In a small cavern in south central Thousand Needles, the harpies of Roguefeather Den have made their nest. They screech at any who draw near, and in the back of the den they hoard their gathered foodstuffs, guarding the stores as fiercely as they guard their young.

## Splithoof Crag

Splithoof Crag stands against the northern wall of Thousand Needles, east of Darkcloud Pinnacle, home to the Galak centaur. At its center lies Splithoof Hold, a cavern in the mountainside where the Galak keep the Cloven Hoof, an ancient artifact guarded by the Ancient Brazier and its Sacred Flame.

## Splithoof Hold

The Galak centaur made Splithoof Hold their stronghold in the caves of Splithoof Crag. Within it burns the Sacred Flame upon the Ancient Brazier, a fire said to endure even underwater.

## Tahonda Ruins

A tauren village once stood where the Shimmering Flats now lie, long fallen to ruin and swallowed by sand. Saltstone basilisks nest among the stones now, and some say it was they who drove the tauren out.

One clue remains: on the leather wall of a building, two sets of three parallel tears, the mark of claws, not wind or time. Something came for that village with purpose.

No names survive. Only the ruins remain, near the mountain path to Tanaris.

## The Great Lift

The Great Lift is a tauren-built elevator carrying travelers between the Barrens and Thousand Needles below. Grish Longrunner and Brave Moonhorn keep watch over it for the Horde.

## The Rustmaul Dig Site

The Rustmaul Dig Site lies in the Shimmering Flats, its trenches left mid-work. The Explorers' League had come here to uncover old ruins, working the ground in the shadow of a cave that held a nest of silithid.

The digging stopped before it was finished. The trenches stand open and empty, the tools of the dwarves abandoned where they were left, and the cave beneath the site remains home to the silithid that dwell there still.

## The Screeching Canyon

The Screeching Canyon cuts through southern Thousand Needles, its walls echoing with the cries of the Screeching harpies that infest it. At the canyon's back lies the Roguefeather Den, the harpies' stronghold.

## The Shimmering Flats

The Shimmering Flats lie at the southeastern edge of Thousand Needles, where the land runs flat and bare to the border with Tanaris. Nothing grows here. The ground is hard-baked salt, home only to basilisks, scorpids, turtles, and vultures. Once this was the bottom of a lake -- the wrecked hull of a sailing vessel still sits half-buried in the northern flats, a ship stranded far from any water, proof of what this place used to be before it dried to salt and cracked earth.

That flatness drew goblins and gnomes from opposite sides of their long rivalry. They raised the Mirage Raceway here and raced to build machines faster than one another's. The contraptions rarely held together. Craters pock the flats where engines went wrong, and the wreckage of failed racers lies scattered across the salt, left to bake where it fell.

## The Weathered Nook

The northern wall of Thousand Needles hides a small cave known as the Weathered Nook, easy to pass by unless one knows to look for it. Here Dorn Plainstalker and Prate Cloudseer made their home, tucked away from the winds and dust of the canyon below.

## Weazel's Crater

A great scar opens in the northern Shimmering Flats, deep enough to swallow the light by midday. No one knows how it came to be, though its name hints at some figure called Weazel, whether finder or maker. Scattered rubble and lesser craters nearby suggest goblin or gnome tinkering gone wrong, some invention pushed too far.

Saltstone basilisks have since claimed the pit as their own.

## Whitereach Post

Whitereach Post is a small Horde camp along the road near Camp E'thok in Thousand Needles.

## Windbreak Canyon

Windbreak Canyon lies between Freewind Post and the Shimmering Flats, its walls riddled with nests. Wind serpents claim the place as their own, rising on the currents that give the canyon its name. A path here leads on to the Weathered Nook.
