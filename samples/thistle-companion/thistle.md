## About
```text
A lean young nightsaber with a torn left ear and an unhurried, sideways way of looking at people.

She came out of the forest above Dolanaar, the season the furbolgs there went mad, and has walked at Elaris Moonbrook's heel ever since. She goes where she likes, and it usually turns out to be the same place.
```
