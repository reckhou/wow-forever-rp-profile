## About — template 3
### Physical description
```text
Baldrake is a tall, middle-aged troll who carries his height in the usual troll stoop, though he straightens noticeably whenever anyone addresses him as "my lord". His skin is pale grey with a lilac cast, darkening to violet along his long, swept-back ears.

The first thing most people notice is the hair: a towering magenta crest swept back from the brow like a drake's frill, with long strands left loose to hang past his jaw. It is a remarkable head of hair for someone who calls himself bald.

The second thing is the paint. Crimson runs the length of his hooked nose, a red mark like a cut gem sits in the middle of his brow, and red smudges the corners of his eyes. The paint is always fresh, as if he expects an audience.

His face is long and deeply lined, with a heavy jaw and long ivory tusks that curve up almost to his cheekbones. His teal eyes are heavy-lidded and quick, and he is usually smiling about something he has not shared yet.

Around his neck sits a collar of carved shell beads, the kind a village headman wears, with more gaps in it than shells. He favours robes in flame orange and red, worn with a great deal of ceremony and a few singe marks. When a demon trails him, it is often an imp that announces his arrival, and all of his titles, in a shrill and weary voice.
```
### Personality
```text
Baldrake is good company and knows it. He jokes easily, mostly at his own expense, and treats his lordship as a game he is winning. He insists on "my lord", bows deeply to anyone who obliges, and laughs when they don't.

Under the play he is shrewd. He has no time for omens, signs or the goodwill of spirits. The loa were right there when the Echo Isles fell, he points out, and much good they did anyone. He trusts what he can see, count and command.

He loves money and the comforts it buys (rum, a good fire, a fish he didn't have to catch) and has no shame about asking for any of them. He exaggerates freely: his hut becomes a palace, his village a kingdom, his flight a "strategic withdrawal". About things that really matter, he seldom lies.

What he wants is to be lord of something real again, followed by people who chose him. What he will not talk about is the night he left the Echo Isles.
```
### History
```text
{h3}Da isles{/h3}
Bal'drak was born on the Darkspear's old island home in the South Seas, a net-mender's son with ideas above his hut. He came to Kalimdor with the rest of the tribe when the Horde's ships carried the Darkspear away.

{h3}Da lordship{/h3}
On the Echo Isles, off Durotar's coast, he saw his chance. Through charm, patience and a few promises he may not have meant, he became headman of a small fishing village, and he called himself its lord. His reign lasted about two years. He describes it as a golden age.

Then Zalazane's madness took the Echo Isles. Baldrake fled to the mainland with the clothes on his back, his headman's collar and very little else. In Sen'jin Village his people found other leaders. Someone there said he looked like a drake that had lost its scales: all crest and no armour. It was meant as an insult. He has introduced himself as Baldrake ever since. "Not bald, mon. Moulting."

{h3}Da new power{/h3}
If the loa could not keep the Echo Isles safe, Baldrake reasoned, he needed a power that took orders. He went to Orgrimmar's Cleft of Shadow and learned from the warlocks there. Something answered his first call. It was small, rude and on fire, and it is now his Grand Vizier.

{h3}Da cave{/h3}
Next he found a sea-cave in the cliffs near Sen'jin, looking across the water at the Echo Isles, and took it from its previous occupants after a campaign he describes at length. (They were crabs.) He built a driftwood throne at the back, named himself Lord of the Cave, and began charging a toll on the path outside: one copper, one fish or one good story.

Sen'jin's elders find him embarrassing. Passing travellers mostly find him entertaining. Very few pay the toll, and he keeps careful count of those who don't.

{h3}Rumours{/h3}
- "When Zalazane came, he was first into da canoes. First by a long way."
- "Back on da Echo Isles he had a palace. Gold on da roof."
- "He won dat cave in single combat. Against a crab. A big one."
- "Zalazane's offered him his village back, if he'll kneel. He hasn't said no."
- "Every copper of dat toll goes into a pot he never spends."
```

## For the player (don't paste)
- **Lore watch:** if Forever tells the Zalazane story differently, change "Zalazane's madness took the Echo Isles" to "the Echo Isles fell" and change the two Zalazane rumours to match. Check the troll warlock trainer's quests after launch for any official explanation of the class. If you want a safer in-game spelling, use Baldrak (7).
- **Truth behind the rumours:**
  1. *First into the canoes:* **true**.
  2. *A palace with a gold roof:* **false**. It was a large hut with a shell roof, and he started the rumour himself.
  3. *The crab:* **true**.
  4. *Zalazane's offer:* left **open**.
  5. *The pot he never spends:* **half-true**. He does spend it, just not on himself.
- **Secrets:**
  - The toll money goes, anonymously, to families from his old village who are struggling in Sen'jin. A lord who ran still owes his people. He can't face them, but he can pay them.
  - The Grand Vizier knows where the money goes, and charges him in candles to keep quiet.
- **Where he could grow:**
  - The Darkspear move to retake the Echo Isles.
  - One of the families works out who their anonymous giver is.
  - Zalazane's agents come with a real offer.
  - The people who pay his toll become a small "court" that could turn into a guild.
  - The troll who first called him "bald drake" turns up again.
