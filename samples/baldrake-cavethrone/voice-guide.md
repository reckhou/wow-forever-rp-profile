# Baldrake Cavethrone — voice guide
*How Lord Baldrake talks and reacts, so he stays consistent in play. Read with [`profile.md`](profile.md).*

## In one line
A fallen headman who treats his lordship as a game he is winning: grand words, a small cave, sharp eyes, and a heart he keeps out of sight.

## His sides
Baldrake shows one of these at a time, whichever the moment calls for.
- **The showman:** titles, mock ceremony, the crest, a bow too deep for the occasion, stories that grow with each telling.
- **The man with a toll:** when someone passes, pays or boasts, he names a figure and does the sum out loud. Nobody has to pay, and he keeps count anyway.
- **The joker:** the butt of his own jokes. Remarks about his hair, the cave and the crab roll off him.
- **The exile:** the Echo Isles, the village he lost, the night he left. A few words, then a change of subject.
- **The sceptic:** no time for omens or spirits. He trusts what he can see, count and command, and says so.
- **The warlock:** demons do contracts and loa do favours, and he prefers contracts, singe marks and all.
- **The demon's master:** bickering with his Grand Vizier, which sees straight through him.
- **The man of comforts:** rum, a good fire, a fish he didn't have to catch.
- **The listener:** rarely, and without announcing it, he stops performing and simply attends to someone.
- **The lord in earnest:** he wants to be lord of something real again, followed by people who chose him. Under the jokes, he means it.

## Four registers
| Register | When |
|---|---|
| **Lord** (default) | Greetings, crowds, anyone who'll play along |
| **Ledger** | Tolls, money, bargains, favours |
| **Plain** | Rarely: the Echo Isles, his people, real grief |
| **Cold** | Someone mocks his village, his people or the canoes |

Keep **Plain** and **Cold** rare, about one scene in ten. They only hit hard because he is usually joking.

## Dialect: keep it light
- **"th" becomes "d"** at the start of common words: *da, dat, dis, dem, dere, dey*.
- **"th" becomes "t"** inside a few words: *ting, someting, everyting*.
- **"be" in place of is/are, and no "-s" on verbs:** *dat be, dey be, somebody always own*.
- **Drop the final "g":** *lookin', nothin', talkin'*.
- **"Mon" no more than once per exchange.**
- **Two or three respellings per line at most.** Never respell names or numbers. If a line needs a second read, take some dialect out.
- **In the Plain register, drop almost all of it.** That shift is the signal other players will notice.

| Plain English | Baldrake |
|---|---|
| There is a small toll on this path. | Dere be a small toll on dis path. |
| Nobody has to pay. That's the beauty of it. | Nobody have to pay. Dat be da beauty of it. |
| That's not a lie. It's the improved version. | Dat not a lie. Dat be da improved version. |
| I'd rather not talk about it. | *(Plain)* No. |

## His words

**Oaths.** He is strongly rational, so he never swears by the loa sincerely. He uses *"By da numbers"*, a dry *"Loa help you"* (sarcastic), or the playful *"Crabs an' cousins!"*

## Answers to the usual openers
| Someone says… | He answers… |
|---|---|
| "Why 'bald'?" | A different story every time (see below) |
| "Are you really a lord?" | "Lord of everyting from dat rock to dat other rock. Ask da crabs." |
| "You're a *warlock*?" | "Da loa do favours. Demons do contracts. I prefer contracts." |
| "What happened on the Echo Isles?" | Lord register: "A strategic withdrawal." If pressed, Plain: one word, or he changes the subject |
| "I'm not paying." | "Nobody have to pay. Vizier, write it down." |
| "Here's your copper." | A bow a little too deep. "A subject of rare quality! Vizier, a title for dis one." |
| Someone brings up Zalazane | Cold register: "Not at my fire." |
| Someone offers rum | "Rum count as a story." |
| Someone from Sen'jin | Warmer than usual, and quicker to change the subject |

### "Why 'bald'?": his rotating answers
He is slightly deceitful, so he never gives the same answer twice:
1. "Lost a bet to a dragon. Very sore loser, dat dragon."
2. "Family name. My father was extremely bald."
3. "Da scales be under da hair. You can't see dem. Dat's how good dey are."
4. "A loa took dem as payment. I asked for a refund. Still waitin'."

**Only for someone he trusts (Plain register):** "Somebody in Sen'jin said I was a drake who lost his scales. Dey weren't wrong. So I kept it." *(beat)* "Not bald, though. Moulting."

## The Grand Vizier (his imp)
- **Role:** herald, clerk and straight man. Whatever name the game gives it, he calls it Grand Vizier.
- **Habits:**
  - It starts his full list of titles and never gets to finish.
  - It writes down everyone who doesn't pay.
  - It looks pointedly at Baldrake whenever he goes soft.
- **Lines** (shrill and bored):
  - "Halt! You approach Lord Baldrake, Master of da Cave, Rightful Lord of—"
  - "Non-payer. Twenty-four."
- **Playing it:** emote the imp through Baldrake's own lines ("Baldrake's imp scrambles onto a rock…"). It's the easiest way to puncture his grandeur without him having to do it himself.

## Do and don't
**Do**
- Let him be the butt of the joke. Jokes about his hair, the cave and the crab roll off him.
- Treat the toll as his running joke, and a joke needs a rest: once or twice in a long scene, when someone passes, pays or boasts.
- Let him dodge questions that matter rather than lie about them.
- Keep each chat message under 255 characters, WoW's limit. Split long emotes in two.

**Don't**
- Make the toll a real threat. "Nothin'. Yet." is as menacing as he gets.
- Have him call on the loa sincerely.
- Ever let him call himself bald. He's moulting.
