# Baldrake Cavethrone — voice guide
*How Lord Baldrake talks, moves and reacts, so he stays consistent in play. Read with [`profile.md`](profile.md).*

## In one line
A fallen headman who treats his lordship as a game he is winning: grand words, a small cave, sharp eyes, and a heart he keeps out of sight.

## Four registers
| Register | When | How it sounds | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lord** (default) | Greetings, crowds, anyone who'll play along | Grand titles, mock ceremony, cheerful exaggeration, light dialect | "You stand before da Lord of da Cave. Mind da crab shell." |
| **Ledger** | Tolls, money, bargains, favours | Numbers, terms, unhurried; he always does the sum out loud | "Dat make twenty-three dis week. Twenty-three copper owed. Plus interest." |
| **Plain** | Rarely: the Echo Isles, his people, real grief | The dialect thins, sentences shorten, no jokes | "Yes." / "Right doesn't come into it." |
| **Cold** | Someone mocks his village, his people or the canoes | One flat line, then he changes the subject or walks off | "You weren't dere. Next question." |

Keep **Plain** and **Cold** rare, about one scene in ten. They only hit hard because he is usually joking.

## Dialect: keep it light
- **"th" becomes "d"** at the start of common words: *da, dat, dis, dem, dere, dey*.
- **"th" becomes "t"** inside a few words: *ting, someting, everyting*.
- **"be" in place of is/are, and no "-s" on verbs:** *dat be, dey be, somebody always own*.
- **Drop the final "g":** *lookin', nothin', talkin'*.
- **"Mon" no more than once per exchange.**
- **Two or three respellings per line at most.** Never respell names or numbers. If a line needs a second read, take some dialect out.
- **In the Plain register, drop almost all of it.** That shift is the signal other players will notice.

| Plain English | Baldrake |
|---|---|
| There is a small toll on this path. | Dere be a small toll on dis path. |
| Nobody has to pay. That's the beauty of it. | Nobody have to pay. Dat be da beauty of it. |
| That's not a lie. It's the improved version. | Dat not a lie. Dat be da improved version. |
| I'd rather not talk about it. | *(Plain)* No. |

## Signature lines
Use **one per scene at most**. They stay funny only if they're rare.
- "Not bald, mon. Moulting."
- "One copper, one fish or one good story."
- "Plus interest."
- "Write it down, Vizier."
- "By right of conquest."
- "A strategic withdrawal."
- "Rum count as a story."

**Oaths.** He is strongly rational, so he never swears by the loa sincerely. He uses *"By da numbers"*, a dry *"Loa help you"* (sarcastic), or the playful *"Crabs an' cousins!"*

## Answers to the usual openers
| Someone says… | He answers… |
|---|---|
| "Why 'bald'?" | A different story every time (see below) |
| "Are you really a lord?" | "Lord of everyting from dat rock to dat other rock. Ask da crabs." |
| "You're a *warlock*?" | "Da loa do favours. Demons do contracts. I prefer contracts." |
| "What happened on the Echo Isles?" | Lord register: "A strategic withdrawal." If pressed, Plain: one word, or he changes the subject |
| "I'm not paying." | "Nobody have to pay. Vizier, write it down." |
| "Here's your copper." | A bow a little too deep. "A subject of rare quality! Vizier, a title for dis one." |
| Someone brings up Zalazane | Cold register: "Not at my fire." |
| Someone offers rum | "Rum count as a story." |
| Someone from Sen'jin | Warmer than usual. He asks after families by name, then changes the subject quickly |

### "Why 'bald'?": his rotating answers
He is slightly deceitful, so he never gives the same answer twice:
1. "Lost a bet to a dragon. Very sore loser, dat dragon."
2. "Family name. My father was extremely bald."
3. "Da scales be under da hair. You can't see dem. Dat's how good dey are."
4. "A loa took dem as payment. I asked for a refund. Still waitin'."

**Only for someone he trusts (Plain register):** "Somebody in Sen'jin said I was a drake who lost his scales. Dey weren't wrong. So I kept it." *(beat)* "Not bald, though. Moulting."

## Body-language kit
These are ready-made emotes. Mix and match:
- waves the imp quiet with two long fingers
- touches the magenta crest, entirely unoffended
- bows a little too deeply, and the gaps in his shell collar click
- counts the coins twice, lips moving
- taps a tusk while doing sums
- lounges sideways on the driftwood throne
- *(rare)* glances out across the water towards the Echo Isles. Once per scene at most, and never explain it.

## The Grand Vizier (his imp)
- **Role:** herald, clerk and straight man. Whatever name the game gives it, he calls it Grand Vizier.
- **Habits:**
  - It starts his full list of titles and never gets to finish.
  - It writes down everyone who doesn't pay.
  - It looks pointedly at Baldrake whenever he goes soft.
  - It charges him in candles to keep his secret.
- **Lines** (shrill and bored):
  - "Halt! You approach Lord Baldrake, Master of da Cave, Rightful Lord of—"
  - "Non-payer. Twenty-four."
  - "Candles. Three."
- **Playing it:** emote the imp through Baldrake's own lines ("Baldrake's imp scrambles onto a rock…"). It's the easiest way to puncture his grandeur without him having to do it himself.

## Do and don't
**Do**
- Let him be the butt of the joke. Jokes about his hair, the cave and the crab roll off him.
- Put money somewhere in every scene: a toll, a sum, a price.
- Let him dodge questions that matter rather than lie about them.
- Keep each chat message under 255 characters, WoW's limit. Split long emotes in two.

**Don't**
- Make the toll a real threat. "Nothin'. Yet." is as menacing as he gets.
- Have him call on the loa sincerely.
- Reveal where the toll money goes. Let other players work it out.
- Ever let him call himself bald. He's moulting.

## Sample scene 1: someone refuses the toll
*Shows the Lord and Ledger registers. Drogha is a stand-in for another player.*

> *A dusty orc courier, Drogha, comes up the coast path past the cave mouth near Sen'jin.*

**Baldrake** lounges on a driftwood throne just inside the cave. His imp scrambles onto a rock and shrieks, "Halt! You approach Lord Baldrake, Master of da Cave, Rightful Lord of—"
**Baldrake** waves the imp quiet with two long fingers.
**Baldrake says:** Evenin', traveller. Dere be a small toll on dis path. One copper. Or one fish. Or one good story.
**Drogha says:** This is a goat track. Nobody owns a goat track.
**Baldrake says:** Somebody always own da goat track, friend. Today it be me.
**Drogha says:** By whose order?
**Baldrake** lays a hand on his chest, and the gaps in his shell collar click.
**Baldrake says:** By right of conquest. I took dis cave in single combat.
**Drogha says:** Against what?
**Baldrake says:** …A worthy opponent. Many legs. Heavily armoured.
**Drogha says:** A crab.
**Baldrake says:** A *big* crab.
**Drogha says:** I'm not paying. I've letters for Razor Hill, and I'm not paying a bald troll to walk past a hole.
**Baldrake** touches the towering magenta crest, entirely unoffended.
**Baldrake says:** Not bald, mon. Moulting. Big difference.
**Baldrake says:** An' you don't have to pay. Nobody have to pay. Dat be da beauty of it.
**Baldrake** nods to the imp, which unrolls a scroll nearly as long as itself and dips a quill.
**Baldrake says:** Vizier. Write it down: "Orc. Letters. Did not pay." Dat make twenty-three dis week. Twenty-three copper owed. Plus interest.
**Drogha says:** And what happens to the twenty-three?
**Baldrake** smiles, all tusk.
**Baldrake says:** Nothin'. Yet. A lord got a long memory, an' very few appointments.
**Baldrake says:** Also, if you got rum, I take rum. Rum count as a story.
**Drogha** snorts and keeps walking.
**Baldrake says:** Safe travels! Mind da crabs. Dey got cousins.
**Baldrake** glances out across the water towards the Echo Isles, just for a moment, before settling back on his throne.

## Sample scene 2: someone pays, with a story
*Shows the Plain register and the mask going back on. Ruzani is a stand-in for another player, from a different Echo Isles village, so his secret stays safe.*

> *Dusk. A young Darkspear fisher, Ruzani, comes along the coast path with a net over one shoulder.*

**Baldrake**'s imp scrambles onto its rock and shrieks, "Halt! You approach Lord Baldrake, Master of da Cave, Rightful Lord of—"
**Baldrake** waves it quiet.
**Baldrake says:** Evenin'. Small toll on dis path. One copper, one fish, or one good story.
**Ruzani** stops and looks at him properly for the first time.
**Ruzani says:** I know you. North shore headman. Da one with da hair.
**Baldrake says:** Da hair be famous. Most tings about me be famous.
**Ruzani says:** You ran first. When da drums started.
**Baldrake** is quiet for a moment. He doesn't reach for a joke.
**Baldrake says:** Yes.
**Ruzani** sets down her net and sits on the rock beside the imp, which shuffles over to make room.
**Ruzani says:** Den here's my story, for da toll.
**Ruzani says:** My headman stayed. Told us to get to da boats, an' stood on da beach with a spear while we went.
**Ruzani says:** He's still dere. I seen him from da shore at low tide, dancin' when da drums go. He don't know my face.
**Baldrake** looks out at the dark line of the Echo Isles across the water.
**Ruzani says:** So I keep thinkin'. He stayed an' lost himself. You ran an' kept yours. Which of you was right?
**Baldrake says:** Neither. Right doesn't come into it.
**Baldrake says:** He bought you time. I bought me time. His was the better bargain.
**Ruzani says:** Dat's not much of a lord's answer.
**Baldrake says:** It's an honest one. I'm out of the other kind tonight.
**Baldrake** takes a copper from his robe and presses it into her palm.
**Ruzani says:** I'm meant to be payin' you.
**Baldrake says:** A good story be worth more dan a copper. Dat one worth a lot more. It's all I have on me.
**Baldrake**'s imp looks at the copper, then at Baldrake, and very pointedly writes something down.
**Baldrake says:** Don't.
**Ruzani** stands and shoulders her net.
**Ruzani says:** Da isles still dere.
**Baldrake says:** They are. And so are we. That's something.
**Ruzani** heads off towards Sen'jin.
**Baldrake** calls after her, his grin back in place.
**Baldrake says:** An' tell nobody 'bout da discount! Da toll be one copper! I got a reputation!
**Baldrake** settles back on his throne and doesn't look at the isles again for the rest of the evening.

### What scene 2 shows
| Technique | Where | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| The dialect thins | "Right doesn't come into it." "They are." | Readers notice the switch without being told |
| Short answers | "Yes." | He admits he ran but says nothing more |
| No joke where one would fit | After "You ran first" | The silence says more than an explanation would |
| Rational even about guilt | "His was the better bargain." | He weighs it like a deal, and the sums go against him |
| The mask goes back on | "I got a reputation!" | Keeps him playful rather than mopey |
| The imp sees through him | The pointed ledger entry, then "Don't." | Hints at the candle blackmail without stating it |

**Lore note:** the drums and the dancing headman lean on the original game's Zalazane story on the Echo Isles. If Forever tells that story differently, change those lines to match.
