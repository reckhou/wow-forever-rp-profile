**Spoilers:** each profile ends with player-only notes: the truth behind the rumours, the secrets, and where the character could grow. In real play those stay private. They're included here to show the skill's full output.

## How Baldrake was made
1. **First request:** "a troll warlock (male) named Baldrake Cavethrone, with the idea of 禿龍洞主" ("bald-dragon cave lord"). The skill drafted straight away and listed what it had assumed.
2. **Character screenshot and four trait sliders:** a magenta crest (so not bald), teal eyes, slightly grandiose, moderately bon vivant (loves money), slightly deceitful, strongly rational. The concept was reworked to fit.
3. **Direction:** "a playful middle-aged troll lord who lost his power, fled, became a warlock and claimed a cave". This became the final version: an Echo Isles headman who fled Zalazane, with 禿龍 read as a drake that has lost its scales ("Not bald, mon. Moulting.").
4. **Extras:** two test scenes, a template 1 layout and a voice guide.
